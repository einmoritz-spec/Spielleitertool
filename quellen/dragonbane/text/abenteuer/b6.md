## ADVENTURE OPENING
Read the following text aloud to the players:
> The sea wind howls and the smell of seaweed and decay
> stings your noses. You pull your cloaks tighter around
> you as the rowboat cuts through the restless sea towards your destination. Then the fog clears to reveal the sinking
> tower rising from the water. This is the moment you have
> all been waiting for, the day when the enchanted stone
> tower said to have been built by a mad mage rises from
> the sea once more. It only happens once every twenty years,
> according to the fishermen around the inn’s warming
> hearth, and only stays above water for two hours. Then it
> sinks back into the deep along with its treasures. There is
> a ghostly green light glowing ominously at the top of the
> tower. That is where the sorcerer’s emerald is said to be.
> Perhaps you will be the first to find it?
### THE ONE-­EYED MAN
In a rowboat at the foot of the tower stands an old man wrapped in a brown robe. His face is weathered and lined with age, and a black patch covers his left eye. He begins to speak:
*“You are not the first to seek what is not yours, but I hope* *you are the last. Who I am does not matter, but up in the* *tower there is something that belongs to me. A green emer­* *ald that was stolen from me long ago. Bring me the emerald* *and you may keep the rest of the treasures you find in there.* *In exchange, I offer you an elixir of life. Whoever drinks this* *is brought back from the realm of the dead.”* The aged man, who is actually the lindworm Krakul in human form, holds out a glass bottle with a green liquid inside. If any of the characters accepts the offer, they will receive the elixir if they promise to return the emerald to him. This will play a role in the confrontation at the obser­ vatory (#7) later in the adventure. The bottle contains one dose of elixir which allows a player character with zero HP to automatically recover and stop making death rolls. The elixir has no effect on instantly fatal wounds.
[P] It is possible for a player character to climb the outside of the tower, but it is not easy. It requires a successful ACROBATICS roll with a bane for each floor. On failure, the adventurer loses their grip and plunges into the sea. The victim takes no damage but must choose a con­ dition. A successful climber can enter the tower through windows on floors four and five.

- **Mural:**  The far wall is covered with a mural of an idyllic scene: a cottage by the sea, two children playing, birds gliding over the water.
- **Statue of Kamandur:**  The western statue in the center of the room depicts a cloaked man with a long beard looking straight ahead. The statue’s right arm is raised, index finger pointing up. See adjacent image.
- **Statue of Magdala:**  The eastern statue depicts a young woman wearing a robe, her face tilted down toward the floor. The statue’s left hand is resting on its chest, with its middle and ring fingers extended. See adjacent image.
- **Copper Plates:**  Six round plates of green copper, each one meter in diameter, are embedded in the floor between the statues. Anyone who examines them and makes a SPOT HIDDEN roll will find that they are pressure plates. If someone presses hard or stands on one or more plates at the same time, there is a loud click. What happens next depends on how many plates are pressed.
– **One, three, four, five, or six plates are pressed:** A bolt of lightning strikes from the ceiling, dealing D6 damage to anyone standing on the flooded floor.
Armor has no effect.
– **Two plates are pressed:**  a faint rumble is heard from the ceiling and a stone staircase magically unfolds in the southwest corner of the room. The staircase leads up to the next floor. Note that two plates must be pressed at exactly the same time to open the staircase.
[P] Kamandur was obsessed with his dead sister.
[P] His entire life revolved around her memory.
[P] Together they were a unit, which is why most of the tower was designed with the number two in mind. This is not something the players need to understand (though it would make things easier for them), but as GM, you should emphasize the siblings’ presence in the tower. The two of them can be seen in the murals, there are two statues, the tower rises for two hours, and so on. The number two is the solution to several puzzles in the tower, such as the copper plates on the bottom floor or the symbols used to solve the puzzles two floors up.
