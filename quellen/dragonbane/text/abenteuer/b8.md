### GATES OF POWER

<!-- S. 55 · PDF 59 -->
*ates of Power* is the second adventure in the P*ath* *of Glory* trilogy and picks up right where *The Dead* *Forest* left off. This adventure describes an under­ ground dwarven kingdom, abandoned for many centuries but recently discovered by a band of orcs and undead in the service of the necromancer Dakoth. There are great treasures here but perhaps even greater dangers, although many of them can be avoided with cunning and caution.
The text first describes the opening of the adventure, followed by a summary of the background, some key char­ acters and creatures, and general information on how to play the adventure. Next, the five sectors of the cave system are described in detail, room by room. An overview of the plot can be found on page 8 of the campaign introduction.

### TIME TO ENTER…
The poem in the pendant that the player characters have probably received from the hermit in Deepmire reveals how to use the three keys:
#### TEFALAS’ DAGGER
[P] The dagger given to Tefalas by his master God­ vindel is magical and mastercrafted, granting a boon on all attacks against undead, and making its bearer immune to fear attacks and magical attacks from undead.
[P] The first time an attack with the dagger hits a death wight and inflicts at least 1 HP of damage, the magical fetters binding the wight to the world are severed and its spirit is sucked into the dagger’s blade. The dagger can only be used to trap one death wight once.
[P] Trapping the undead spirit in the dagger and keeping it there requires mental energy. The bearer loses D6 WP when the wight is caught – if they lack sufficient WP the spirit is not trapped – and then another 1 WP each night. During this time, the character cannot recover WP at all, except through magic.
[P] If the bearer loses all WP while carrying the dagger, their spirit is sucked into the dagger and the trapped death wight takes control of the bear­ er’s body. The bearer senses this when they have 5 WP left. The character begins to feel weak and is plagued by dreams of a shadowy figure emerging from the dagger and seeping up their arm, which no longer feels like part of the bearer’s body.
[P] If the bearer lets go of the dagger, the effect ceases and WP can be recovered normally, but the wight breaks free after one shift. The characters can take turns carrying the dagger, however. A new character who takes the dagger immediately loses D6 WP and keeps losing WP as per above.
[P] The dagger cannot be destroyed by any means available to the player characters. Read more about the true function and purpose of the dagger on page 123 in Heart of Darkness.

## CURRENT SITUATION
For eight hundred years, the Gates of Power stood closed.
After the civil war between Queen Gwinna and her brother Kalin (page 5) had ended and the last of the dwarves had left, the stillness of death long reigned in the kingdom.
Recently, however, this has changed. The halls within are no longer empty.
The necromancer Dakoth has found a way in from the east, via an underground river that flows into the Eastern Lands. He has sent his greatest servant, the death wight Mogdath, to lead an expedition of undead skel­ etons, enslaved orcs and ogres, a couple of manticores, the nightwarg Sharch, and the two shadow wraiths Daz and Gozar.
Their mission is to loot the dwarven realm and put its mines and forges back into operation to make weapons for the ongoing war. They are also looking for the Heart of Darkness, not knowing that the artifact is actually hidden in the Eastern Lands (page 145).
### TRAPS AND CAVE-INS
Once inside, Mogdath began to map the dwarven halls.
It soon became clear that the realm had succumbed to infighting, and much of the city had been destroyed by traps set off in the final stages of the war.
But it also turned out that some traps were still active, and clumsy orcs triggered several of them in search of treasure. Numerous halls and corridors col­ lapsed and the last passages into the deeper parts of the realm were blocked forever. Many orcs lost their lives in the cave-­ins.
To avoid future accidents, the wights have now banned the orcs and ogres from the upper levels of the realm. Skeletons were posted at the entrance, both to guard against intruders and to keep the orcs away. Some of them also patrol the empty corridors. The manticores guard the last of the treasures.
### MINING
The ogres are now mining the rich iron ore of the moun­ tain, which is then processed by the orcs in the dwarves’ old forge. But after all the cave-­ins, Mogdath’s servants are too few to run the forge at full capacity. Dakoth has sent word that he will come to inspect the forge at the turn of the moon and assess its future operation. He also expects Mogdath to have found the Heart of Darkness or at least information regarding its whereabouts.
This puts great pressure on Mogdath to achieve results, and he pushes his underlings hard. He wanders randomly through the underground passages and corri­ dors, occasionally using magic to make sure everything is in order, but he cannot be everywhere at once and he barely trusts even his closest subordinates, the shadow wraiths Daz and Gozar.
[P] Once opened, the Gates of Power will stay that way and cannot be closed by any means available to the player characters. This means that other people from the Dead Forest can enter the dwarven kingdom as well, such as Stormhand or Doriath. After a shift, patrolling skeleton guards or one of the wights will also notice that the gates are open and report to Mogdath that intruders have come to the old kingdom.
#### CLOSING THE GATES?
[P] If Stormhand and Doriath survived The Dead Forest, they may have a role to play in Gates of Power as well. They may even accompany the player characters here. However, both of them seek the Heart of Darkness for their own gain and will betray the characters as soon as it benefits them.

## KEY OPPONENTS
This section describes the undead that play an important role in the adventure and that may be encountered in var­ ious places: the death wight Mogdath, the shadow wraiths Daz and Gozar, and the nightwarg Sharch. Other monsters, creatures, and individuals are described where they appear in the adventure.
### MOGDATH
Mogdath was once a knight of Eledain. Hungry for power and prone to anger, he constantly sought to increase his magical might. Eventually he was drawn to dark secrets forbidden by the teachings of Eledain and made contact with the necromancer Dakoth.
Mogdath was slain in his castle by his own order, and though Dakoth soon found the dying Mogdath, he could do nothing but recall Mogdath’s spirit from the nether­ world. Thus, a new Mogdath arose as a bone-­chilling death wight. He grasped his ancient sword and swore eternal allegiance to Dakoth.
A death wight is a very powerful type of undead that appears in a form similar to the one it had in life, but completely black with glowing red eyes. The death wight’s body is formed of concentrated darkness and is fully material.
Unlike normal wights (page 87 of the Rulebook), death wights are not bound to a particular place but follow their master’s command wherever it takes them.

## OVERVIEW
The dwarven stronghold known as the Gates of Power consists of five areas spread over four levels, connected by various staircases and openings. See the layout below.
**The Halls of Arrival (level 0):**  The gates from the Dead Forest open into the Halls of Arrival. They are populated by undead skeleton guards. There is a dwarven temple that houses the ghost of the kingdom’s last ruler, Queen Gwinna, which can provide valuable information and a safe resting place. A hidden staircase and two spiral staircases (locked) lead down to the Forge. A large grand staircase leads up to the Halls of Council.
**The Halls of Council (level +1):**  This is Mogdath’s base until he learns of the player characters’ intrusion. The area is mostly unpopulated, but holds a potential ally of the characters: the little brandy dragon. From here, the large eastern staircase leads down to the City, and the western staircase to the Halls of Arrival.
**The City (level 0):**  This area was largely destroyed by fighting before the realm was abandoned, and later by the traps set off by the orcs. It is now home to the orcs who work in the forge, ordered about by their leader Black­ tooth, who in turn is terrified of Mogdath and the shadow wraiths. A couple of manticores guard the remaining trea­ sures. The area is only connected to the Halls of Council by the eastern staircase and to the Forge by another large set of stairs. Although the City and the Halls of Arrival are at the same level, there is no direct route between them.
A large staircase leads down to the Forge.
**The Forge (level −1):**  Here orcs and ogres are forging weap­ ons. From here, a hidden staircase and two spiral staircases lead up to the Halls of Arrival, and another staircase to the City. The Mine below is reached by stairs and an ore bucket.
There is also a dock by the underground river leading out of the dwarven realm to the Eastern Lands.
**The Mine (level −2)**  is mostly a dead end. This is where the ogres work and live. The Mine is connected to the Forge by an ore bucket and a staircase.

## TIPS FOR THE GM
*Gates of Power* is a location-­based adventure with no pre­ determined plot. The player characters enter the dwarven realm and, at Tefalas’ behest, presumably want to trap Mogdath’s spirit in the magic dagger, and also find another way out, but how they go about completing these objec­ tives is up to the players.
As the GM, you will mainly be using the description of the cave system and its denizens, as well as random event tables. You are also free to manage the main antagonists, Mogdath and his henchmen, as you see fit. Use the Mog­ dath’s Reactions sidebar (page 61) and adapt the events to the player characters’ actions.
The outcome of the following events is particularly important:
- Do any of the first skeleton guards in the Halls of Arrival succeed in alerting Mogdath? If the characters prevent this, they will have more time to explore the dwarven stronghold. Once Mogdath becomes aware of their intrusion, the risk of encountering the shadow wraiths increases.
- Do the characters find their way to the temple (#13) and chapel (#47) and speak with the ghosts of Queen Gwinna and the priest Kaldur? There, they can take shift rests and gain valuable information about the dwarven realm. From the priest, they can learn that the Heart of Darkness is no longer here, but probably in the Eastern Lands. This is key information – try to make sure the characters get it.
- Can the characters form an alliance with the orcs against Mogdath and the shadow wraiths? This takes care of many of the enemies in the adventure, but requires a great deal of diplomacy (page 81).
### RANDOM EVENTS
The descriptions of the different areas of the dwarven realm include tables for random events. An event can occur multiple times unless otherwise stated.
Roll for random events every stretch except in the City.
When Mogdath finds out about the characters’ intrusion, dangerous events become more likely.
Remember that the tables are only a tool for you as the GM – you can decide for yourself whether an event should occur or not, and choose events instead of rolling randomly.
**Escalating Events:**  Some random events escalate each time they occur. This is indicated by the letters A, B, and C – event A occurs the first time, B the second time, and C the third time.
### TIME
As the player characters explore the cave system, you should count stretches to keep track of time. As a rule, it takes one stretch to explore a room. After each stretch you roll to see if their torches go out as well as for random events, if they can occur in the area the characters are in.
On the next page (and available for download on the Free League website) is a sheet that you can use to keep track of time. In the box for each shift, you can tick each stretch that passes. After 24 stretches a new shift begins.
**The Undead Reanimate:**  At the beginning of each shift, all defeated skeletons in the cave complex come back to “life” – as does Mogdath, unless he is trapped in Tefalas’ dagger.

### THE WAY OUT
Apart from the Gates of Power themselves, there is only one other way out of the dwarven realm since most of the city has been destroyed, and that is through an under­ ground river that can be accessed via the dock in the Forge, one level below the entrance level. The characters can gain information about this from Queen Gwinna’s ghost in the temple (#13).

## CONCLUSION
he adventure likely ends when Mogdath is defeated, preferably trapped in Tefalas’ dagger, and the player characters have left the Gates of Power via the underground river, heading for the Eastern Lands.
However, there are other possible endings. Mogdath might escape and reappear in the final adventure, *Heart* *of Darkness*. The same applies to Doriath, Stormhand, and Tefalas, if any of them are still alive.
At this point, *Gates of Power* is concluded and each player character is rewarded with a heroic ability of their choice (chapter 3 in the Rulebook). Their adventures will continue in *Heart of Darkness*.
