## THE SITUATION
A long time ago a brutal robber knight ravaged the area in which this adventure takes place. His name was Rothgar Wolfsbane, and he fought everyone in his path – humans, dwarves, elves, and orcs. The sound of his dreaded war horn made the blood of his enemies run cold.
So loathed was Wolfsbane that the elder folk, who for ages had been sworn enemies, put aside their feuds and united to face their common foe. Eventually, a horde of orcs laid siege to the castle where Wolfsbane was entrenched. The battle for the castle was bloody and many orcs fell to the robber knight’s blade, but in the end, they overpowered him – Rothgar Wolfsbane had his head cut off by an orc scimitar while his castle was engulfed in flames. The robber knight’s reign of terror was over.
Now, many years later, the sounds of battle have faded but the ruins of the robber knight’s castle still stand.
On moonlit nights, passing travelers have heard the blast of Wolfsbane’s war horn and seen the headless robber knight appear in ghostly form. Rumors tell of caravans and adventurers disappearing near the ruin, and nowadays most people take detours around the hill.
It is true that the robber knight walks again in his castle. But what nobody knows is that a band of goblin bandits led by the cunning Jaldo are summoning the head­ less wight to scare people away from the ruin. They have made the castle their own and it is from there that they set out to raid the surrounding area.

#### THE GOBLINS
[P] For each full stretch the player characters spend in the castle, perhaps searching a room or taking a short rest, you can roll D6 on the table below.
[P] **D6**  **EVENT** 1 **Goblins.**  Two goblins suddenly appear. After a moment of confusion, they draw their short swords and attack with a shriek.
[P] 2 **Rooting Pig.**  Merle the pig grunts contentedly in a corner. When it notices the player characters, it freezes with a startled oink and flees through the nearest exit. If they hurt Merle, they will have made an enemy for life in the orc Grunta (see the cellar, location #5).
[P] 3 **Evil Spirits.**  Suddenly the air begins to vibrate and a cold whirlwind swirls around the player characters. The outlines of three long-dead warriors in chainmail appear in the howling wind, reaching out to the player characters as if to drag them into the realm of the dead. Everyone must make a WIL roll to resist fear before the wraiths disappear.
[P] 4 **Storm.**  Dark clouds gather over the castle.
[P] Suddenly the sky opens up in torrents of rain and thunder rumbles through the valley. For the remainder of the adventure, all ranged attacks are rolled with a bane while outdoors.
[P] 5 **Ravens.**  A flock of ravens suddenly bursts from a crack, heading straight at the player characters.
[P] Everyone must make an EVADE roll, and those who fail take D3 piercing damage from the ravens’ beaks and talons. Then the cawing flock flies off towards the top of the tower.
[P] 6 **Trap!**  The goblins have set a cunning trap in the form of a suspended warhammer that is triggered when a player character (the one with the lowest AGL) accidentally steps on a loose stone or floorboard. The player character who triggered the trap must succeed with an EVADE roll or take 2D6 bludgeoning damage.

### 4. GREAT HALL
> What must once have been the great hall of the castle is
> now a sad mess of half-rotten rafters, broken dishes, and
> fallen mortar. The center of the room is dominated by a
> long oak table, and a large painting hangs askew on the
> north wall. You hear a faint gurgling sound coming from a

- **The Oak Table:**  This is where the robber knight and his adjutants used to eat. Now the table is covered in debris – pottery shards, old tin cups, broken wine bottles, and moldy food scraps. The latter indicates that someone has been eating here recently. A successful CRAFTING roll reveals that the pottery shards and wine bottles are contemporary items of halfling origin.
- **The Portrait:**  A large painting hangs askew on the north wall. It depicts a stern man with evil gray eyes, a wicked grin, and raven hair swept behind his head. He wears a full suit of armor and carries a horned great helm under his arm.

- [!] **The Sleeping Goblin:** *The goblin chief Jaldo has made* *himself a little den among the rotten tablecloths and* *broken crockery in the corner cupboard. **Jaldo’s Sob Story:**  If the player characters surprise Jaldo and wake him up, he realizes that he is outnumbered and pretends to be a poor lonely goblin who got lost and sought shelter in the castle. He quickly concocts a ram­ bling and ill-conceived story about a brutal orc clan taking over the castle and forcing him into the cupboard. Jaldo offers to show them where the orcs are hiding – he plans to lead them to the cellar, where the not-so-violent orc Grunta is confined, and lock them in.
[P] Small, sneaky, and very imaginative. Jaldo has the gift of the gab and has gathered a dozen goblin followers with promises of wine, silver, and a home of their own – promises which, to his great surprise, he actually managed to keep by finding the castle and quickly realizing that he could use the legend of the robber knight to his advantage.

### 5. CELLAR
> Below the hatch is a steep stone staircase leading under­
> ground. It is damp and smells of earth. The stairs end
> in a large space with stacks of barrels lining the eastern
> wall. The southern part of the room has several alcoves
> that the torchlight cannot reach. In a corner sits a large
> orc who turns to you in surprise.
- **The Hatch:**  The wooden hatch to the cellar is not locked when the player characters arrive, but it is bolted from the outside. The hatch has armor rating 8 but can be forced open with 20 points of damage.
- **Grunta:**  In the darkness of the cellar lives the orc Grunta, cast out from her clan and used by Jaldo and his goblin bandits. Grunta’s only job is to blow the robber knight’s horn and summon his wight on Jaldo’s command (the goblins’ lungs are too weak, so there is little more than a pitiful squeak when they try to blow the horn). Grunta is deeply unhappy and wants to leave the castle but does not dare for fear of Jaldo. Her only joy in life is Merle the pig.
- **The Skeleton:**  If the player characters investigate the room and succeed with SPOT HIDDEN, they find a skeleton hidden under the rubble. Rusty chains reveal that the deceased was chained to a wall. Searching through the bones, they find a rolled-up piece of parchment with a sketched self-portrait of a young man, titled *“The artist Embarius in his youth”*.
- **The Goblin Den:**  The alcoves along the southern wall are equipped with simple mattresses made of straw and sticks. Unless the alarm has been raised, there are gob­ lins (one more than the player characters) resting here.
#### THE ETERNAL WATCH OF ROTHGAR WOLFSBANE
[P] When Rothgar Wolfsbane fell during the orcs’ siege of the castle many centuries ago, only his corporeal form died. Despite losing his head, Wolfsbane was so filled with hatred and cursed by so many that he was doomed to eternal undeath. Since then, he has been patrolling the castle as a headless wight, forever looking for his mortal enemies: orcs.
[P] The robber knight can materialize at the GM’s discretion, but the wight can also be summoned by someone with strong lungs (STR 14 or higher) blow­ ing Jaldo’s war horn (location #4). The wight attacks all living creatures except the person carrying the horn. Orcs or people wearing the orc helmet (loca­ tion #1) are attacked first.
[P] With a little bit of cunning, the player characters can convince the undead robber knight that the gob­ lins are in fact tiny orcs (this wight is so blinded by its hatred of orcs that it can be PERSUADED with a successful skill roll). If they succeed, the characters can just sit back and enjoy their brilliant idea while the wight makes short work of the goblins.
[P] There are three ways to overcome the robber knight:
[P] - Defeating it in combat. However, the robber knight rises again after one shift (about six hours) and continues to haunt the castle.
[P] - Crushing the robber knight’s skull at the bottom of the tower (#6). If this happens, the wight runs amok for two rounds (its Ferocity increases to 3) before collapsing in a pile of bones. Then the entire castle begins to shake and collapse. All player characters must make an EVADE roll (not an action) every round they remain inside the castle walls. On the first failure they take D6 bludgeoning damage, the second failure inflicts 2D6 damage, and so on.
[P] - Delivering the skull to the headless wight. If this happens, the wight stops and puts its head in place. Then it lets out a long sigh and collapses as described above. The castle crumbles in the same way.
[P] Grunta is an orc without a home or purpose.
[P] Cast out of her clan, she wandered the lands in search of a new one. Her constant companion is Merle the pig, whom she rescued from a hungry wolfman. They have been inseparable ever since.
[P] When she met Jaldo and his goblins, Grunta was blinded by the promise of new friends and fell for his lies. Now she is stuck in the cellar because she is too afraid to leave – afraid of Jaldo and the robber knight’s wight and that the goblins might eat her beloved pig. If there was a chance to escape with Merle, she would take it.
[P] **Mov.:**  10 **Damage Bonus STR:**  +D4 **HP:**  12 **Skills:**  Awareness 14, Evade 10 **Weapon:**  Small wooden club (skill level 12, damage D8)

### 6. TOWER
> What must once have been a mighty stone tower is now
> just a hollow shell. The floors above have collapsed, and
> the roof is long gone. All that remains are fallen stones,
> mossy stone walls, and a stone staircase that winds its way
> down, partly covered in rubble and debris.
- **The Ravens:**  A flock of ravens has nested high up in the tower. Where the third floor once was there is now only a small stone ledge protruding from the wall. A player character who succeeds with an ACROBATICS roll can climb up to the ravens’ nest. Roll once on the treasure tables on page 121 to see what’s there.
- **The Stairs:**  The way down is almost completely blocked by stones from the collapsed floors, but it is possible to get past the rubble by crouching and moving carefully.
- [!] **Hoofprints:** *A successful* SPOT HIDDEN*roll reveals* *the muddy hoofprints of a pig leading down the stairs.*
- **Falling Boulder:**  When the player characters are halfway down the stairs, a large stone further up comes loose and bounces towards them. Have everyone make an EVADE roll – those who fail are pushed forward and end up in the water at the end of the stairs, taking D8 bludgeoning damage.
- **The Pig:**  The staircase disappears into black water at the bottom level of the tower. Decades of rainwater have turned what was once a wine cellar into a deep well. On a ledge by the water, Merle the pig has made himself a nest. If the player characters have not yet encountered Merle (see random encounter #2), he is resting in the nest and greets the adventurers with a surprised grunt.
- **The Helmet:**  If the player characters peer down into the depths, they see something shimmering in the black water. The well is four meters deep and it takes a successful SWIMMING roll to reach the bottom and find the treasure. Roll twice on the treasure tables on page 121 to see what it is. There is also a grinning skull inside a rusty, horned helmet – this is the skull of Rothgar Wolfsbane, which can be used to banish the wight. If the player characters have seen the portrait in the great hall (#4), they immediately recognize the helmet that the robber knight carried under his arm.
The helmet has armor rating +2 but gives a bane on AWARENESS rolls and ranged attacks.
