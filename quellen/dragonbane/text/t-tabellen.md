[P] **D6**  **EFFECT** 1 **Boiling Cauldron** - Throw the boiling water in a cone that is 4 meters in both length and width.
[P] - Everyone within the cone suffers 2D6 damage.
[P] - The attack can be dodged, but not parried.
[P] - Armor has no effect.
[P] 2 **Bucket of Soapy Water** - Throw the soapy water in a cone that is 4 meters in both length and width.
[P] - Everyone within the cone falls down.
[P] - The attack can be dodged, but not parried.
[P] 3 **Burning Firewood** - Hit an enemy within 2 meters with the firewood.
[P] - Requires one free hand.
[P] - The attack hits automatically and inflicts 2D6 fire damage plus damage bonus.
[P] - The attack can be dodged or parried.
[P] - The firewood can then be used as a torch.
[P] 4 **Rummaging Pig** - Make a BUSHCRAFT roll to agitate the pig and make it attack an enemy of your choice within 10 meters.
[P] - The attack hits automatically and inflicts 3D6 bludgeoning damage.
[P] - The attack can be dodged but not parried.
[P] - Then the pig storms off.
[P] 5 **Wine Bottle** - Hit an enemy within 2 meters with the bottle.
[P] - Requires one free hand.
[P] - The attack hits automatically and inflicts 2D6 bludgeoning damage plus damage bonus.
[P] - The attack can be dodged or parried.
[P] - The enemy gets wine in their eyes, which stings so bad that it gives a bane on all actions until your next turn.
[P] 6 **Chandelier** - Swing yourself from the chandelier and perform a melee attack.
[P] - You can take a normal movement action without triggering free attacks.
[P] - The melee attack must be unarmed, but cannot be dodged or parried.
[P] - If the attack fails, you fall to the ground and take D6 bludgeoning damage.
[P] **D6**  **EFFECT** 1 **Stalactite** - Shove an enemy within 2 meters into the sharp rock formations hanging from the ceiling.
[P] - The attack hits automatically and inflicts 2D6 piercing damage plus damage bonus.
[P] - The attack can be dodged but not parried.
[P] - Armor has no effect, except helmets.
[P] 2 **Torch** - Grab a torch from the wall and strike an enemy within 2 meters.
[P] - Requires one free hand.
[P] - The attack hits automatically and inflicts 2D6 fire damage plus damage bonus.
[P] - The attack can be dodged or parried.
[P] - You may then keep the torch.
[P] 3 **Stalagmite** - Shove an enemy within 2 meters into a tall rock formation on the cave floor.
[P] - The attack hits automatically and inflicts D6 bludgeoning damage plus damage bonus and knocks the enemy to the ground.
[P] - The attack can be dodged but not parried.
[P] 4 **Puddle** - Roll to topple the enemy (page 48).
[P] - If you succeed, the enemy must spend one action and make an ACROBATICS roll to get back up.
[P] 5 **Crevice** - Roll to topple the enemy (page 48).
[P] - If you succeed, the enemy falls into a 2D6 meter deep crevice and suffers falling damage.
[P] - Climbing up requires an ACROBATICS roll.
[P] 6 **Bats** - Make a BUSHCRAFT roll to agitate the bats and make them attack an enemy within 10 meters.
[P] - For stats, see page 99.
[P] - The bats keep attacking the enemy for D3 rounds.
[P] - If your BUSHCRAFT roll fails, the bats attack you instead.

[P] **D6**  **EFFECT** 1 **Low Brach** - Swing yourself from the branch and perform a melee attack.
[P] - You can make a normal move without triggering free attacks.
[P] - The melee attack must be unarmed, but cannot be dodged or parried.

[P] 2 **Wasp Nest** - Roll BUSHCRAFT to pick up the wasp’s nest and hurl it at an enemy within 10 meters.
[P] - The enemy gets badly stung, which inflicts 2D6 damage and gives them a bane on all actions for a Shift.
[P] - The attack can be dodged but not parried.
[P] - Armor has no effect.
[P] - If your BUSHCRAFT roll fails the wasps sting you instead.
[P] 3 **Gnarled Roots** - Roll to topple the enemy (page 48).
[P] - If you succeed, the enemy must spend one action and make an ACROBATICS roll to get back up.
[P] **D6**  **EFFECT** 4 **Boulder** - Make an ACROBATICS roll to jump up on the boulder and throw yourself at an enemy within 2 meters.
[P] - The attack inflicts 2D6 damage (bludgeoning) plus damage bonus and cannot be dodged or parried.
[P] - If it succeeds, both you and the enemy drop to the ground.
[P] - If your roll fails, only you fall over and suffer D6 damage.
[P] 5 **Viper** - Roll BUSHCRAFT to pick up the snake and throw it at an enemy within 10 meters.
[P] - The enemy gets bitten, which inflicts D6 damage and a lethal poison with Potency 12 if the bite pierces armor.
[P] - The attack can be dodged but not parried.
[P] - If your BUSHCRAFT roll fails, the snake bites you instead.
[P] 6 **Patch of Dirt** - Throw dirt in the eyes of an enemy within 10 meters.
[P] - The enemy suffers D6 damage (armor has no effect) and gets a bane on all actions for the rest of the combat.
[P] - The attack can be dodged but not parried.
