## Wickhead
**HP**  10 **Morale**  7 **No armor,**  **Knife**  d4 **HP**  8 **Morale**  7 **Leather**  -d2
**Staff/Femur**  d4
Wickheads are solitary by
nature, but the wildest and
vilest are cast even from that
loose community. Wandering
forests and rambling ruins,
they take any company they can
find. Fickle, suspicious and
bad, you would be well-advised
to keep them chained.
These freaks are roughly
three parts human to one
part dog. Society sees
them as foul, diseased
and disloyal for a
carrier of canine blood.
Isolation and contempt
have made them self-
reliant scavengers.
**Trait (d4)**
1. Grumpy 2. Apathetic
3. Careless 4. Moody
**Trait (d4)**
1. Arrogant 2. Quiet
3. Joking 4. Hypochondriac
**Specialty (d4)**
1-2. Walking lightsource
3. Expert with a knife (d4+2)
4. Backstab (test DR8 with d20 rolled
by the GM. Deals weapon damage + 3)
Aside from their specialty they
will carry up to five items.
**Speciality (d4)**
1. Excellent cooking
(d4 extra HP when resting)
2. Finds the right path
3. Senses danger
4. Makes/repairs items
**Values (d6)**
1. Fireplace discussions

5. Carnal relations

**Values (d6)**
1. Holding long monologues about

3. Getting paid in sharp weapons
4. Burnt meat 5. Depressing stories
6. Captured slaves
