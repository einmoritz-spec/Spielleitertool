Greetings, those-who-would-be-tiny-bugs,
And welcome to the full release of PICO! I'm Felix Isaacs, creator of *The Wildsea*  and
*Wild Words Engine*  and, now, this!
What you have on the page (or screen, if you're going to be all modern) in front of you
is the finished version of PICO, a much larger game than I ever had in mind when I
first contracted zkarab to draw me some cute bugs back in 2021. This was, after all,
as my publisher has pointed out to me numerous times, initially meant to be a quick,
60-page project.
... I may have overshot a little there.
But hopefully you enjoy the end result, so without any additional preamble
I'll let the you turn the page and (hopefully) enjoy embodying some
tiny bugs.
Good luck, piconauts - its a big world out there.
## - Felix
