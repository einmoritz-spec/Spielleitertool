The Never Ending Story Slugblaster Mad Max Round the Twist Crocodile Dundee Deltora Quest Mortified Avatar the Last Airbender Ever since I was a kid, running around playing spaceship with my Papa, I’ve created so many fun little worlds in my head, Roob is the first one of those I’ve written down. When I first Read Mikey Hamms Slugblaster, I knew I had to make something for it based off of the wild city I grew up in, While I was writing Roob, I got to include so many of the iconic parts of perth, the wierd statues, alien creatures, beautiful beaches and wonderful communities. Almost everything in here is based off of a place or person that’s significant to me, or perth as a whole (if you’re from here, see if you can figure out where all the locations are). During this process, I’ve fallen so much more in love with both the fictional and real city of Roob, and I’m so excited to share that passion with you all!
Oolroob was made on and based off of the occupied Whadjuk Noongar land of Boorloo, Western Australia, I’d like to pay my respects to the elders, past, present, and emerging, who never ceeded soverignty.
Thank you to everyone who helped make this passion project a reality.

1 Located in a pocket dimension called Karlup, stuck between Calorium and Empyrian, Oolroob (Roob to the locals) is a sprawling, suburban maze, it somehow feels like a small town despite being a massive city.
In every direction, beside the west, Roob is surrounded by seemingly endless deserts, to the west is a vast and perilous ocean that splits Roob into the north and south by way of the darkswan river.
The weather here is so hot that It’ll melt the soles of your brand new kicks. The only escapes from the year round sun are surfing at one of the many beaches, hiding in shade or ripping a portal to a cooler dimension.
Up until very recently, Roob was almost entirely cut off from the rest of the multiverse. The only viable way in or out of the city was by sea. But ten years ago, the first portal between worlds was created and a new era began. A Giant portal frame was built in the city centre to create a more stable link between Roob and more bountiful dimensions. This caused a massive boom of technological development, hoverboards, hardlight, rayguns, phase-shifters, gravtech, you name it, Roob now has it.
Because of this massive boom, the southern docks have now fallen into disuse and disrepair, so has become a hangout spot for slugblasters, artists, stoners and all  sorts of wierdos.
- Mysterious doorways appearing in the southern docks that are rumored to be portals to strange places...
- Everyone seems to know each other here, gotta be careful not to get caught getting into trouble or your parents might find out, everyone calls this getting Roobed.
- Half the bushes and trees seem to be in an almost magical state of eternal fire, nobody seems to know how they keep burning.
- A river that feels like it’s boiling your skin off when you swim in it but is somehow perfectly safe

## Oolroob
Everyone runs into Mad Metasaur at some point while in Roob. He appears all around town cycling on his hoverbike and ranting about a secret facility and some kind of “Roob Coob” Mad Metasaur Due to being incredibly isolated, Oolroob only just got the technology to rip portals between dimensions, create hard light structures, hoverboards and phase-shifting surfboards.
Despite only just getting this technology, Roob’s rebellious teens have embraced the high tech future. Unfortunately most of this tech is extremely expensive so many ‘blasters that can’t afford it resort to building their own, stealing them from the local factory or working for the corps that moved in when the portal was built.
Before this tech was around, Roob had a very strong skating and surfing culture. This culture, combined with the divide between north and south of the river, caused “The Great Beef of ‘23”. A massive rivalry between northies and southies that, while resolved, left tension across the divide.
