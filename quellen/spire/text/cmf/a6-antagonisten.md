# ANTAGONISTS {farbe=#194d71}
To add colour to any plot, roll once on the City-level
table and twice on the Street-level table below to see
who else is involved in the situation – the City-level
faction is in charge, and they (directly or indirectly)
control the two Street-level factions. If you do it twice,
you could generate an entire campaign frame with a
bit of work to figure out what they’re fighting over.
