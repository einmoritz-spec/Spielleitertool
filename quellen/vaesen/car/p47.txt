A drekavac can typically be driven out when the root
of the local tragedy is exposed, and locals are no lon-
ger distressed. For example, members of the Soci-
ety might solve the gruesome murder that initially
attracted the drekavac – the culprit gets sent to jail, and
people celebrate, chasing the shadows of tragedy away.
     Another way to expel a drekavac is to perform a com-
munal baptism at night. The number of participants
directly impacts the likelihood of a successful ritual.
     For temporary protection, one might also get a dog.
It is said that drekavaci hate dogs and try to avoid them.
