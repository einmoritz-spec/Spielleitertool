                                  UDILNYTSIA
I visited this man in a local asylum, and he looked just like a person who should be there:
wild eyes, messy hair, murmuring something… It was difficult to believe that once he had been
a doctor, a man of science, calm, resourceful, clever, quite famous, and perfectly sane. I caught
myself wondering what could lead such a bright personality to such a horrible state. I tried
talking to him and asking questions, but I don’t think he understood a single word. So, I
just wrote down everything I was able to understand in his muttering. Again and again, he
mentioned a woman with a knife that didn’t leave any wounds. Poor lunatic!
   —Antoine Augustin Calmet, May 16, 1750
                                                   An udilnytsia is an evil female spirit, a thief of talents,
                                                   fate, and good luck. They can prey upon any unpro-
                                                   tected person but prefer pregnant women and their
                                                   unborn babies. If the pregnant woman does not wear
                                                   any protective amulets – such as a cross, holy water, or
                                                                       a shirt with special embroideries
                                                                             – and the house has unpro-
                                                                                tected windows, doors, or
                                                                                   chimney, an udilnytsia
                                                                                    can sneak inside.
                                                                                          They use magical
                                                                                       knives, made from
                                                                                        the bones of an
                                                                                        unborn child, to
                                                                                        cut the womb and
                                                                                        take an unborn
                                                                                       baby. The udilnyt-
                                                                                      sia sucks all the tal-
                                                                                    ent and all the fate out
                                                                                  of the baby, and then
                                                                                returns it to the mother’s
                                                                             body, using the same knife to
                                                                       heal the cut and leave no marks.
                                                   Such a baby is born sickly and crippled, if it is born at
                                                   all. Sometimes an udilnytsia may take a baby for itself.
                                                       Udilnytsias looks like pale women with long,
                                                   messy dark hair hiding their faces. Their skinny,
                                                   long hands have sharp nails (or rather, claws). They
                                                   wear rags instead of clothes. Udilnytsias like being
                                                   together with others of their kind. They gather in
                                                   abandoned places to party and chat, sometimes
                                                   turning into magpies.
