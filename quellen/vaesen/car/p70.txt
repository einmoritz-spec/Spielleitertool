                                           VODIANYK
To learn more about the undead, I delved into stories about where they come from. Over and
over, I heard about one spirit in particular that is not undead by nature but somehow rais es
and rules over drowned ones—the Vodianyk. All through my journey, fishermen warned me
that the Lord of the Rivers is compelling and can grant wishes, but it’s dangerous to even listen
 to his words because they are full of lies, and it is mortally dangerous to accept his offers.
Almost every fisherman to the east of the San pays their fee and releas es their first catch back
 into the river—as their way of paying respect to traditions founded by Vodianyk. I wish I
 could encounter this creature in my future travels.
   —Antoine Augustin Calmet, July 1746
