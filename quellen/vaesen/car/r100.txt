When the villagers realize that the beast is among
them, panic ensues. Some villagers take refuge in
the church and ring the church bells to alert the knez
of the threat. If the player characters are not in the
village during the celebrations but up at the castle,
they will be alerted by this sound and can follow the
knez down (below).
The attack can play out in many ways. It can happen in the streets or inside the church.
Maria stays close, controlling the beast with her
spindle. If the player characters spot her and realize
that she controls the vârcolac, they may attack her
or try to take the spindle from her. If confronted,
she may admit that she controls the beast but claims
that her actions are justified to get revenge on the
knez, revealing the truth of his past crimes.
Eve initially stays back, letting the player characters deal with the vârcolac, and helping villagers
take shelter in the church. If they attack Maria, Eve
will furiously attack them and defend her mother.
However, if told the truth about the beast, Maria
and the knez, she may be reasoned with (MANIPULATION test).

THE VÂ RCOL AC
“I… am… hungry…”
The beast in this mystery is one of the first vârcolaci, believed to have been sent by God to
punish people for their sins. It is an ancient
being that spent centuries under the Carpathian Mountains and was freed by an earthquake.
Weakened, it hunted in the nearest village to
satisfy its hunger but encountered the local
knez and his warriors. The vârcolac killed
everyone except for the knez’s champion, who
lured it into the dungeons and sealed it there.
The champion was mortally wounded in the
process, but despite that, he was chosen to
become the new knez. He died shortly after
and turned into a vampire. Now immortal, the
new knez tried to kill the vârcolac by different
means with his new powers but failed each time.
The vârcolac tried to escape a few times,
driven by its eternal hunger and desire to
devour heavenly bodies. The first time was
when it offered the knez assistance to win
Maria’s favor. The knez freed the vârcolac,
who then killed Maria’s parents, but the vârcolac was not strong enough to escape the knez
and was sealed back into the dungeons. The
next time it made an escape attempt was when
Maria found it in the dungeon cell. It told her
about the knez’s original plan and promised to
get revenge for her if she set it free. Shocked,
Maria refused but agreed to take a piece of its
fur with her when she left the valley.
At the beginning of the mystery, the vârcolac looks like a creature between a man and a
wolf and can communicate coherently. The
vârcolac’s appearance and power depend on
its hunger. The more it eats, the stronger it
becomes, but at the same time it becomes
more bestial and incoherent. See the vârcolac’s countdown (page 80) for more information. As the vârcolac kills, the thread to
Maria’s spindle tightens more and more and
will eventually break, setting the beast free.

THE KNEZ A RRI V ES
Soon after the initial attack by the vârcolac, the knez
arrives on horseback, accompanied by his six “children”
residing in the castle. The knez is dressed like a warrior of old, with a metal cuirass protecting his heart.
Unless the knez arrives in the middle of the attack,
he is met by the villagers led by Eve, accusing him
of failing in his duties and endangering his people.
This meeting can take place in the town square or
inside the church.
Eve encourages the villagers to renounce the treaty
and denounce the knez. Her resolve in this may
strengthen or falter if the truth about the knez,
her mother, and the vârcolac is revealed to her.
