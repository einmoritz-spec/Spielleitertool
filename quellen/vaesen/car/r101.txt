If the vârcolac is Broken during the Confrontation,
it’s only temporarily banished and will return later the
same night. However, if no longer under the control
of Maria’s yarn spindle, and if the beast is Broken a
second time, it will disappear into the night.
EV E

Eve is a key character in the finale of this mystery.
During the Confrontation (or even earlier), she will
likely learn the truth not only about her own nature,
but also about her father the knez and his murder of
her grandparents. She will naturally be hostile toward
him but realizing she will soon become a vampire
herself, she may accept a pact with him and an unlife
in the castle by his side. Her reactions depend on the
actions of the knez, Maria and the player characters
during the final Confrontation.
