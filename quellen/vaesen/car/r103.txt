PRELUDE
This first section describes the background of the
mystery and the conflicts on which it is based. This
is followed by an invitation to kick off the session
and a description of the journey to Lemberg (Lviv)
in the Ukraine. It concludes with a countdown of
events which you as Game­master will initiate at
some appropriate time during the mystery and a
catastrophe that describes what happens if the player
characters do not take action or fail in their efforts.
But first we will look back at the events leading up
to the mystery.
