IN V ITATION
This mystery occurs in the middle of fall, when the
weather is typically rainy and windy. The invitation
comes in the form of a letter (handout 2A on page 130,
also available for download on the Free League website), delivered to the Society headquarters. It reads
as follows:

some additional information, let each player make a
LEARNING test. Player characters who search for information about Brygidki can find records of the prison’s
complicated history in the city archives. If they check
local newspapers, they can find an article discussing
the prison’s future and information about its current
state. The prison is overcrowded and some advocate
moving it out of the city’s boundaries.

To whom it may concern,
My name is Josef Strasser. I am the Warden of Brygidki prison in the
city of Lemberg, in the western part of Galicia. Lately, some unusual
events have occurred here – several prisoners fell ill, and some of
them have died rather gruesome deaths since then. I fear this streak
might continue, and the thought that I can’t do anything to help
people who are my responsibility haunts me.
I believe in the safety of my prison, and those deaths looked out
of the ordinary. I am no expert on the supernatural and, therefore,
I am in great need of your assistance.
Here is a list of things that are not allowed within the Brygidki
prison:
- explosives or weapons of any kinds,
- anything sharp, like knives or scissors,
- tobacco or any products containing it,
- alcohol or any drinks/food containing it,
- money or any equivalent in valuables,
- medicine or drugs,
- lighters or matches.
If you are able and willing to intervene in this matter, your attention will be greatly appreciated. As lives are endangered, time is of
the essence.
Most respectfully,
Josef Strasser

THE JOURNEY
Getting to Lemberg from Prague takes around two
days by train, with an overnight stop in Vienna. You
can play out the journey in detail or just handle it
summarily, letting each player briefly describe what
their character does. As usual, the characters can
gain an Advantage during the journey (page 182 of
the core rulebook).
Even before the player characters can see the city,
they notice something resembling a small mountain peak or a massive hill. Those who did any initial
research realize that the city is not located near but
around that hill, which surprisingly appears uninhabited. If asked, locals call it Sandberg or Castle
Mountain because the ruins of an old castle are still
visible there. The player characters may be advised
to visit Castle Mountain for the sweeping view and
the lovely café located there.
The city itself is noisy and lively. Life is booming
here. People go about their daily routines, and shops
are open and welcoming to visitors. The streets are
full of students discussing science issues and young
men studying to become priests. Meeting priests-­
to-­be and actual priests on the streets of Lemberg is
nothing out of the ordinary.
The player characters’ carriage stops by the lovely
Potocki Palace, near the picturesque historical center
of the city. The Palace is owned by a loyal friend of
the Society, who is now absent from the city, so the
players are allowed to stay in the Palace during their
visit. As they arrive in the city in the late evening, they
can spend a night in the comfortable and cozy beds
before going to Brygidki prison.
