JOSEF STR ASSER, WA RDEN
“This is my prison and my duty.”
Josef Strasser is an Austrian officer who took a respectable position as warden in Brygidki before retirement. Strasser works in the penitentiary system with
an earnest belief that given enough effort and support, every person can change and reenter society as
a reformed person. He treats guards and prisoners
with the same respect and expects them to be better
versions of themselves.
Warden Strasser is tall, in his late 50s, and with a
remarkable physique for his age. He has a thick mustache, deep wrinkles on his forehead, and bushy eyebrows. Almost all the guards call him “Walrus” behind
his back, with a note of slight disrespect for his methods of building communication with prisoners. He
walks with the help of his elder wood walking stick,
which defends him from the vykhrovyk.
His beliefs come from personal guilt. Twenty-­eight
years ago, as a young officer, he accidentally killed a
nun in Brygidki. The accident happened when they
argued in a bell tower about moving the monastery out
of the building. He left the crime scene in panic, full of
regret, and did not tell anyone about what happened.
Strasser still feels constant guilt about what he did
and keeps it a secret. His life and philosophy of atonement through actions brought him back to Brygidki as
its Warden. Now, he tries to repent by helping prisoners but is too afraid to admit what he did because it
would destroy his name and career. He is also scared
of what the vykhrovyk would do to him. Thus, he is
interested in driving the vykhrovyk away, keeping his
secret, and continuing his work in the prison.

In the morning, breakfast is served to the player characters, and they leave for their destination. It is only a
20-minute walk from Potocki Palace through the city
center. It could be a nice morning walk if not for the
crowd of protesters who the player characters witness as
they come closer – there are many people gathered at the
prison’s gate. The atmosphere is tense, and it is evident
that these people are unsatisfied with something: they
shout and demand that the prison be moved outside
the city’s borders. However, they do not interfere if the
player characters approach the prison gate.
The weather, on the other hand, is getting worse.
When the player characters woke up in the morning,
it was just a bit cloudy, but now gray clouds cover most
of the sky, the wind is bitterly cold, and they get wet
and uncomfortable as the drizzle starts. The player
characters will likely wish to take shelter from this
unfriendly weather within the prison walls.

The player characters’ first stop is a security checkpoint.

prohibited items (page 101). The player characters
are thoroughly searched, and each suspicious item they
bring is questioned. They can try to conceal an item
if it is reasonably small by passing a STEALTH test. They
may also persuade the guards that an item is crucial to
their work by passing a MANIPULATION test.
