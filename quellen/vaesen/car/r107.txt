There is a first shift of 30 guards in the prison
when the player characters arrive. A second
shift of guards is coming the next day to replace
them. The guards are young to middle-­aged
men, mostly with good physiques and negative attitudes toward prisoners. They treat
prisoners harshly and justify their attitude
with the pressures of being short-­staffed in
an overpopulated penitentiary.
The guards are not completely loyal to the
new warden, Josef, and prefer old methods
of keeping everything safe, but they still follow his orders. Many of them attend Father
Hryhorii’s chapel and consider themselves
devoted Christians.
Nonetheless, player characters could make
OBSERVATION tests while talking to the guards
to learn that they are low-­paid and taking
bribes or even trading contraband inside of
the prison. They can even make MANIPULATION
tests to bribe the guards to get their help.

Eugene Shvets is a guard assigned to lead
the player characters through the prison’s
halls and corridors. At first glance, he is a
charming and helpful man just over 30, giving a tour that unravels the history and background of this prison. Eugene is trying to
build a connection with the player characters, flattering them and paying them compliments, but later into the tour, he shows
his true colors: those of a small man with an
eye towards power.
He is aggressive and displays dominance over prisoners at every opportunity, while also gossiping about the new
warden – Josef. He never refers to prisoners as fellow human beings, but performs
harsh cell searches, confiscates every personal item, and completely ignores their
complaints. His behavior heavily suggests
that the guards here beat the prisoners to
keep their mouths shut.
Eugene’s philosophy is that most of these
criminals are “not like us” – they made their
own bad choices and would do so again, and
the only way to change them is through severe
punishment that will make them fear ever
returning to prison. Unlike many of the
guards, Eugene does not attend chapel sermons and does not take bribes.
