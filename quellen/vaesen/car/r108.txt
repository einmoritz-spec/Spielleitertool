As the mystery unfolds, the conflicts will escalate step
by step. The following events can be used to increase
the pace of the game when necessary, and to push the
players toward the final confrontation. Not all steps
have to occur.
COUNTDOW N

1. Heavy dark clouds gather in the sky, and a hailstorm
starts, blocking all escape routes from the prison.
2. A new series of murders happens, and three more
people die. One of the victims is Oleksa, a terrified
prisoner who was a witness to a previous murder.
The vykhrovyk uses her magic to sow discord –
light sources go out, fog fills the corridors, and
spirits of the dead prisoners appear in their cells.
3. Murders and magic cause panic and riots in the
prison. The vykhrovyk’s storm breaks in, opening all the cell blocks and causing a prison break.
CATASTROPHE

Many prisoners and guards are killed, and dangerous convicts escape into the city. The strong winds
tear the warden into pieces. The vykhrovyk escapes,
infecting many people on her way out of town and
starting an epidemic. The city of Lemberg witnesses
the most powerful storm of the century that causes
massive destruction. The prison is eventually closed
and relocated to the outskirts of Lemberg.
