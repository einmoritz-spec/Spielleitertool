FATHER HRY HORII,
CH A PL A IN
THE EVENING SERMON

“Only the Lord has the power to judge.”

As evening falls, the bell rings, signaling the time
for the sermon. A selected group of prisoners is
taken out of their cells and accompanied by the
guards to the church. Due to extreme weather
conditions at this point, a couple of prisoners are
hurt by the intense hail, which causes fights with
the guards. The atmosphere is increasingly tense.
Once the group gets inside, one of the prisoners
curses the chaplain. “Why do we have to listen
to the old man’s empty words? We could’ve sat
comfortably in our cells, and nobody would get
hurt!” His words cause a new round of strikes
from the guards’ batons. Warden Strasser is not
in attendance, so the guards feel free to mistreat
the prisoners as much as they like.
Once everyone is gathered inside and
calmed down, Hryhorii gives a fiery speech
about the importance of faith, redemption, and
God’s judgment. He emphasizes that recent
tragedies are punishments from God for the
prisoners’ lack of atonement. The only way to
avoid further deaths is to redeem their sins, confess, and return to the light of the Lord. About
half of the prisoners seem to listen carefully,
while others spit on the ground or mutter quietly.
If they do not attend, the service allows the
player characters to sneak around while no one
is paying attention to them.

Father Hryhorii is a Catholic priest and
chaplain of Brygidki Prison. He is a charismatic man in his late 40s who claims to be
the hardest-­working person in the prison.
He says he is doing holy work among sinners,
saving this desecrated place from God’s wrath.
He interprets the vykhrovyk’s actions as the
Angel of Justice’s warnings to sinners, and he
spreads this myth to gain more authority and
power within the prison.
The chaplain believes only in God’s judgment and has a strong opinion of the place
where he is “conducting a holy mission.”
Father Hryhorii tries to look humble but is
obviously proud to be in the center of God’s
work. His true goal is to restore the former
monastery in Brygidki and move the prison
elsewhere, so he is challenging the authority
of the new warden, who wants to keep it here.
Father Hryhorii is a red herring in this
mystery. His extreme views on justice, his
desire to restore the monastery, and his
defiance of the warden’s authority will likely
attract the player characters’ suspicion.
