CH A LLENGES

The wind pierces to the very bones here, causing
the guts to feel frozen. It is cold, and the wind
acts like unseen fingers getting under the clothes,
touching, scratching, and grabbing every single
bit of warmth in one’s body. The player characters
will need to pass a Fear 1 test.
Cold: It is freezing here. Staying within the bell
tower for more than a short scene requires a successful FORCE test – otherwise, the player character
gets a Condition (physical or mental) and needs
to go down to warm up.
The Vykhrovyk: This is the nest of the vykhrovyk,
and soon after the player characters arrive here,
she will show herself – initially as a disembodied
spirit in the wind. Talking to the vykhrovyk without
calming her emotional state first is extremely challenging – the awakened spirit is anxious and consumed with rage. To get more than short answers
from the vaesen, and to avoid being attacked, the
player characters must pass a MANIPULATION test
and get at least one success, plus another success
for each countdown step (page 104) that has
already passed.

OLH A KOBZA R,
V Y KHROV Y K
“But if there is harm, then you shall pay life for life,
eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,
burn for burn, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.”
Olha is a former nun of the Bridgettine
nunnery who was accidentally killed by Josef
Strasser. She rose as a vengeful spirit who
wants to punish and torture criminals and
murderers and their souls and bring vengeance upon her killer. Though her memories
of the past have almost dissipated in a continuous whirlwind of emotions and senses, she
can recall some parts of her former life and
remembers that she is here to bring judgment
upon the unpunished. She can recall some
quotes from the Bible, her former name, and
her parents, but does not link those memories to herself, as it was another person’s life.
Now, she is a vaesen, and human ethics do
not apply to her or her actions.
Olha has the stats of a vykhrovyk (page
69). Remember that her MIGHT and MAGIC
scores are increased by +1 for each victim
she has killed. When the mystery starts, her
MIGHT and MAGIC have risen to 8. After step
#2 in the countdown, they rise to 11, and if
she claims any further victims, to 12 (and
she gains a second initiative card in combat.
Olha uses the following enchantments
and curses from the Vaesen core book (pages
118 and 119): Contact With the Great Beyond,
Darkness, Fog, Spread Disease, Storm,
Deadly Cold, Infect, and Wound.
