CLUES

The Murderer: If the player characters manage
to calm the vykhrovyk, they can engage in a brief
conversation. The spirit speaks with an unnatural voice, created through the bell and wind. She
senses that the person who killed her is here, in
the prison, but she cannot find him, as if he is
somehow shielded from her power. The vykhrovyk
grants the player characters three hours to find the
killer and bring him to the courtyard – other­wise,
she will take matters into her own hands.
Attack: If the player characters fail to calm the
spirit, they get short phrases instead: “GET OUT,”
“MURDERER,” “FIND HIM,” or “HIDES HERE.” If they
do not leave immediately, she transforms into her
full monstrous form – with a pig’s head and tail,
bat’s wings, several hands with long claws, and legs
and arms covered in tar – and attacks.

CONFRONTATION
The player characters’ investigation and visit in
Brygidki can pan out in many ways. Some possibilities are listed below:
The player characters can gather clues and successfully talk to the vykhrovyk to realize that Warden
Strasser has something to hide. To temporarily
calm the vaesen and be able to communicate with
her, they need to get a mistletoe plant or branch
and bring it to Olha’s grave. The subsequent confrontation will probably occur in the warden’s
office when the player characters ask him about
his crime, though he might come upon them at
Olha’s grave, drawn there by a guilty conscience.
The players decide to banish the vykhrovyk. To
achieve this, they must perform a ritual – throwing a sanctified knife into the whirlwind. This will
require getting such a weapon from one of the
guards or prisoners and persuading the prison’s
chaplain to sanctify it. Alternatively, player characters can sanctify it themselves if one of them is a
priest. The confrontation will probably take place

in the graveyard or the bell tower. The vykhrovyk
will confront the players if she sees the knife, or if

The player characters fail to gather enough information and bring a random person – such as the

to discover that the player characters are trying

vykhrovyk will go mad and confront the players.
The countdown will advance.

TA LKING TO OLH A
The player characters can gather mistletoe from the
courtyard tree. Once they bring it to the lonely grave,
the storm around the bell tower slowly subsides. The
vykhrovyk appears in her human form – a gentle, sorrowful image of a woman in monastic robes floating
in the air. Now, however, she remembers a lot about
her past life. In this calm state, she does not want Josef
Strasser dead – the spirit says that it will be enough if
he publicly confesses his crime.
Getting Strasser to do this will not be easy. He
regrets his crime, but he also wants to run the prison
and do something good in Brygidki after the previous
warden abused the role so cruelly. Depending on the
player characters’ approach and success with applicable MANIPULATION tests, Strasser will either coldly
ask them to do their job and banish the spirit, or he
will tell them why he is reluctant to fulfill Olha’s wish.
The player characters can try to persuade the warden, intimidate him, or use the chaplain’s documents,
including confessions and monastery records, as
leverage to convince him.

BA NISHING THE V Y KHROV Y K
To prepare a sanctified knife, the player characters
must obtain a knife, which they can take from a guard
or from one of the prisoners if they make a good
impression on them.
