AFTERMATH
The outcome of the mystery will have considerable consequences for both Brygidki and the NPCs
involved.
If the player characters accept the vykhrovyk’s
ultimatum and bring the warden to the wind’s
judgment, the vykhrovyk kills Josef and leaves

·

·

the prison, going forth to wander the world and
collect souls.
If the player characters persuade or manipulate the
warden to talk to the vykhrovyk and admit his guilt
at Olha’s grave site, he confesses his crime before
everyone. Strasser survives and goes to jail, and
the vykhrovyk turns into a harmless spirit of air.
If the player characters banish the vaesen using
the information found in the library, peace is
restored in the prison, and Warden Strasser keeps
his position.
If the vykhrovyk gets enough power (the player
characters decide not to stop it or fail in the
confrontation), the events of the catastrophe
come to pass.

The player characters have little left to do but return to
their headquarters in Prague, where they are awarded
an appropriate number of experience points.
