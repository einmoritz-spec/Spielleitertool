THE SECRET
OF THE MAHARAL
This mystery takes the player characters to 19th
century Prague – a city of many cultures and, in a
way, a city of many cities, consisting of several diverse
urban entities merged together.

PRELUDE
“People are dying in Prague.”
Such information may not have been unusual – after
all, Prague is a large city in the 19th century. However,
it is notable for the nature of the victims’ injuries,

indicating the use of enormous force. One might
even say, unnaturally great force. People of different communities are being killed, triggering fears of
religious unrest. All the victims have only one thing
in common – they were killed at night.
Virtually helpless, the city police are turning to
various associations and groups – including kabbalists, spiritualists, and experts in ancient cultures and
beliefs – for help. The city is on the verge of declaring
a state of emergency and sending in the army – after
all, riots could break out at any moment. Some think
that the crimes are religiously motivated, but not even
the faintest of clues has been discovered.
