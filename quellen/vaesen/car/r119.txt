CONFLICT

SECONDA RY CONFLICT

The central conflict concerns a private argument from
250 years ago – between Rabbi Judah Loew ben Bezalel
and a Catholic priest by the name of Andrzej Krasowski.
For a good part of his long life, Loew lived in Poznań,
where he served as chief rabbi of the Greater Poland
region. Poznań was a well-­known center of Kabbalism,
where some famous adepts received their training.
At the same time, the priest Andrzej Krasowski at
St. Adalbert’s parish was said to be practicing magic.
He was disturbing Jewish funerals. When a funeral
procession passed under the windows of the rectory –
it was the only way from that quarter to the cemetery –
the priest would lean out of the window to cast a spell.
The deceased would then rise from their coffin and
confess their sins. The spell was only undone when
the mourners paid the ransom.
On one occasion, the priest leaned out of the window to cast the spell, as he always did, but the rabbi was
quicker – with the muttering of Hebrew words, the clay
used in the building’s construction shifted as if alive,
and constricted around the priest so that the window
was too small for him to pull his head back inside.
Only after the priest promised to give all the money
he had taken back to the Jews did the rabbi undo the
spell. The priest swore revenge. Meanwhile, the rabbi,
Judah Loew ben Bezalel, left permanently for Prague
to become chief rabbi there. In Prague, he became
famous for his great wisdom and for the creation of a
golem – a creature made of clay and silt from the Vltava
River, brought to life by powerful magic. For this, the
rabbi was later called “the Maharal of Prague.”
His golem was supposed to be utterly obedient to
its creator and would serve to protect the Jewish people
from persecution. However, something went wrong –
various reasons are given in legends. The golem went
on a destructive rampage, and Bezalel had to destroy
his creation. The rabbi lived to a ripe old age and after
his death was laid to rest in one of Prague’s cemeteries.
Little did he know, however, that his old nemesis,
the priest from Poznań, had also moved to Prague in
an unsuccessful attempt to repeat the rabbi’s spectacular achievement in creating the golem.

250 years have passed and now the second conflict is
related to the situation in the multinational city, one
shared by Czechs, Germans, and Jews. It turned out
that the rabbi’s opponent was much closer to success
than he thought. Shortly before the start of this mystery, a young Czech researcher into the history and
traditions of the Slavs, named Jan Černý (a descendant of the old priest), came across his ancestor’s
workshop hidden within the Church of Our Lady
of the Snows.
Černý decided to complete the work so that the
new “anti-­golem” could stand alongside the Czechs
in their future struggle for an independent state. To
his astonishment, he succeeded where his ancestor
had failed. What he didn’t know, however, was that the
creature was flawed and no one was able to control it.
When Černý attempted to deactivate the anti-­
golem, he was killed by it, and the creature began a
nightly rampage throughout the streets of Prague.
The creature started its killing spree, one which will
undoubtedly lead to massive riots. In the daytime, it
rests in the crypt under the church.

IN V ITATION
The player characters are invited because they are
members of the Pravda Society, which is mostly
secret to the public. Some of them may be known
for their interest in ancient folklore, which the
German city administration considers superstition
and does not take as a serious subject. The Pravda
Society also occasionally cooperates with the locals
and the authorities.
A young clerk working for the chief of police, Otto
Grabeck, comes to the Powder Gate and asks the Society members for help. He offers a monetary reward,
but more important than that, he tells them that if
they succeed in stopping the killing spree, they will
be lauded as the city’s heroes who have brought peace
and thus can count on the benefits of fame.
The clerk invites the characters to meet Grabeck
at the Municipal Police Garrison to learn more.
