CATASTROPHE

The situation in Prague is dire and getting worse by
the hour. The countdown is directly connected to
the moves of the anti-­golem. Its first random victims
were a group of Jewish worshipers, triggering fears
of a pogrom. The next group of victims happened to
be Czech, leading to rumors of revenge killings. As
the situation evolved, it became more and more dire,
and no one feels safe anymore. The communities lock
themselves up, and prominent figures demand that
the police take action to stop the killings.
Soon, the risk of riots grows. Jewish communities
organize militias to protect themselves. This action
leads to confrontations with other communities in
Prague, who, in turn, organize their own militias.
As murders continue, violence escalates to the point
where the whole city riots and innocent blood flows in
the streets. Overwhelmed police are asking for military help, and the army on city streets never means
anything good.

Full-­fledged riots fill the streets. People kill each other
in local skirmishes all around the city. The military
uses full force to stop the unrest, and there is blood
in the streets. If the Society does not stop the anti-­
golem, the creature will eventually face the military
in the streets in a bloody battle.

LOCATIONS
The following paragraphs summarize the most
important locations the player characters may explore
in the city. See the map on page 118.
If the characters wish to investigate other locations,
you are free to improvise, but it is perfectly alright to
simply explain that the place in question is not part
of the mystery and that there is nothing for them
there. Make sure that the game does not come to a
halt because the players are unsure where to go next.
Use the countdown to keep the plot moving forward.

COUNTDOW N

1. After the most recent killing, a protest against
the inability of the police to stop the murders is
dispersed with force by the city garrison, commanded by colonel Bernd Ebersbach (page 119).
The player characters can witness the event.
2. At night, a new murder occurs by the riverbank
just south of the National Theater in Nové Město
district, opposite the Žofín Palace which is located
on a small island in the river. This time, the anti-­
golem attacked a group of Czech merchants who
stayed too long in one of the restaurants and were
coming home late at night. They were massacred,
some torn limb from limb. See New Murder Scene
on page 120.
3. The riverbank murder triggers violent riots in
the streets. The military imposes martial law and
colonel Bernd Ebersbach takes command of the
streets. The player characters, like all other inhabitants, are stopped at military checkpoints in the
streets and risk being caught up in the violence.

CENTRAL & PERIPHERAL CLUES
The clues that the player characters can find
are divided into central and peripheral ones.
Central clues are vital for solving the mystery
– peripheral clues are not, but provide additional information. The players do not need to
be handed central clues without effort, but there
are several ways to access them in the adventure.
Central clues are marked with a in the text.
