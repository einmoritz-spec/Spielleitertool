players of the location of the most recent murder.

the Jewish district of Prague. The victims were a

some struggle marks on the husband suggest that
he was fighting the assailant. David Soukup at the
City Morgue can give more information about the
deaths. There were no witnesses. It was surprising, though, that the victims were not robbed.
