DAV ID SOUKUP

The commander of the city garrison is a colonel in the Imperial Army, age 48. He is a
brutal man who takes his duty very seriously
– he will go to any lengths to quell the unrest
in the streets of Prague.

The city morgue does not have a permanent
staff member responsible for dissections
and determining causes of death. Soukup, a
trained surgeon, fills the role of the city doctor who gives opinions in courts, at administrative cases, and presides over the city
morgue, determining causes of death other
than dull “freezing, death by starvation or
drowning in the Vltava.” He compares the
new mysterious victims to heavy industry
accident victims – their injuries are similar to
those resulting from being hit by a machine
that weighs several hundred kilograms. As a
scientist, the doctor does not believe in any
“supernatural” powers but is willing to provide player characters with any information
regarding the victims.

large room with arched vaults. A row of stone tables

never be established – those are then buried in nameless graves in the town cemetery. The last victim of
the anti-­golem can be found here, in the city morgue.
