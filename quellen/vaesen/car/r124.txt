CLUES

The Victims: The player characters can get
detailed information on how the murder victims
died. All of them were attacked with brute, almost
inhuman strength. Their bones are shattered,
and some died from the impact alone. However,
others are practically ripped apart. These are the
ones who apparently tried to defend themselves.
The Mud: Stuck under the nails of some of the
victims was clay or mud, probably from the river.
It is strange, as some of the victims were killed
some distance from Vltava.
The Dust: There is something else that the doctor found striking. He noticed bits of dust on the
victims’ clothes, a mix of old stone and bone dust.
This is very strange, as the victims were killed far
from any cemetery, and none worked as gravediggers or had any immediate access to old bones.
This could lead the player characters to search for
a burial ground, perhaps some crypt.

L ATEST MURDER SCENE
The latest murder scene is located in Josefov, the
Jewish district of Prague. The victims, Simeon and
Asa Altschul, were a respected Jewish couple. The
murder took place in a narrow alley at the edge of the
district. It all happened so quickly that nobody even
heard anything. Therefore, there are no witnesses.
The police didn’t do a thorough search of the area.

NEW MURDER SCENE
As step #2 of the countdown, a new attack occurs by
the riverbank just south of the National Theater in
the Nové Město district, opposite the Žofín Palace in
the river. This time, the anti-­golem attacked a group
of Czech merchants who stayed too long in one of
the restaurants and were coming home late at night.
They were massacred, some torn limb from limb.
Police arrive quickly, but their job is sloppy at best.
After the attack, the player characters can investigate this usually crowded place, but most of the
clues were already destroyed by the police and later
by onlookers.
