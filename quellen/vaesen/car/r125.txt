THE SEW ERS
Prague has a sewer system and storm sewers like any
other large, modern European city. Of course, these
only cover some of the city, but one can navigate them
without drawing much, if any, attention to oneself.
However, a special police permit must be obtained
– otherwise, anyone wandering in the sewers may
end up arrested. The known exits from the sewers
(including the ones by the river) are patrolled by the
police (mainly to catch loiterers and criminals who
might be hiding there).
