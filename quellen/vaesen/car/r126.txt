This necropolis, which dates back to the Middle Ages,
is full of tombstones. Among them is the tombstone

Getting In: If player characters visit the cemetery
at night, they might have to break in (STEALTH test)
and thus get into some trouble with the cemetery
guard. He is open to bribery or a MANIPULATION test.

STA RONOVA SY NAGOGUE
The oldest synagogue in modern Europe, it is located
in the Josefov District – now a Jewish quarter and before
that, an independent town. It was built in 1270 and was
spared from significant cataclysms, so it has retained
much of its medieval character. In the attic is the alleged
studio of the long-­dead Rabbi Judah Loew ben Bezalel. The current chief rabbi, Gedalya Isaaman, will
greet the players characters if they visit the synagogue.
