This very old man and Orthodox Jew is well
acquainted with the legend of the golem, but
initially, he does not connect current events
with the legend from three centuries ago. If
a player character, or an NPC in their company, is of Jewish background and familiar
with the community’s customs, the rabbi is
helpful. If not, he will require some serious
persuasion.

KABBALAH AND VAESEN
Kabbalah is one of the oldest types of magic.
To master it requires decades of study not only
of the Torah but also the writings of scholars
(kabbalists). It is based on completely different
mechanisms than typical European magic and
thus requires entirely different study methods.
Kabbalah uses incantations (“words” or “formulas”) written on parchment. The incantations
thus crafted can be used in a variety of ways.
However, the Jewish community closely guards
the secrets of Kabbalah, and Kabbalists are
held in high esteem.
