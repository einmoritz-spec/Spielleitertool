AFTERMATH
Solving this mystery quickly stops the anti-­golem’s
killings and eventually quietens the community. If
player characters deactivate the anti-­golem and dispose of its remains, there are no negative consequences to their actions.
If they decide to take a more dangerous approach,
such as using fire or explosives, they might get on the
bad side of the local Catholic church and probably
also the municipality. Proving that it was necessary to
stop more violence from happening will calm things…
a little. If everything is resolved peacefully, Otto Grabeck is grateful to the player characters. The Jewish
community may also be thankful, depending on how
they managed the case.
Finally, the player characters can return to their
headquarters and get their well-­earned experience
points.
