FACILITIES
These Upgrades are rooms and functions in the Powder Gate.
A RMORY

Over the centuries of its activity, Pravda has collected numerous
melee weapons, thrown weapons, and firearms. Some pieces have
a purely decorative function – others are entirely usable. Several
modern high-­powered hunting rifles are also in stock. The Society
purchased these from famous London gunsmiths on the pretext of
hunting elephants.
Prerequisite: Resources 2
Development Points: 4 (can be purchased up to
five times)
Function: During the preparation of a mystery, allows
the player characters to bring any weapons of their
choosing (temporary gear) at a total cost of up to 3
per rank in this Facility, without using Resources.

PA NOPTICUM

A cabinet of curiosities displaying rare, exotic, or frightening things.
Open to the public.
Prerequisite: Room on the first floor.
Development Points: 3
Function: Gives the player characters 2 points of
extra Capital to spend during the preparation
phase of each mystery.
SECRET LIBR A RY

Grimoires collected over the centuries lie in trunks brought from
various caches in the Sudetenland. They could be organized into a
neat library, with books available only for members of the Society
who are privy to vaesen matters.
Prerequisite: Room on the second or third floor.
Development Points: 1
Function: A list of books available in the Secret
Library can be found on pages 31–32.

EVACUATION ROUTE

Due to the Gate’s specific design, an additional staircase can be created,
passing through its first three floors. There is no exit at street level; it is
instead located in the network of storm drain tunnels under the Old Town.
Prerequisite: Resources 5
Development Points: 8
Function: The tunnels lead straight to the Vltava
River, with several exits at various points in the
Old Town.
