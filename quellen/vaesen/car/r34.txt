AV I ATION SOCIET Y

Balloon flights are organized outside Prague’s walls, on Vitkov Hill
(within the town of Žižkov). They are operated by the Aviation
Society, which is happy to make their balloons – mainly produced
by German and French manufacturers – available for a fee. They
dream of building the first Czech balloon.
Prerequisite: None
Development Points: 6
Function: This enables the player characters to hire
a balloon that, given the right weather conditions, will take them up to 200 kilometers from
Prague in a single day, even to places the railway does not reach. It also makes it possible to
observe the surroundings from a great height
and gather information impossible to obtain
otherwise. Note that this mode of transport is
highly visible and attracts considerable attention from locals.

Prague is well-­known for its moving puppets, which are used in theaters (including street theaters) and as children’s toys in many homes.
Prerequisite: Resources 3
Development Points: 2
Function: Once per mystery players can use a specially crafted puppet, which will play the role of a
“scarecrow,” posing as a human being, for example as bait.
USURER

In the dark corners of Prague, one can find people who are in the
business of lending money at high interest rates. It is possible to get
even large sums of money from them in a very short time.
Prerequisite: None
Development Points: 4
Function: Once per mystery, one player character
gains up to 3 points of extra Capital to spend. A
cost of 4 must be incurred to repay the loan and
interest at the start of the next mystery.

JUNIOR CLERK ERIK ERTL

Commandant of the Cadet School, Johann von Redd German is an
aristocrat at the rank of colonel. He is a middle-­aged man who is
not thrilled with his role as commander of what he believes to be a
provincial cadet school, but he is trying to enjoy life.
Prerequisite: A roleplayed scene of getting acquainted
with the commandant.
Development Points: 2
Function: The player characters gain access to the
rooms of the Cadet School, some of which they
can rent for an affordable fee.

Prerequisite: Available from the beginning.
Development Points: 0
Function: Handles administrative matters and takes
care of the premises.
