home. If I would have seen those fangs that he was hiding.

and him laughing, biting, and biting again, and pushing
me to the floor – refusing to kill me, even after all was done
and I begged him to do so. Now I seek him and others like
him – it is I who come knocking on his door. And I bring
knives, stakes, and holy crosses, and it is he who will beg on
his knees for me to spare his unlife. I will grant no mercy.
