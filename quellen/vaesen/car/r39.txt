Baba Yaga is a powerful vaesen from Eastern Europe
who appears as an old, frightening woman with iron
teeth and a bone leg. Once, she was revered as a forest
goddess, with people offering sacrifices and praying
for the health of their children or abundant harvests. With the rise of Christianity, she suffered the
fate of many old idols – demonization and eventual
obscurity. Now she lives deep in the forest, in a hut
that stands on chicken legs, harboring resentment
towards humankind that she once helped. At night,
she enjoys flying in her giant mortar, summoning
storms or abducting children – some say she eats them.
Baba Yaga’s hut stands where the boundary between
the living and the dead is thinnest. Yaga acts as a guide
to the great beyond and knows its secrets. Those who
find her may bargain for power and knowledge, but the
price is always steep – often the most precious things
people possess: parts of their soul, memories, or years
of their lives. Since she’s still embittered by humanity’s
“recent” betrayal, every deal with her carries hidden
dangers. Yet, once a bargain is struck, Baba Yaga always

keeps her word. She can be a powerful ally or a dangerous foe, depending on what people are willing to give.

RITUA L
The stove inside Baba Yaga’s hut can be turned into
a weapon against her. Lure her into the stove and
quickly shut the door to seal her inside. Baba Yaga
will be trapped, unable to use her powers to escape.
