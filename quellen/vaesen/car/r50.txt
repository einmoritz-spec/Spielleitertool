The Society receives an invitation to investigate
the mysterious disappearance of factory workers
in Prague. Local police found no signs of bodies.
According to the missing workers’ friends, they
had all been recently fired by the factory owner –
he said that he planned to close the factory, but
they suspected something was wrong and wanted
to investigate. What happened is that the factory
owner discovered a Jewish manuscript in his family library, which explained how to create a golem.
He pretended to close the factory, laid off all the
workers, and replaced them with a golem. All was

A woman lost her husband in a long battle with
illness. Her mourning was interrupted in a week
by a letter from her deceased husband. It said he
had left “a helper,” something that would “protect
her, no matter what happens.” In the basement,
she found a golem and instructions for animating it. So she followed her husband’s wishes. The
golem was helpful and as time passed, the woman,
devastated by grief, started seeing the golem as
her deceased husband returned from death to
be with her again. Waiting for love that a golem
can never provide, she convinced herself that he
had lost his memories, and started teaching him
emotions. The situation worsened when the golem
learned to be jealous and protective against other
men, injuring those who sought the attention of
his mistress. No one believed one of these admirers, who claimed to have fought with the woman’s
long-­deceased husband, so the man asked the
Society for help to determine what was going on.
