HOMUNCULUS

A homunculus is an artificial living being created by
humans using magic and science. A creature is considered a homunculus if it was born without the participation of either God or a woman’s womb. Some
homunculi can see into the future, while others can
see and speak with spirits and
demons and share their powers with their master. Ambitious
alchemists all over the world try
to create homunculi, because
they have mystical powers and
secret knowledge that they can
share with their creators. But
some fear and despise such
ambition because those attempts
are already an act of defying God
by creating life without a soul.
In the writings De natura rerum
(“The Nature of Things”), the
alchemist and physician Paracelsus describes a procedure for
creating a homunculus. The
process involves maintaining a
man’s sperm at a warm temperature using decomposing horse
manure, allowing it to mature
within a sealed glass container
for a minimum of 40 days.
Once it has reached a grotesque
human fetus form, it must be
regularly fed with blood, and 40
weeks later, it will become a fully
developed homunculus. It can
be induced to continue to grow

as large as a human or even larger if the flask has
enough space. Once the homunculus is out of the
flask, it can no longer grow. Alchemists often use
homunculi as their servants in secret laboratories,
hiding them from society when possible.
