After a new mine opened near a major city, it
grew rapidly, with people coming in from nearby
villages. Many of these new arrivals brought with
them new traditions. The city authorities decided
to build a sewage system in the area of the city where
the new arrivals settled. But as soon as the construction began, strange things started to happen.
Mining tools were damaged, seemingly gnawed
by rats. Meanwhile, the excavations uncovered
underground caverns full of teeth, and the teeth
of everyone close to that place began to hurt and
fall out. Gradually, the sewers are approaching the
khokha-­yarokhas’ lair, and the vaesen are united
to fight back.
