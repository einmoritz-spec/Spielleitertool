An isolated fishing village deep in the woods is suffering from misfortunes: boats are destroyed and
fishing nets are torn. This is caused by rusalki, who
are greatly displeased. Until recently, the village used
to honor the ancient tradition of “Rusalki’s Easter,”
in which the prettiest girl in the village had to be sacrificed to the water. But this year, the celebration
went wrong: Oleh, the son of the village elder, was
in love with Maryna, the girl chosen for the sacrifice.
He decided to free her and is now hiding with her
within the village, planning their escape together.
In a small village, a local pastor’s family strives to
spread Christianity and fight the local superstitions.
His son decides to further prove the point by marrying during a prohibited time – “Rusalki’s Week.”
A local witch writes a letter to the Society, pleading
