Strigoi, relatives of vampires, are most often found
in the territory of Romania. Their appearance can
range from human-­like to ugly and monstrous, often
with goat hooves and a tail. Strigoi can rise for various reasons, such as a curse or an unfair execution,
but the common feature of these people is their evil,
envious, or sinful ways of life before death.
The personality of strigoi will also depend on how
they lived as humans. Robbers and scoundrels usually
turn into aggressive and bloodthirsty monsters that
attack nearby villages, kill people, and drink their
blood. Sinners and envious people return as strigoi
who can feed not only on blood, but also on the surrounding energy from people, animals, food, and
nature, and even sell this energy to others.
Despite the diversity of strigoi, they share some
common traits and abilities. At night, they can turn
into any animal or person. Their bodies in the graves
are usually red and full of blood, and their nails and
hair continue to grow long after death. Strigoi also
typically stay close to their former families or homes,
and return to these places to feed, plaguing those
who live there.
