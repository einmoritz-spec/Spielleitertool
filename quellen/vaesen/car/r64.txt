traditions of burial, which had been strangely adapted to the Christian tradition. People of

The most drastic measures that I heard of were decapitation of the corps e and placing the

There are many ways to become a vampir, or vampire as
they are also known. Some people may be fated from
birth to return as undead. Others may be condemned
to rise from their graves due to their wicked lives. Furthermore, the curse may be passed from a vampire to its
victim, willingly or accidentally. Regardless of the reason
for returning as a vampire, the person is doomed to eternal unlife with a permanent hunger for human blood.
There are many different local versions of the
­vampir, including the strigoi (page 55) and the
nach­zehrer (page 51). There are no general rules
regarding the appearance of a vampire, as different people may return as vampires. Although their
immortality influences their aging, their mind may
stagnate, making them archaic in their beliefs and
way of life. Others, as they become older, may lose
human traits and gain chimerical and monstrous
appearances. The diet of a vampire also has an impact.
The better it feeds, the more human it looks. Some
might even be warm and blush as if still alive.
Becoming an undead gives a vampire many traits and
magical powers. Besides being immortal and stronger
than average humans, vampires can turn into animals
or mist, become invisible even to those with the Sight,
charm and control people’s minds, and have many

other powers they use to survive and feed. Combined
with their long life experience, these powers make them
cunning, dangerous, and difficult to identify and kill.

Despite their diverse powers, vampires have flaws
common to all undead, which may be used to weaken
them. They cannot tolerate sunlight but won’t burn
when exposed to it. They don’t like steel, garlic, the
Bible, the crucifix, and the Psalms. Some people
drink a vampire’s blood mixed with the soil from
its grave to become protected from vampiric magic.
However, the most effective way to fight and defeat a
vampire is an ash, hawthorn, or aspen stake or spike
shoved into its chest or head.
