VODIANYK
To learn more about the undead, I delved into stories about where they come from. Over and
over, I heard about one spirit in particular that is not undead by nature but somehow rais es
and rules over drowned ones—the Vodianyk. All through my journey, fishermen warned me
that the Lord of the Rivers is compelling and can grant wishes, but it’s dangerous to even listen
to his words because they are full of lies, and it is mortally dangerous to accept his offers.
Almost every fisherman to the east of the San pays their fee and releas es their first catch back
into the river—as their way of paying respect to traditions founded by Vodianyk. I wish I
could encounter this creature in my future travels.

—Antoine Augustin Calmet, July 1746

Vodianyk is a tricky and malicious spirit, often associated with freshwater bodies such as rivers, lakes, and
ponds. The bigger and older the body of water, the
more powerful Vodianyk is. He resembles a green-­
skinned old man with a fish tail, frog-­like webs
between his toes, a catfish mustache, a long green
beard twined with algae, and other fishlike traits.
He is known for granting wishes and luring people into making blood contracts with him. Those
tricky deals often result in humans drowning and
becoming Vodianyk’s servants – utopce (drowners)
or rusalki, who often become his wives or daughters. Unlike the rusalki, he can freely step on land
but prefers a domain of his own. He sleeps during
winter and wakes up hungry, causing floods and
breaking dams. He likes to make fun of fishermen
by turning into a giant catfish and flipping boats
or getting caught in their nets and scaring them.
But even though he is a wicked spirit, Vodianyk
still respects traditions and obeys laws and contracts of his own.

against building the dam and destroying the river’s natural flow. To settle this conflict, Vodianyk

A little girl has found Vodianyk and made a deal
with him to grant her a wish. Later, her family
learns about “grandpa who makes wishes come
true.” Rumors spread fast, and eventually, almost
everyone in the village has made a deal with Vodianyk. Slowly, those who made this deal are starting
to change, gaining a fish-­like appearance. Some
accept it as a price for their wishes, but some are
afraid and eager to break the deal, so the tensions
between the villagers increase.
