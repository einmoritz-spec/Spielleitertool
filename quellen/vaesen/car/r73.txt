VYKHROVYK
When I journeyed to the Carpathian Mountains to study the local undead spirits, I noticed
that Oleksa, my Hutsul companion who showed me the way, always carried a strange stick
with him. It was made of elder wood and had a copper head in the shape of a horrible
monster who res embled a winged old man with his insides out. Oleksa said that this stick
protected us from the vykhrovyks, undead air spirits, and was made in their likeness.
—Antoine Augustin Calmet, October 1749
Vykhrovyks are Slavic spirits of air and whirlwinds, born
of children who were cursed or renounced by their parents. They most commonly appear as whirlwinds but
can also take the shape of winged serpents. Occasionally, a vykhrovyk assumes its true form – an old person
without a belly, exposing its guts. They have thick black
fur, a pig’s head and tail, bat’s wings, several hands with
long claws, and their legs and arms are covered in tar.
Vykhrovyks live in the fields and mountains. They
favor crossroads and field edges where unbaptized
children have been buried.
Despite their old appearance, vykhrovyks act like
children, with childish playfulness and cruelty. They
have an immature notion of justice and tend to see
everything in black and white. These wandering spirits collect the souls of criminals to torture them by
tearing them apart with strong winds. As they do
this, they may ruin houses and uproot trees, cause
drought, and spread diseases.
These spirits are most active during daytime, which
makes them different from the rest of the undead.

If a vykhrovyk is hit with a sanctified knife, it loses
all the souls it collected, and the knife pins it to the
ground. It is unable to move until the knife is removed.
A stick made of elder wood can conceal the owner
from vykhrovyk’s senses.

In the Carpathian Mountains, a rumor spreads
about a great winged serpent ruining homes and
stealing people from nearby villages. The truth
is that the local molfar’s apprentice, who wanted
to help his fellow villagers, stole a magical source
of water from the vykhrovyk’s territory. The spirit
seeks retribution for trespassing.
