Some mirrors are inhabited by a zerkalytsia (or dzerkalnitsia) – a dead person’s soul who was trapped in
the mirror, becoming its mistress and guardian of
the gates between realms. Zerkalytsias typically act as
spirit protectors of the house from evil beings, but
they can be corrupted by dark magic and haunt people. A zerkalytsia doesn’t like changes in the household, such as new family members, or moving into
different rooms or new houses.
A zerkalytsia takes on the appearance of what she
sees in front of it. But her terrifying true form can
only be seen if one looks in the mirror at night: a
pale person who seems to be constantly breaking into
pieces like a reflection in a broken mirror, with mirror shards carved into their skin. Zerkalytsias hate
the presence of other vaesen in the house – as a result,
their mirror does not reflect vaesen and she does not
take their appearance. Typically, a zerkalytsia’s soul is
bound to one mirror, but it can easily travel between
mirrors and drag people into the Mirror realm.

A new mirror maze has opened in Prague. It attracts
many visitors, but some of them have disappeared
inside. The reason for this is a conflict between two
zerkalytsias. One appeared because of an accident at a
glass working factory – a worker died and was trapped
in a new mirror. The other one came into the maze
from the Mirror realm portal, which opened because
many mirrors looked at each other. The two overprotective spirits started competing over territory,
cursing each other and imprisoning people. The
maze’s director invites the Society to intervene and
solve the mystery of the disappearances.
A letter comes from a married couple. Something
is haunting the wife and child in their house. Not
too long ago, the spirit of the husband’s dead wife
was trapped in the mirror and became a zerkalytsia. She knew about her husband’s affair while
she was alive and now is angered by him bringing
his lover to their home. She wants her husband
to join her in the Mirror realm and terrorizes his
new family more severely each day.
