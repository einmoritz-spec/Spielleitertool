A TREATY OF BLOOD

This mystery leads the characters into a village in
a secluded valley, deep in the Carpathian mountains
in Transylvania. It is winter, and harsh weather conditions quickly trap the player characters inside. All
the while, a conflict brews around them between the
vampiric knez of the region, his defiant dhampir
daughter, and the vârcolac, a caged monster biding
its time, waiting for a chance to escape.
As is common, in this feud, the people of the land
suffer the worst. The Pravda Society must quickly
understand what is going on, find out the truth about
the past crimes at the heart of this story, and find a
solution before the valley is drenched in blood.

PRELUDE
This first section describes the background of the
mystery and the conflicts on which it is based. This
is followed by an invitation to kick off the session
and a description of the journey to Transylvania. It
concludes with a countdown of events which you as
Game­master will initiate at some appropriate time
during the mystery and a catastrophe that describes
what happens if the player characters do not take
action or fail in their efforts. But first we will look
back at the events leading up to the mystery.
