THE SOCIET Y A RRI V ES

About a hundred years ago, several murders occurred
in the valley. A dhampir son of the knez, who lived
as an outcast, died and turned into a vampire. The
knez tried to calm his son and teach him how to live
after death, but failed. The new vampire was violent
in his desire to get revenge on the valley’s inhabitants
for all the pain and mistreatment he and his mother
had experienced during his life. Since the knez could
not allow himself to kill another immortal, his family
invited the Pravda Society to intervene.
The Pravda Society representatives arrived at the
valley and successfully captured and killed the ravaging vampire. However, they discovered that he had
been a dhampir before transforming into a vampire, and their further investigation brought them
to Knez Vlad. During the confrontation with the
knez, opinions among the Pravda Society members
diverged strongly. The knez persuaded most of the
Pravda Society members that he was a reasonable ruler,
compensating for his vampiric nature.
A young priest with the Pravda Society, Karel
Benes, disagreed. He believed that the knez needed
to pay for his crimes and that all undead should be
put to rest, as their existence violated the laws of God.
When tensions grew high, a conflict sparked, and the
priest was accidentally killed by a fellow member of
the Society. After that incident, the Society left the
valley and never returned, hoping to forget the entire
incident for good.

A few decades ago, a seamstress named Maria caught
the eye of the vampire knez. His affections were
returned by Maria, but her parents were against the
marriage. The rejections only inflamed the knez’s
desire to win her hand. During a conversation with
the vârcolac, he shared his concerns and received an
unexpected offer that led to a plan.
One night, the vârcolac was set free and attacked
Maria’s house. It killed her parents and seemed
about to kill her as well when the knez appeared, all
according to their plan. He fought the vârcolac, who

secretly believed it might be able to trick the knez
and escape. After the fight ended, the knez returned
the vârcolac to its dungeon and then went back to
Maria’s house and took her, scared and grieving,
to his castle.
As the knez slept in his coffin, Maria spent her
days exploring the castle alone. One day, she found
the vârcolac in human form, imprisoned and weakened, and felt sorry for it. The vârcolac saw in Maria
another chance to escape and told her about the true
nature of Knez Vlad.
It revealed that it was the knez who created the
plan to win her hand, forcing the vârcolac to kill
her parents. Maria was devastated by the truth and
decided to run away. Despite the vârcolac’s manipulations, she refused to let it free but accepted a lock
of its hair as a memento.
M A RI A’S REV ENGE

Maria escaped from the valley with the aid of a wandering theater company – the Wallachia Mystique
Extravaganza – that was visiting the area at that time.
Soon after her escape, she gave birth to a daughter, a
dhampir she named Eve. While with the theater company, Maria studied occult lore in Prague, Vienna,
and other cities, discovered the dhampiric nature
of her daughter, and learned more about Knez Vlad
and the beast he kept under the castle.
Over the decades that followed, Maria found out
that dhampirs’ lives are shorter than those of humans,
and that after death they are doomed to become vampires. She also learned that a vârcolac could be controlled by spinning a yarn from its hair or fur.
Now, Maria has devised a dangerous plan. Using
her spindle of yarn, she has released the vârcolac from
its dungeon, to terrify the villagers and turn them
against the knez. She then traveled back to the Bralnic Valley with the theater company in time for the
yearly celebrations of the knez’s victory over the beast.
The time has come for Maria to get revenge against
Knez Vlad for the deaths of her parents and to overthrow him to guarantee a safe unlife for her daughter’s eventual transformation.
