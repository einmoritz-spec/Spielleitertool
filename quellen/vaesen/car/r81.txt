CONFLICTS

IN V ITATION

The primary conflict of this mystery is between Maria
and Knez Vlad. In her old family house, now ruined,
Maria has used a spinning wheel with a spindle to spin
a thread with the lock of the vârcolac’s hair, which
granted it the power to escape and binds it to her
will. Maria wants to get her revenge on the knez and
to ensure a safe unlife in the valley for her daughter
Eve after she becomes a vampire. She uses the vârcolac to terrify the villagers, forcing them to break
the ancient agreement with Knez Vlad and setting
up her daughter as an heir to his title.
Eve knows nothing about the plan but is guided by
her mother’s prophecies. She believes it is her destiny
to defeat the evil in this valley, which she thinks is Knez
Vlad. However, Knez Vlad wants to retain the status quo.
He is bound by the treaty to protect the valley but does
not know that someone is behind the vârcolac’s escape,
and he’s unable to track or kill the creature. Thus, Knez
Vlad takes a risk and asks the Pravda Society for help.

Knez Vlad becomes aware of the killings in the valley and has discovered the vârcolac’s absence from
the dungeons. As he is not able to find the vârcolac
and does not know that the creature is under Maria’s
control, the knez takes a risk by writing a letter to the
Society he encountered a few centuries ago (handout 1A
on page 127, also available for download on the Free
League website). The letter is sealed with a wax stamp
showing a warrior killing a wolf-­like beast. It is written
in elegant handwriting on expensive French paper.

The secondary conflict reflects the central one but
represents the people of the valley. There are two
groups: the knez’s family, who were raised in the castle
and returned to the village, and the regular villagers.
The knez’s family constitutes the minority and consists of only nine people, but they have better status
as they are educated and have been granted land and
support from the knez.
The rest of the villagers are ordinary people who
are not privileged but live in safety and with their
basic needs satisfied. They grew up hearing stories
about Knez Vlad the First and respect the current
knez. However, some are unsatisfied with the current
social hierarchy, as it is almost impossible for someone outside the knez’s family to earn any position of
authority in the village.
The recent killings and the inability of the knez
to fulfill his part of the agreement have resulted in a
state of smoldering discontent, encouraged by Eve
– who is at the heart of this conflict. Her reaction to
learning the truth about the knez can determine the
fate of the village.

To The Honorable Pravda Society,
I am writing this letter on behalf of all the people of Bralnic Valley,
whom I humbly serve as Knez Vlad the Tenth. My castle and the valley
are located among the beautiful Carpathians in Transylvania, far
from the worries of the Empire.
Just as we prepare for our yearly celebrations of my forefather
Knez Vlad the First, our peaceful life has been suddenly interrupted
by an unknown beast. It has brutally killed two farmers, and we
found their bloodless and heartless bodies. Only God knows how
many more will become the beast’s prey. Our best hunters, including
myself, cannot establish the nature of the beast, nor can we find
its tracks or lair.
My grandfather, the Knez Vlad the Eighth, was honored to have
members of your Society visit our castle. He witnessed their remarkable
knowledge and craftsmanship and described it in his diary, which I
found while exploring the library for clues on the beast. As soon as I
read about the Society, I wrote this letter with confidence that your
organization still exists in peace and prosperity.
I know the road to my domain is long and may be tiring, but my
children in Europe will arrange everything they can to make it comfortable for you. You will be guests of honor in my castle, and I will
provide you with generous remuneration for your services. I have no
doubts that your visit will help our valley and become the beginning
of our long-­lasting friendship.
Please follow the itinerary I have prepared for you. As mentioned
above, my children will make all necessary reservations for the Society.
With anticipation of your visit,
Sincerely,
Knez Vlad the Tenth
