COLD & WIND
It is winter, and the frost is numbing. Each time a
player character spends any significant length of
time outside without very good winter clothing –
including during their initial walk to the castle or
the village – they must make a FORCE roll to resist
the icy winds and snow. A character who fails
suffers a physical Condition. The knez’s children
can offer them sheepskin coats and boots that
will protect them against the cold (page 84).

THE CASTLE

As the snowstorm lets up a little, the player characters see the castle looming like a mighty dark silhouette hanging over the valley. Down in the valley, they
see the village with what looks like a church, a guesthouse, and a large tent in an open area. The valley
is surrounded by mountains and forest on all sides.
Give the players the player version of the village map
(handout 1B on page 128, also available for download
on the Free League website).
THE V ILL AGE

Even though the player characters are expected at the
castle, and it would be wise to hide there from the
storm, they are free to go to the village first if they
choose. The road there takes twenty minutes to walk.
For more about the village, see page 88.

COUNTDOW NS
A ND CATASTROPHES
This mystery includes two countdowns. The main one
is between the knez on one side and Eve and the villagers, instigated by Maria and the vârcolac, on the
other. During that countdown, the villagers are slowly
brought to the termination of their agreement with the
knez and his defeat by the vârcolac and Eve. The second
countdown indicates the growing power of the vârcolac,
which results in it breaking free from Maria’s control.

At the beginning of the mystery, three villagers have
been killed: two farmers on the edge of the village
some weeks back and one hunter closer to Bralnic
Castle, just a few days ago. This has influenced the
mood of the villagers, who are feeling gloomy and
distressed. Maria has been using her daughter to
manipulate the villagers, making them hesitate and
lose faith in their knez.
1. In the evening of the day when the player characters arrive, the traveling theater troupe (page
92) gives its first show, where they perform a
play depicting the Last Warrior defeating the beast.
This lifts the mood of the villagers and Eve, who
plays the Last Warrior, makes a speech about the
story and states that the people must be as courageous as the Last Warrior was, to fight against
the monster.
2. Spurred by Eve (page 94), the villagers decide
to go hunting for the beast. This will likely happen at a town meeting at The Last Warrior inn
(page 90) but can happen anywhere in the village. If the player characters are present, they may
try to dissuade the villagers, but their decision is
unchangeable, as they feel that they have been
doing nothing for too long. The player characters may also want to join the hunt, which they
are welcome to do.
During the hunt, two villagers are attacked
and killed by the vârcolac at Maria’s command.
The player characters cannot stop this, but they
may track the beast and hear the screams and confront the horrifying vârcolac in combat (see page
95 – and don’t forget its very high Fear value).
If defeated, the vârcolac disperses into gray mist,
but returns in a few hours. If the player characters
are Broken, the vârcolac leaves them and they will
be rescued by villagers who bring them to the Last
Warrior to treat their wounds.
After the battle, the player characters can track
the vârcolac towards Maria’s abandoned house
(page 93) with a VIGILANCE roll.
