THE KNEZ’S “CHILDREN”
“It is an honor to join Knez Vlad’s family. Our father is not perfect, but we will do everything to repay his kindness.”
Under the treaty, the knez must adopt a youngster
from the village every few years. The person may
be an orphan, but it is also seen as an honor for
parents to give their child to the knez. In return,
the knez sees to the children’s education and well-­
being. The knez’s family lives in the castle and visits
the village only on business. Most of the children
accept this fact without question, but there are
exceptions (see below).
After a few years in the castle, the children may
choose to stay in the castle, move back to the village, or be sent to Europe by the knez. There are
ten adult children of the knez scattered throughout Europe and nine more living in the village,
including the priest and his sister-­in-­law, who
owns the Last Warrior

Adrian: An open and cheerful young man interested in history and science. He wants to study in
Europe but for now must help in the castle.

Mihai: A severe and moody hunter with messy
black hair. When he came of age, he decided to
stay in the castle. He is competitive and proud of
his hunting skills.

Alina: An observant and straightforward girl with
piercing brown eyes. She has a tiny pet mouse
that she carries with her everywhere. Like Daniel,
Alina recently settled in the castle. She misses her
parents and feels unhappy, but fears to speak out.
