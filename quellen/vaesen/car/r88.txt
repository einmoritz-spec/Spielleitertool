THE GR A ND H A LL
From the inner courtyard, the main doors lead into
the castle through the long, high-­ceilinged central
corridor to the grand hall. It is a spacious room with
four cold fireplaces. Long ago, it hosted aristocratic
celebrations with music and dances, but now it has
been repurposed as storage. Only the artful carvings
on the walls and ceiling hint at its forgotten glory.

·

DINING H A LL
The dining hall on the second floor is much smaller
than the grand hall and is currently used by the knez
to dine with guests and with his family. The walls of
the room are covered with gorgeous tapestries and
carpets. A hidden door conceals stairs leading down
to the kitchen.
