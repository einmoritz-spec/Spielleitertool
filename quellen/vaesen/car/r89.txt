LIBR A RY
From the dining hall, stairs lead to an extensive library
on the third floor. Numerous bookshelves create a
labyrinth inside the room, leaving free passages along
the walls. Only closed lamps are used here, protecting precious books and scrolls from fire.

Books: The knez has assembled an impressive
collection of books, ranging from occult lore and
folklore to science and philosophical papers, and
will gladly allow the player characters to study here.
You can let the players make LEARNING tests and
give out one clue for each success rolled in total.
Multiple attempts are allowed, but each roll takes
a few hours of study. See Clues below. If you have
access to the art book The Undead by Johan Egerkrans,
the players may freely reference it. It describes the
vârcolac and even features it on the front cover.

KNEZ V L A D
“By the treaty signed centuries ago,
I’m bound to guard this valley and its people.
The treaty is law, and I’ll follow it.”
Knez Vlad is a 500-year-­old vampire. He was once the
champion of the prior knez. He survived the hunt for
the vârcolac, sealed it in the dungeons, but was bitten by
the creature and died. The actual reason for his transformation into a vampire is unclear, whether a consequence of the vârcolac’s bite, or the treaty that made
him the new knez and protector of Bralnic Valley. He
obeys the treaty, killing no one, and supports the village.
At the same time, the knez is torn by guilt over his
actions a century ago, when he called in the Pravda
Society to hunt and kill his dhampir son (page 76),
and forty years ago, when he let the vârcolac kill the
parents of the seamstress Maria, who opposed their
union, and then took her to his castle to be his wife
(page 76). When the knez sees Eve (step #4 of the
villagers’ countdown on page 79), he immediately
realizes that she is his daughter, and that Maria is
behind the whole thing.
The knez is a tall man who looks to be in his mid-­
thirties, with short black hair and a sharp beard. His
face is not pale, and his body is warm. He tries to gather
knowledge of all news and developments in the world
through his family outside the valley. With their help, he
invests in industries, buys art, and increases his wealth.
Vampires are described on page 60, but the treaty
gives Knez Vlad +2 to all stats. If the treaty is broken,
his stats are reduced to normal levels.
