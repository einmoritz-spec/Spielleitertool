On the second floor are rooms for the knez’s children,
and the third floor holds rooms for guests. The player
characters will be offered separate rooms with all necessary amenities. The fireplaces are already burning,
heating the rooms. The carved wooden furniture,
tapestries, and carpets create a cozier atmosphere
than elsewhere in the castle.
However, after spending some time in silence, the
player characters might hear the wind howling somewhere in the sealed corridors of the castle, and they
may experience a constant feeling of being watched.
During the night, the players may experience strange
dreams and visions (page 81).

Also on the second floor, behind the children’s rooms,
are the knez’s quarters. His room is dark and cold,
with thick curtains drawn and a large wooden coffin
placed in the center.

THE DUNGEON
A door in the mountainside in the inner courtyard
leads to the dungeons. Behind the door is a root cellar with food stocks and a separate room for wine. At
the back there is a solid wooden door, that has clearly
been broken down from the inside.

CH A LLENGES

Mihai, one of the children of the knez, always
works in the courtyard and will take note of player
characters going into the root cellar unless they
sneak by rolling for STEALTH . If they are seen, he
warns them against going into the dungeons below,
stating that it is too dangerous as the old tunnels
may collapse – an OBSERVATION roll will reveal this to
be a lie. A MANIPULATION roll can convince Mihai to
let them go in, but then he will accompany them
and later tell the knez everything they found or said.
The Dungeon: In the tunnels there are cells for
prisoners and an old but well-­kept armory. In
one of the cells, the bars are broken, as if from the
inside. The vârcolac was kept here before its escape.
CLUES

The Fur: If the player characters examine the cell
with the broken bars, an INVESTIGATION roll will let
them find a piece of thick black fur stuck to one
of the bars.
Star Marks: In addition, the walls and ceiling of
the cell are carved with chaotic star-­shaped marks.
Another INVESTIGATION test reveals that the marks
resemble the map of the night sky above the village. An additional success shows that the map
reflects the positions of stars from more than five
centuries ago.
Bloodstains: A successful MEDICINE test gives away
layer after layer of bloodstains on the stone floor,
dating from different times. These were left after
feeding the imprisoned vârcolac with cattle, but
there is no apparent way to tell this.
Confronting the Knez: If the player characters
confront the knez with their finds in the dungeon,
he will admit that the vârcolac was held there for
centuries but recently escaped – and that this is
probably the beast now on a killing spree in the
valley. He claims he didn’t reveal this earlier as
he was not sure. He can also give them general
information about vârcolaci unless they already
found it in the library (page 85).
