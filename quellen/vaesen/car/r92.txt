THE VILLAGE
In the center of the village square stands a bronze
monument of a warrior killing a wolf-­like beast, similar to the image on the wax stamp of Knez Vlad’s letter.
On one side of the square is an Orthodox wooden
church with a tall bell tower, and on the other side
is a large circular guesthouse, with a chimney in the
middle of the roof.
On the outskirts of the village, the player characters notice a large, colorful tent with five vardos
(wooden, horse-­drawn wagons) behind it. The knez
has invited the wandering theater to the valley to
perform during the upcoming annual celebrations
of signing the treaty by Knez Vlad the First, and his
miraculous recovery the next day.

THE CHURCH
The orthodox church is an old wooden building on
one side of the square in the middle of the village.
There is a large statue of the “first” Knez Vlad defeating a wolf-­like beast in the center of the main square
facing the church, and one tall steeple in the front of
the building. Out back is a small churchyard.
A warm, dim light shines from the small windows
of the church. The church is in good condition, with
benches set under the walls and a beautifully carved,
gilded wooden iconostasis (a screen bearing icons) with
embroidered hangings.
At all times of day, the priest Father Iacob and
some parishioners are preparing it for the celebrations, wiping dust, polishing the gilt, and hanging
red banners. The smell of hay strewn on the floor
and burning incense greets everyone who enters.
The light from small windows under the ceiling and
the sea of shimmering candles make it feel warm and
sacred but leave corners draped in shadows.

The Churchyard: If the player characters explore
the churchyard, they may notice footsteps in the
snow leading to one grave. An INVESTIGATION roll
reveals that the same person, with quite small feet,
seems to have walked to this grave many times
recently. For more, see Clues below.
Mourners: After the death of the two hunters,
their bodies are brought here for burial and many
mourners gather here for a meeting about what
to do next (see steps #2 and #3 in the villagers’
countdown on page 79). Sad, sorrowful people in dark clothes look incongruous amidst these
colorful decorations.
The Confrontation: During the final Confrontation with the vârcolac (page 95), the villagers
will hide here and ring the church bell to summon Knez Vlad.
CLUES

The Graves: The tombstone for the grave mentioned above has two names on it – Alexandru and
Magdalena Weber (Maria’s parents, see page 76).
They both died on the same date, just over 40 years
ago. Nearby, the player characters can also find an
older grave of Karel Benes, the priest killed during
the first visit from the Society (page 76).
Exhumation: Father Iacob will not allow digging up any graves. Doing so anyway requires a
FORCE roll. A MEDICINE roll reveals that the remains
of Alexandru and Magdalena Weber have severe
damage to the bones, which are broken and torn
apart, while the skull of Karel Benes seems to have
been bashed in.
