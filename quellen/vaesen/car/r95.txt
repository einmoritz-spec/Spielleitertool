Radu is an energetic, loud man in his mid-­
thirties. He has curly blonde hair, blue eyes,
and a kind smile. Because of a lifetime of
hard work, he is well-­built and confidently
moves through the tavern, serving tables with
food and drinks.
Radu married the knez’s daughter, Lidia,
and Knez Vlad had The Last Warrior built
as a gift for their wedding. Shy and quiet,
Lidia prefers cooking with her mother-­in-­
law, leaving Radu to care for the tavern guests.
He is loyal to his wife and their job but also
understands those he grew up with. At the
beginning of these terrible events, he appears
to be stuck between both sides, so he currently chooses to take neither.

These folk are from the valley and their job is
to hunt animals for their skin and meat. Like
everyone in the village, they grew up believing
that the knez protects them from all dangers.
After the horrible murders of their friends,
they feel afraid and angry. Eve’s speeches
make them question the role of the knez and
their own power in that relationship.
