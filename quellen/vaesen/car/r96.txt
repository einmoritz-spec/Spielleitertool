The Troupe: The theater is open to everyone and
performs in the evenings. During the day, the
troupe is busy preparing for their performances,
maintaining their tent, and decorating the area,
and some villagers occasionally pass nearby to
look at and talk to the guests. The troupe is happy
to interact with the player characters – see Clues
for more.
Mother Maria is interested in the player characters, especially if they are dressed in sheepskin
coats from the castle, as the Society may become
an obstacle to her plan. To learn more about them,
she invites them to her vardo, offering to tell their
fortunes and at the same time asking them questions about their current case.
Maria is an occultist who has the talent Medium,
but does not require a dice roll to tell the parts of
the player character’s backstory or hint at their dark
secret. While talking with Mother Maria, during
her fortune telling or on other occasions, the
player characters may take an OBSERVATION test to
understand that she is worried about their presence and that she is hiding something. However,
she will not reveal the truth until the final Confrontation (page 95).
