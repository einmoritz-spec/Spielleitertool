CLUES

The Troupe: Anyone from the Extravaganza can
tell that the knez invited them when they were in
Brașov. Upon arrival, they were invited to the castle
for dinner, but Mother Maria said no and told the
troupe to avoid that place. They are unhappy with
this decision since sleeping in a wooden wagon in
the winter is not very comfortable.
A successful INSPIRATION or MANIPULATION test
persuades the troupe to share their worries. When
Maria became the director, she led their troupe
to different places, pursuing her own unknown
interests and disappearing for long periods upon
each arrival in a new city. It appeared to the troupe
members as if she had some plan of her own rather
than focusing on the theater, resulting in the loss
of profits and lower morale.
Octavian: If player characters talk to Octavian
and win his trust (INSPIRATION ), he tells them how
Maria joined the Extravaganza. Forty years ago, the
troupe had been invited to this valley to entertain
people. On the night before they were to leave, a
terrified young woman asked them to take her out
of the valley. The former director could not refuse
as they all heard rumors that the woman’s family
had been killed in their home by a bear.
A few months after departing the valley, the
woman, Maria, gave birth to a strange child with
gray skin and black hair. This was Eve. Her mother
Maria was vigilant, constantly looking over her
shoulder as if someone was after her. She went to

·

libraries wherever they stayed and read many occult
books. After she became the troupe’s director, she
changed the former name of the troupe – “The
Carpathian Wandering Theatre” – to its current
title and led them through Europe, from Sweden
to Spain and Greece.
Eve: Player characters who meet Eve notice that
her skin is not covered in gray makeup but is actually gray and looks like it is covered in dirt. With a
successful OBSERVATION test, they understand that
she is sick and trying to hide it. She occasionally
coughs, and her muscles contract as if she is in pain.
If asked, she answers that she was born with a rare
skin condition, and because of that, her mother
was forced to leave her home, despite being with
a newborn child.
Maria’s Vardo: If the player characters manage to
sneak into Mother Maria’s vardo, they find many
occult books about the undead. The marked paragraphs and pages describe cases of children born
from an undead parent. Despite their supernatural talents, they are destined to die in their thirties
or forties. Further search requires an INVESTIGATION test to find a hidden box under the floor. The
box contains an old but fine silk napkin with the
initials “KV IX” and a simple wooden spindle with
rough black thread wrapped around it.

THE A BA NDONED HOUSE
The house is located on the outskirts of the village,
close to the forest that cascades from the mountains.
It is a small wooden building on a stone foundation,
with just a main room and a side room inside. There is
also a root cellar with a separate door from the outside.
Maria’s family lived here, and no one took over
the place after her parents’ death. Since Maria left,
this deserted house has been slowly collapsing – the
door is leaning inside and the roof leaks, although
some things remain of its former owners.
After Maria recently returned to the valley, she
uses this place as a hideout for the vârcolac.
