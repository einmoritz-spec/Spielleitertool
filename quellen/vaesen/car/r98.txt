MOTHER M A RI A

EV E

“An ancient evil is cunning and insidious, like the Serpent in Heaven.
One should be cautious before it is too late to do anything.”

“The world is changing. People should be free to decide
things for themselves. No one can take away this right.”

Mother Maria is in her sixties. Despite her hair being
gray and her body looking fragile, her eyes glow, and
her voice is powerful. She has the posture of an artiste,
graceful and mysterious. Maria wears colorful clothes,
which make her look extravagant and impressive.
She has had a challenging life full of misfortunes.
Fleeing from Bralnic Valley after discovering the knez’s
vampiric nature and the things he had done to her family,
she left everything she loved behind. But soon, she gave
birth to a gray-­skinned child, Eve. Nothing was more
important than her love for her daughter, and her new
friends from the theater troupe supported her. Seeking
out occult lore from all over Europe took years, but she
found explanations for the true nature of her daughter,
the knez, and the creature sealed beneath Bralnic Castle.
Unfortunately, Maria discovered that her Eve was
going to die in the near future and was destined to
turn into a vampire. She realized that there was no
safe place for her daughter after such a transformation, except for the place she came from. Maria came
up with a plan to guarantee her daughter’s safety in
her unlife and to exact revenge for her parents’ death
and the knez’s cruel lies, no matter the cost.
Maria told Eve about the knez and claimed that she
had a prophetic vision of Eve freeing people from his
vampiric tyranny. She did not, however, reveal that
Knez Vlad is Eve’s father.

Eve is the daughter of Maria and Knez Vlad.
When Maria escaped from Bralnic Castle, the
troupe that later became known as the Wallachia Mystique Extravaganza accepted her and
helped her leave the valley. Eve was born and
grew up in the theater, traveling all around
Europe. Since her parents were a mortal
woman and a vampire, Eve was born a dhampir, a creature between two worlds. She has
gray skin, thick black hair, and extraordinary
flexibility, which would provoke unwanted
attention in a normal life but instead makes her
a unique and valuable member of the troupe.
Eve spent all her life performing and
meeting different people, which influenced
her innately rebellious and idealistic character. Her abilities also help the troupe protect itself from locals, bandits, and competitors. Her mystic origin has an unpleasant
downside: she started to feel unwell when she
turned forty, but no doctor could explain why.
Eve tries not to pay attention to her condition
and believes in her mother’s prophesies that
she is destined to save people.
Toward the end of the mystery, Eve may
learn the truth – that the knez is her father,
that he killed her grandparents, and that she
is a dhampir destined for unlife. Her reactions to these revelations may determine the
fate of the valley. For more, see the Confrontation on page 95.
Dhampirs are described on page 39.
Eve is armed with a saber and a crossbow.
