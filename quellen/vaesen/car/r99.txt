CONFRONTATION

CH A LLENGES

Footprints: The house was abandoned a long time
ago, but the player characters may notice indistinct snowy footprints on the stairs that disappear
inside. An INVESTIGATION test reveals that the footprints are from a small person.
The Cellar: The vârcolac’s hideout is in an empty
root cellar underneath the house. The beast is likely
not here when the player characters arrive – but it
can be if you want it to. It will immediately attack if
confronted. If combat ensues, use the guidelines
in step #3 of the villagers’ countdown (page 79).
Someone Comes: When the player characters visit
the house, Maria and/or the vârcolac may return.
You can use this to create a tense scene. The player
characters may attempt to hide (STEALTH test) or
confront whoever is coming.
CLUES

The final Confrontation of this mystery will likely
start in the village. As the celebrations start in the
evening, the day after the player characters arrive,
crowds of people dressed in wolf and bear skins dance
and sing in the streets.

THE VÂ RCOL AC’S ATTACK
The vârcolac, controlled by Maria who is skulking
nearby with her spindle, sneaks through the crowds,
looking for the next victim. If the player characters
have confronted Maria or if she otherwise has concluded that they are a threat to her plan, the vârcolac
will be looking for them. Otherwise, it attacks someone close to the knez’s family who is respected in the
village, such as Radu Harnicu (page 91). See the
sidebar Fighting the Vârcolac below.

Inscriptions: Anyone examining the main room
will notice old carvings in the ceiling. A LEARNING test
will reveal the inscription in Romanian: “Knez Vlad
IX built this house for Alexandru Weber, his wife Magdalena, and
their daughter Maria. God bless them for a long and happy life.”
Love Letters: If the player characters examine
the main room, an INVESTIGATION test lets them also
find a bundle of love letters in the fireplace, partly
burned. It’s still possible to make out that the love
letters are addressed to Maria and signed by Knez
Vlad IX. They will recognize that the handwriting
on the love letters from the ninth knez and the
script on the invitation they received from the
tenth knez are identical.
The Spinning Wheel: The door to the side room is
skewed and twisted with age. The player characters
can force it open and see that it leads to a sewing
workroom. Some equipment is left, including a
spinning wheel. Apart from a missing spindle, the
wheel is still functional and seems to have been
recently repaired and used.
Black Fur: Player characters who pass a VIGILANCE
test notice a piece of thick black fur caught on an
iron nail in the door frame to the root cellar. It
looks as if it was torn free not long ago.

FIGHTING THE VÂRCOLAC
Vârcolaci and their stats are described on page
63. Its strength has been increased by Maria’s
yarn spindle (MIGHT +1, for a total of 10), which
helped it escape from the castle’s dungeons.
When confronting the vârcolac, people with the
Sight (like the player characters) may make an OBSERVATION test to spot a very thin, magical thread stretching from the beast’s back to Maria’s yarn spindle,
wherever she is, that gives her control of the vârcolac.
Cutting the thread requires a CLOSE COMBAT
attack with a bladed weapon. Doing so will reduce
the vârcolac’s MIGHT by 1 but also turn it loose. For
dramatic purposes, we recommend that you don’t
let the player characters see the thread until the
final Confrontation (page 95).
It’s also possible to take the spindle from Maria
with an opposed FORCE test, or without a roll if Maria
is Broken – this must happen in the same round
of combat, or the vârcolac will go free. A player
character with the spindle cannot fully control the
beast as this requires years of study, but with a slow
action and an INSPIRATION test they may slow the
beast down and eliminate one full upcoming turn
for the beast for each success rolled.
