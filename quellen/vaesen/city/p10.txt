AUGUST T. LYSA NDER
The son of a fisherman, August Torstensson grew
up in poor conditions in the Stockholm archipelago.
He was a bright and ambitious boy who dreamed of
a life beyond mending nets and doing odd jobs on
the islands, but for a long time nothing changed –
until one day he found an old, gilded snuff box on
the shore. Inside it was a worm-­like creature, and
it seemed to be alive.
August’s grandmother used to tell him stories of
strange vaesen when he was a child, and his fascination
with such tales had not waned. He was convinced that
this creature would bring him good fortune as long
as he cared for it properly. And it did. August was
lucky in everything he did and quickly made plenty
of money playing cards at the Falk’s Salons hotel in
the archipelago town of Vaxholm.
Soon August had amassed enough capital to start
a business in Stockholm. He changed his last name
to Lysander, a combination of the Greek words lysis
(freedom) and andros (man), and kept the initial T
as a reminder of his roots. Lysander’s interest in
the supernatural eventually led him to Linnea Elfe­
klint (page 83 of the core rulebook) and the Society,
which at that time was withering away. With Lysander’s financial backing, Castle Gyllencreutz was preserved and the Society survived for a few more years.
Now, more than thirty years later, August T.
Lysander is one of Stockholm’s wealthiest industrialists. He has built a highly successful cork import
business, and his factory was the first in the world to
mechanize the production of bottle corks. Lysander
Cork Factories Inc. now has factories on the continent
and in the Baltics. The cork bark is shipped from the
Mediterranean to the Nordics aboard the company’s
own steam-­powered freighters.

obsessed with the pursuit of occult knowledge, driven
by an awful feeling that his past is catching up with
him. He knows that his success is largely due to the
spertus, and through his research he has realized

Director August T. Lysander is tall and always
wears elegant suits. His gray hair and beard
are neatly trimmed and his gaze sharp behind
his monocle. He is an energetic man who
gesticulates as he speaks. August talks with
pride about how he used his brilliant business
acumen to build a good life for his family. He
is often seen at Stockholm’s most prestigious
social events. He is also a Freemason, which
gives him access to a wide network of friends
and contacts. August has a deep interest in
the supernatural, hosting spiritualist circles and collecting occult books and objects.
