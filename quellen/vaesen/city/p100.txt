CLUES

The Snuff Box: Have the characters make a VIGILANCE test during their meeting with Lysander to
notice that he is fidgeting with a gilded snuff box
bearing the monogram “CL.” They might recognize the snuff box from previous mysteries in
this book. If asked about it, Lysander admits (if

·

he has not already) that it is the snuff box of Carl
Linnaeus himself, which he came across in his
youth. But he refuses to show them its contents –
if the characters insist, he gets annoyed and asks
them to leave, calling for his guards if necessary.
Picture of the Palace: There is an open book on
the table in front of Lysander. If the characters
try to see what the book is about and pass an INVESTIGATION test, they notice a picture of the Royal
Palace on the cover. If they ask about it, Lysander
brushes the question aside by saying that he has
always been interested in royalty. A character who
passes an OBSERVATION test realizes that he is not
telling the whole truth.
