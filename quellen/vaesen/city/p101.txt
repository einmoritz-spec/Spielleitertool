LYSANDER’S REACTION
How Lysander reacts to the player charac­
ters’ unexpected arrival in Stockholm depends
largely on their behavior. At first, he is friendly
and gracious, but if the characters express
doubts about him or start investigating his
activities, Lysander will see them as a threat
that must be eliminated – when appropriate,
he will have Helena von Arensburg send her
night raven to kill them. This attack can take
place at any point during the game, but only
at night. For stats, see page 146 of the core
rulebook. The night raven cannot be killed, but
if banished, it will not attack them again.
If the characters do not voice suspicions about
Lysander, he will try to recruit them to his cause,
without revealing the whole truth. When the
time is right (in his office or at Berns’ Salon), he
tells them of his plan to use the upcoming pro­
test march on Castle Slope (see step #3 of the
countdown) as a cover to break into the Royal
Armory and steal the Neck’s lyre – the Rosen­
bergers are after it, and the Society must get
to it first and take the artifact somewhere safe!
Lysander wants the characters to help him carry
out his plan. If they agree, see Confrontation. If
they refuse, Lysander now considers them a threat
and tries to eliminate them as described above.

BERNS’ SA LON
Berns’ Salon is located in Berzelii Park near Newbridge Bay. This newly built entertainment palace
has a gilded interior with mirrors, red velvet curtains,
opulent chandeliers, and large windows overlooking Berzelii Park. The Red Room is a popular haunt
for Stockholm’s bachelors, who come here to drink
and discuss all sorts of topics. Posters at the entrance
advertise a variety of shows, performances by international artists and dance troupes. A murmur of voices
and the clinking of glasses are heard from the café as
food is served to well-­dressed guests.
On the Saturday evening following the player characters’ arrival, the famous German illusionist Professor
Xavier Liebholz and his partner, the spiritualist Helena von Arensburg, are performing in the ballroom.
