CH A LLENGES

Tickets: Tickets to the show (for the whole group)
cost 1. If the player characters cannot afford this,
they will have to sneak in using STEALTH .
Pyrotechnics: The show opens with Xavier Liebholz and Helena von Arensburg introducing
themselves to the cheering crowd. Then Liebholz kicks things off with a spectacular pyrotechnic display. Flames erupt from his empty hand,
lighting several fire baskets at the edge of the stage.
The audience gasps as the entire curtain behind
Liebholz bursts into flames. Clapping his hands,
he instantly puts out the fire to roaring applause.
A character who tries to see through the trick
and passes an INVESTIGATION test realizes that it is
no trick – it seems that Liebholz really can conjure
fire out of thin air. A successful OBSERVATION test
reveals that Liebholz is using actual magic.
Levitation: For the second act, Liebholz rolls out a
velvet-­covered table on which Helena von Arensburg
lies down. A murmur spreads through the audience
as Liebholz extends his hands toward the woman,
who suddenly rises into the air. The murmur turns
to cries of amazement as von Arensburg floats over
the audience’s heads, circling a few times before
returning to the table to thunderous applause.
Again, a successful INVESTIGATION test reveals
that it is not a trick, and an OBSERVATION test lets
the characters see what is really lifting Helena
von Arensburg – a huge black raven, majestic and
ghost-­like, carrying her in its talons. No one else
in the audience seems to notice the bird. A LEARNING test can reveal that the bird is a night raven, if
the players do not realize this on their own.
Intermission: Champagne is served during the
intermission. Professor Liebholz appears, and in
the crowd gathering around him, the characters
spot a familiar face. August T. Lysander is deep
in conversation with Helena von Arensburg. He
greets the characters cheerfully and introduces both
Liebholz and von Arensburg as his dear friends.
A successful OBSERVATION test reveals that von Arensburg was the person who interrupted the séance with

Dressing Rooms: The characters can investigate
Liebholz and von Arensburg’s dressing rooms
during the intermission or while they are performing. It takes a successful STEALTH test to avoid
the security guards. In the dressing rooms, the
characters can find items of interest with a successful INVESTIGATION test – see Clues.
Mind Reading: After the intermission, Helena von Arensburg takes charge of the show. She
announces that she will now demonstrate her
ability to read minds, and invites one of the player
characters on stage. Closing her eyes dramatically
and pressing two fingers to her temple, she begins:
“I see great darkness in you.”
Make an opposed roll between her MAGIC (8)
and the character’s OBSERVATION. If von Arensburg
wins, she opens her eyes, turns to the audience,
and dramatically reveals the character’s dark secret
in somewhat cryptic terms. “Is this true, sir/madam?”
If the player character wins, the mind reading fails and the character instead gets a glimpse
into von Arensburg’s mind – see Clues. She then
says something vague and invites a new person
on stage – ideally another player character. The
performance ends to great applause.
Ghostly Visions: The finale of the show is truly
extraordinary. Liebholz rolls a large mirror onto
the stage and announces that Helena von Arensburg will now speak to the dead. She walks up to
the mirror and once again invites a player character on stage. “Think of someone who is no longer with us,
someone who has crossed to the other side.” Ask the player
who their character is thinking of.
Suddenly a shimmering figure appears in the
mirror – it is the person the character was thinking
of! This provokes a Fear test against Fear 1. Helena
von Arensburg speaks with the ghost, asking a few
