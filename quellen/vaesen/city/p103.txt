CLUES

The Pyrotechnics: This act is a clue that Liebholz
started the fire at Fire Mill.
The Levitation: This act reveals that Liebholz and
von Arensburg are in control of a night raven. This
vaesen has been spotted in the city several times
recently, including at the Fire Mill fire.
The Intermission: The meeting with Lysander in
the company of Liebholz and von Arensburg reveals
that they know each other and may indicate that
Lysander was also responsible for the fire at Fire Mill.
The Flyers: In Liebholz and von Arensburg’s
dressing rooms, the characters may find several
copies of the flyer calling for a protest on Castle
Slope (handout 4C on page 119, also available
for download on the Free League website). This
suggests that they have an interest in the protest
and may even be behind it.
The Feather: In the dressing room, the characters
may also find the large, black feather of a raven,
similar to the one they may have found in Linnaeus’s secret library (page 93).
The Mind-­Reading: During this act, a character can gain insight into the mind of Helena von
Arensburg, which may reveal memories of her
standing outside Fire Mill as Liebholz uses his
pyrotechnics to set fire to the mill, and her conversation with Lysander on Castle Slope.

PROFESSOR
X AV IER LIEBHOLZ
Professor Xavier Liebholz is a civil engineer
and a pioneering innovator of magic tricks and
illusions. He is one of the first illusionists in
Europe to use complex devices for his performances, such as the Talking Head, the Sphinx,
and the Indian Basket. What few people know
is that he is also capable of actual magic.
Liebholz and his partner Helena von
Arensburg met August T. Lysander on the
continent many years ago. He recently
approached them with an offer to finance a
performance at Berns’ Salon in Stockholm,
in return for certain favors.
Professor Liebholz is tall and handsome,
with piercing eyes beneath neatly groomed
eyebrows, a bushy beard, and a mustache. He
wears a suit and cape and carries a walking
stick. He speaks good Swedish, but with a
distinct German accent, and treats everyone
around him as intellectually inferior.
Magician: Professor Liebholz is a magician
and can cast the curses DEADLY COLD and FIRE
(page 119 of the core rulebook).
