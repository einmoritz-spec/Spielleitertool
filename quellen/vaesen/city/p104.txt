HELENA VON A RENSBURG
Helena von Arensburg is a spiritualist of
Baltic German descent and the partner of
Professor Liebholz. In their performances,
she conjures hair-­raising visions of wraiths
that seem to float above the audience. She
also contacts the other side, causing many
audience members to excitedly report seeing the ghosts of their loved ones speaking
through the medium.
Helena von Arensburg is tall and broad-­
shouldered, with short dark hair and green
eyes, and wears a full-­length, dark purple
coat. She speaks with a slight accent and
always maintains intense eye contact with
the person she is talking to.
Magician: Helena von Arensburg is a powerful
medium who can communicate with the dead
and strike fear into her opponents. She can
cast the curses SLEEP and FEAR (page 119 of the
core rulebook) and read minds (roll for MAGIC
against the victim’s OBSERVATION). At night she
can also control the night raven (see adjacent
sidebar). The night raven rests during the day
and cannot be used until nightfall.

NIGHT RAVEN
The night raven and its characteristics are
described on page 146 of the core rulebook.
It obeys Helena von Arensburg’s commands
as long as she keeps three of its feathers and
a passion flower tucked inside her bible. Con­
trolling the night raven does not require any die
rolls and does not count as an action for von
Arensburg during combat. If the player charac­
ters manage to take the bible from her (counts
as grappling attempt, see page 62 of the core
rulebook), she loses control of the night raven.

THE ROYA L PA L ACE
The player characters may decide to visit the palace
before Sunday’s protest to warn the Royal Guard about
Lysander’s break-­in. But the soldiers dismiss any talk
of planned break-­ins and magic lyres and shoo the
characters away – there is unrest in the streets and
everyone is on edge. The characters will not be able
to enter the palace (or the Royal Armory) before the
riot on Sunday (see Confrontation on the next page).
