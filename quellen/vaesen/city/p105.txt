CONFRONTATION

THE RIOTS

The final confrontation in this mystery begins after
step #3 of the countdown (page 86), when a large,
angry crowd has gathered on Castle Slope to demand
the abolition of the monarchy. Using the protest as a
distraction, Lysander plans to break into the Royal
Armory and steal the Neck’s lyre. He is aided by his
lackeys – the valet Karl-­Axel Trygg, Xavier Liebholz,
and Helena von Arensburg.
The player characters have probably seen the flyer
calling for the protest, perhaps in Lysander’s office or
in the dressing room at Berns’. Lysander may also have
involved the characters in his plan (page 97). If so,
he has told them to meet him on Castle Slope, where
his lackeys will be ready to come to his aid if necessary.

A great mob has gathered on Castle Slope, chanting
angry slogans about overthrowing the royal family.
Police and royal guards posted around the palace
angrily push back anyone who gets too close. Both
sides are behaving recklessly, as if driven by some
unseen rage.
Player characters who pass an OBSERVATION test hear
intense violin music in the distance, but cannot see
anyone playing.
Before long, protesters and police officers clash.
Riots break out and angry crowds start breaking into
the palace. This is the moment Lysander has been
waiting for. How this plays out depends on whether
or not the player characters are involved in his plan.
