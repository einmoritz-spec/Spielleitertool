Lysander tells the player characters to follow the mob
into the palace. He leads them to a plain, locked
door on the ground floor – the entrance to the Royal
Armory, which is currently unguarded. The door can
be lockpicked with STEALTH or forced open using FORCE.
If you wish, the characters may need to get past
some royal guards trying to stop the rioters on their
way to the Armory. They can sneak past the guards
using STEALTH or engage them in combat. See the
adjacent sidebar for the guards’ stats.
Once inside, the characters must pass an INVESTIGATION test to quickly locate the Neck’s lyre among
all the crowns, scepters, swords, suits of armor, and
other treasures. Failure means their search takes
too long and some royal guards come to investigate.
The lyre is hidden under a dusty cloth deep in the
vaulted cellar. The characters have probably seen it
before, in Linnaeus’s memory in The Haunted Library.
See adjacent sidebar.

THE NECK’S LYRE
The lyre of the Neck, perhaps the instrument
on which Orpheus once played so beautifully
that even death was moved to pity, is exquisitely
carved in silver-­gray wood that shimmers in
the light. Elegant symbols are etched across its
surface – with a successful LEARNING test, the
player characters recognize them as ancient
Greek letters.
The characters cannot play the lyre them­
selves, and it cannot be destroyed by any means
available to them. However, its strings can be
cut with a knife, which can then be used to ban­
ish the Neck. Wresting the lyre from someone
else’s grasp counts as a grappling attempt
(page 62 of the core rulebook).

THE PL AY ER CH A R ACTERS
A RE NOT IN VOLV ED
Accompanied by his valet Karl-­Axel Trygg (page 96;
if he is dead, replace him with another servant using
the same stats) and magicians Xavier Liebholz and Helena von Arensburg (pages 99 and 100), Lysander
enters the palace and breaks into the Royal Armory.
If the player characters are watching the crowd and
pass a VIGILANCE test, they spot Lysander or his associates and can follow them to the Royal Armory. If they
warned Commissioner Utterson, this automatically
succeeds. However, Utterson lacks the authority to
let the characters into the palace in advance, so they
will have to enter with the rioting crowd. Again, the
characters may encounter guards inside the palace.
The characters cannot beat Lysander to the Royal
Armory, and cannot confront him until he is attempting to leave with the lyre.
If the characters fail the VIGILANCE test above, they
must find their way to the Royal Armory by asking
around, which requires an INVESTIGATION or MANIPULATION test. If these tests fail as well, they cannot confront Lysander until he reaches Castle Slope.
