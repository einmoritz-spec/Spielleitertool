CONFRONTING LYSA NDER
If the player characters try to stop Lysander or refuse
to hand over the lyre, they will have to confront him.
This can happen at the Royal Armory, inside the palace, out on Castle Slope, or by the Stockholm Stream
during the encounter with the Neck. If Lysander’s
lackeys Karl-­Axel Trygg, Xavier Liebholz, and Helena von Arensburg have not already appeared, they
show up now, ready to defend their master.
Lysander pleads with the player characters and
tells his whole story. He shows them Linnaeus’s snuff
box and recounts how he found the spertus as a young
man, and how it brought him wealth and success. But
then he realized he was its third owner, doomed to
surrender his soul to the Devil.
Lysander explains that he first contacted the characters – regarding the murder of Sverker von Essen –
to assess their capabilities and see whether they might
be able to help him. The purpose of the conclave on
Goose Island was to find a way to break the spertus’s
curse. And he sought Linnaeus’s secret journal in the

library beneath the National Archives believing it held
the answer – which it did. The attack by the “Rosenbergers” in the library was merely a diversion to throw
suspicion off himself. If Karl-­Axel Trygg is present,
he reveals the wolf’s head tattoo on his forearm and
simply wipes it off – he was never really a Rosenberger.
Now Lysander knows that only the Neck can save
his soul – in exchange for the lyre.
Let the player characters react. If they refuse to
budge, Lysander does not hesitate to use force. He
uses his spertus to gain the STRENGTH ability, which
grants +2 to FORCE and increases his physical Toughness by one step. He has his lackeys fight for him so
that he can escape to Castle Slope with the lyre and
hand it to the Neck, no matter the cost. Keep in mind
that Helena von Arensburg cannot command the
night raven now that it is daytime.
If the player characters are Broken inside the palace, Lysander leaves them there. Then Utterson finds
them and helps them to their feet so they can still catch
up to Lysander at the Stockholm Stream (below).
