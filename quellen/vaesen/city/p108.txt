THE NECK IN
THE STOCKHOLM STREA M
When the player characters exit the Royal Armory, they
are met by chaos. Bloodied and bruised protesters,
policemen, and soldiers lie scattered everywhere. The
sounds of continued violence echo from the alleys –
the uprising seems to have spread throughout the city.
The haunting tune of a violin is heard from the
stream. Darkness falls, and in the purple twilight
they see that the Neck has emerged from the Stream
of Stockholm. He sits on a rock at the water’s edge,
playing his violin. They can now clearly see that the
Neck and Stagnelius are one and the same. He gives
them a look of recognition. Then Lysander walks
down to the shoreline. What happens next depends
on whether or not he has the lyre:
Lysander has the lyre: Lysander wades into the
water toward the Neck and holds out the lyre: “Take
your instrument, take it and free me from my curse.”
Lysander does not have the lyre: The cork magnate begs the player characters to give him the lyre:
“Without it, my soul is lost.”

If the characters let the Neck have his lyre, they
trigger the Catastrophe (page 86) but still do not save
Lysander (see below).

If the confrontation with Lysander (above) has not
already taken place, it happens now. If Lysander has
the lyre, he must be forcibly stopped from giving it
to the Neck; if the characters have the lyre, he will use
any means necessary to take it from them.
If the player characters defeat Lysander and have
the lyre, the Neck may attack to take it from them.
They must then banish the Neck using religious
symbols or by cutting the strings of the lyre with
a knife and stabbing it into the ground. For stats,
see page 148 of the core rulebook. If banished, the
Neck vanishes into the water, and Lysander’s fate
is sealed (see below).
Note: If you think there has already been enough
combat and drama, you can have the Neck retreat
without a fight and vanish into the water when
Lysander is defeated. In that case he simply gives
the characters a murderous glare, suggesting they
will meet again.

THE CORK M AGNATE’S FATE
No matter how the confrontation with Lysander and
the Neck unfolds, Lysander is doomed. The Neck never
had any intention of saving the cork magnate’s soul.
Lysander collapses, clutching his chest in agony.
His face contorts with fear. He screams something
about the gates of Hell opening before him and lashes
out at thin air, as if fending off an unseen attacker.
Lying on the ground, he pulls out his gilded snuff
box as though it might save him.
The player characters then see Lysander being torn
apart by invisible claws. He shrieks in pain and begs
to be saved, but there is nothing the characters can
do. The lid of the snuff box opens and the spertus
bursts into blue flames, reduced to ashes.
The cork magnate’s spirit rises from his body, leaving behind a mangled, bloodless corpse, its face frozen
in a grimace of horror. The spertus has claimed its due.
