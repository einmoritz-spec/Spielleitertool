Mystery 2:
SONG TO THE MOON

CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW
In this campaign, cork magnate August T. Lysander
desperately seeks a way to control the spertus or break
its curse. His plan is to gradually reach out to the Society and other individuals with knowledge of vaesen
and use their insights and abilities to save his soul.
Below is an overview of the four mysteries of the
campaign. They must be played in order, although
it is possible to insert other mysteries between them.
Naturally, the course of events will depend heavily
on the player characters’ actions.

Mystery 1:
SCENT OF A KILLER
August T. Lysander contacts the Society under the
pretense of needing their help with a series of grisly
murders in Stockholm. The police are at wit’s end,
and Lysander suspects that a vaesen is responsible.
But all he really wants is an excuse to meet the player
characters and see what they are made of.
The capital is in turmoil and the characters are
drawn into the hunt for the killer, which becomes a
race against time to uncover the culprit before more
bloody victims are found in the autumn darkness.

August T. Lysander contacts the Society again and invites
them to his summer residence on Goose Island in the
Stockholm archipelago. He has convened a Conclavium
Sub Rosa, a secret gathering of individuals with knowledge of pacts and dealings with vaesen. His purpose,
of course, is to find a way to break the spertus’ curse.
But the pleasant visit to the island is interrupted
by a shocking death, which is soon followed by others. It soon becomes clear that a terrifying vaesen
from the east is responsible, and the player characters must step in to solve the mystery and save the
island’s inhabitants. Clues point to Lysander’s own
family, and dark secrets are revealed.

Mystery 3:
THE H AUNTED LIBR A RY
Following the events on Goose Island, Lysander continues his attempts to free himself from the spertus’
curse. During his research, he makes a remarkable
discovery: the first owner of his spertus was none
other than the renowned botanist Carl Linnaeus,
which explains the “CL” monogram on the snuff box.
Through descendants of Hedda von der Burg,
Lysander also finds out that Linnaeus’s lost writings
on vaesen may be hidden in a secret library beneath
the National Archives on Knights’ Isle in Stockholm.
The problem is that only a person with the Sight can
find the hidden chamber in the catacombs. Once
again, Lysander needs the player characters’ help.
What the cork magnate does not know is that the
National Archives is haunted by the ghost of none other
than Linnaeus himself. The ghost causes a great deal of
trouble during the characters’ visit, and to access the
secret library, they must seek answers in the specter’s
memories – experiencing the history of Linnaeus’s
expedition to Dalecarlia, his spertus, and the Neck’s lyre.
