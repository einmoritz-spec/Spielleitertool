Death in the
Archipelago
Vaxholm police report that Vesper
Lysander, born Stiernkors, has died
on Goose Island in the archipelago.
Mrs. Lysander disappeared the night
after her wedding to Lucius Lysander,
son of cork magnate August Lysander.
According to witnesses, she was in a
confused state and went into the sea
by herself. The police chief of Vaxholm
does not suspect foul play.
