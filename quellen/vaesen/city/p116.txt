Dear friends,

I hope the months since we last met have been kind to
you. I wish I could say the same for myself, but the
events on Goose Island haunt me still. I have buried
my grief in work – running my cork company, of
course, but also researching the glorious history of the
Society. The latter began as a mere pastime, but in
the process I have uncovered secrets that I now feel
compelled to share with you.
You may have heard of botanist Carl Linnaeus’s
scientific expeditions to Dalecarlia during the last
century. As you probably know, Linnaeus was gifted
with the Sight and later became the esteemed leader
of the Society – a true pioneer in vaesen studies. You
may also be aware that Linnaeus inexplicably left
the Society and his research on vaesen a year after
his expedition to Dalecarlia, which he called Societas
Itineraria Reuterholmiana.

His sudden departure has long been a mystery in the
history of the Society, a wound that never healed. But
I ’m on the track of an explanation. I am convinced
that Linnaeus left behind notes about what happened,
and I need your help to find them. But that ’s not all
– I have also learned that our rivals, the vainglorious
Rosenbergers, are on our trail. We must find
Linnaeus’s secret library before they do.
Once again, I ask that you come to Stockholm as soon
as possible. I will meet you at the Golden Peace and
tell you more. The fate of the world is at stake!
August T. Lysander
