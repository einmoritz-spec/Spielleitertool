Friends of the Unseen,
My name is Vilma af Zimt, an artist and medium based in
Stockholm. I am writing you because of a message I received
from the spirit world during a séance I recently conducted at
the Swedenborg Society. The message came to me and the
other participants through automatic writing on a Ouija board.
A powerful spirit sought contact. He seemed deeply troubled and
insisted on conveying the message directly to you. When I offered
to pass on his message, he became violent, knocking over several
candlesticks in the room. Several participants fainted in terror.
Therefore, I invite you to a séance at my apartment at 19 Charity
Street in the Town Between the Bridges, so you may have direct
contact with the spirit.
Yours affectionately,
Vilma af Zimt
