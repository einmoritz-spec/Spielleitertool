THE VOICE OF THE PEOPLE

MUST BE HEARD!
A Call to All Honest Citizens

GATHER FOR A MARCH OF PROTEST AGAINST THE CROWN!
For the king’s excesses grievously
oppress the toiling masses!

We suffer under taxation, hunger, and injustice,
whilst his court feasts upon the fruits of our labor.

Our Patience Is at an End!
We Do Hereby
Demand:
– Bread upon the table of
every household
– An end to the squandering of the people’s purse
– Parliamentary authority
over royal prerogative
– Freedom, justice, and
equality for all!

Come with drum
and banner and
steadfast resolve!
Men, women, and
children—all of
the people shall
arise!

SUNDAY THE 7TH
OF MAY, AT THE
STROKE OF NOON

Castle Slope,
Stockholm

— FOR THE RIGHTS OF THE PEOPLE —
AGAINST THE TYRANNY OF THE CROWN!
