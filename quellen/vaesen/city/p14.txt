SCENT OF A KILLER
This mystery brings the player characters to Stockholm and the Town Between the Bridges – today known
as the Old Town – where a series of gruesome murders have taken place. The characters are contacted by
August T. Lysander, one of the richest businessmen in
the town and an old friend of the Society, who asks for
help in solving the murders as he suspects a vaesen is
responsible. The mystery is a race against time where
the characters must uncover the killer before more
bloody victims are found in the autumn darkness.
