On the first full moon after the battle, Ester woke
up in a pasture with the taste of fresh blood in her
mouth. Next to her lay a dead horse with its throat
torn out. She remembered her grandmother’s tales
of shapeshifters. Werewolves. In old documents at the
National Archives in Stockholm, she found stories
describing ancient cures for the curse she now bears.
Ester Volk left the military and started working
with the police as a coroner to continue her research.
The pay was not great but other people’s aversion to
the morgue allowed her to work undisturbed. For
years she has worked tirelessly in her laboratory to
produce an elixir that can prevent her transformation.

THE CORONER

What Ester did not realize was that the elixir was beginning to blur the line between her human and wolf
forms. The doctor developed a heightened sense of
smell and started picking up the scent of the killers
whose victims ended up on her autopsy table. In her
bestial form, the werewolf remembered these scents
and hunted down the perpetrators.
Several times over the past few months, Ester has
woken up in the gutter at dawn with torn clothes and
the taste of blood in her mouth, and she has begun
to suspect that the maimed bodies she now encounters on her autopsy table are actually her own doing.
The police initially took little notice of these slain
killers, but after the murder of Sverker von Essen, the
wave of homicides has struck fear into the city’s inhabitants. The tenor fell prey to the beast after murdering the Opera’s conductor, Mortimer Cronstedt, at
the Golden Peace tavern by poisoning his turtle soup.

THE SCENT OF A KILLER

Coroner Ester Volk works in a tiled autopsy room at
Utterson’s police station. Born in Ostrobothnia in
what is now Finland, but was then the eastern province
of Sweden, she showed an early interest in medicine
and trained as a doctor at the Collegium Medicum in
the city. She received her military training at Karlberg
and served in the war against the Russian Empire.
Ester is haunted by the trauma of a bloody battle
in which the Swedish army was heavily defeated. She
has fragmentary memories of the panic that ensued
when the Tsar’s forces broke through the Swedish
lines and captured their camp. Soldiers stormed into
the medical tent. The battle was brief and Ester was
knocked to the ground.
