After this event, Ester Volk concludes that her
curse cannot be controlled and must be stopped
at all costs. She does not return to her workplace
but locks herself in her cellar.

During the mystery the conflicts will escalate step by
step. The following events can be used to up the pace
when necessary and push the players toward the final
confrontation. Each step of the Countdown takes
place during the hours of darkness. If the game is
progressing quickly, multiple phases can occur on
the same evening or night.

CATASTROPHE

COUNTDOW N

1. Friedrich Baum, organist at Saint Gertrude’s
Church, is found brutally murdered. The characters can be drawn there by screams or fetched
by Utterson’s constables. The murder was committed by Volk in werewolf form. See the section
Saint Gertrude’s Church on page 32.
2. Field Surgeon Jens Svärdh is brutally attacked
at the statue of Saint George and the Dragon.
Again, the characters can be drawn there by
screams or fetched by police constables. Svärdh
survives the attack, which is witnessed by his wife,
and is taken to the Seraphim Hospital. Once again
it is Volk who is behind the attack, which leads
to Svärdh contracting lycanthropy and turning
into a werewolf. He flees the hospital and begins
to prowl the alleys of Stockholm. See the section
Saint George and the Dragon on page 33.
3. A fire watcher in the Town Between the Bridges
is attacked and viciously killed by Jens Svärdh
in werewolf form. The characters can witness the
attack or be drawn there by the desperate screams
of the fire watcher. Under a magnificent full moon,
the werewolf bares its teeth and growls. The characters can fight or try to escape.
However, the werewolf is interrupted by a distant howl. It howls back at the full moon and runs
away. The characters can go after it – make an AGILITY roll opposed by the werewolf’s BODY CONTROL
(for stats, see page 160 of the rulebook). If the
characters win, they manage to follow the werewolf for a while, in the direction of Ester Volk’s

If the player characters fail to stop Svärdh, the werewolf unleashes a bloodbath in the alleys. Unlike Volk,
Svärdh kills indiscriminately. Panic breaks out and
the police struggle to control the angry and terrified
residents. If Volk is alive, her killing continues as
well. The police pursue the werewolves but fail to
catch them, and the murders keep piling up. A few
months later, Svärdh’s body is found in Haga Park
with his heart torn out.

DREAMS AND VISIONS
The player characters could suffer visions
and nightmares during their stay in Stock­
holm, triggered by the gruesome events. The
dreams show images of Stockholm at night.
The dreamer is chasing someone and feels a
strong sense of hunger, blood, rage, and thirst
for vengeance. Do not describe a werewolf
too clearly in the dream. One tip is to weave
creatures from the character’s trauma or peo­
ple from their dark secret into the dream, to
make it more personal. A typical nightmare
has Fear value 1.
