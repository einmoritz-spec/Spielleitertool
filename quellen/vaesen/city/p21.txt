CENTRAL AND PERIPHERAL CLUES
The clues the player characters can find are
either central or peripheral to the plot. Central
clues are vital for solving the mystery; peripheral
clues are not, but provide additional information
that may be useful. The players do not need to
be handed central clues on a silver platter, but
there are several ways for the characters to
discover them during the adventure. Central
clues are marked with the symbol in the text.
