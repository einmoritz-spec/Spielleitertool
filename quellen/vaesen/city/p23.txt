The Wounds: As for Sverker von Essen, Volk says
his throat was slashed with great force and that he
died from extensive blood loss. She has nothing
more to say. Perhaps it was a wild dog?
The Conductor: If asked about Mortimer Cronstedt,
the conductor at the Opera, Volk says he was found
dead six weeks ago at the Golden Peace, face down in
a bowl of turtle soup. Volk examined the body and
concluded that he had been poisoned, but found
no evidence of who or what might be responsible.
The Society: If Volk learns that the Society has
an interest in the supernatural, she becomes very
curious, asking all kinds of questions about ways
to appease or deal with vaesen. She also tells them
about the National Archives on Knights’ Isle (page
31), which has a special Esoteric section on
vaesen and other supernatural matters, and recommends that they go there for information.
