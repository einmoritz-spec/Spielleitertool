ESTER VOLK
Ester Volk is woman of medium height with
fair hair and piercing green eyes. She speaks
Finnish-­Swedish. Straight-­backed and
methodical, she moves around the morgue
with calm composure. Her face is scarred
from the war against the Tsar’s armies in the
eastern province. You can read more about
her past under Background on page 10.
