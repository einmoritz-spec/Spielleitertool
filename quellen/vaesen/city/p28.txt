THE MUTILATED BEGGAR

Henrik Öhman runs the kitchen at the
Golden Peace. He is loud and warm and
known in Stockholm for his version of Potage
Tortue, or turtle soup – a very fashionable dish
at this time.

At some point in the beginning of the mystery,
a dirty beggar comes up to the player char­
acters and asks for money. His right hand looks
nasty – it is all pale and smells terribly. Given
the rumors of disease in the slums (see the
City Gazette newspaper), the encounter may
frighten the characters.
If they give the man a coin or two (cost
level 1) and take the time to speak with him,
he tells them that a well-­dressed gentleman
offered him money to cut off his hand and
replace it with another. He does not know the
man’s name.
This event can occur anywhere in the city.
The characters can even run into the same man
multiple times.
