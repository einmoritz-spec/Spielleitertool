LYSA NDER
CORK FACTORIES INC.
August Lysander’s company has a mansion on Shipside
Street with a magnificent view of the Stream and the
ships that are anchored there. The building dates from
the 17th century and has a large, ornate door decorated
with mermaids holding gas lamps. A beautiful marble staircase and gilded sconces can be seen inside the
door. Lysander has had the palace renovated with the
latest technology and built an iron-­grate elevator from
the ground floor all the way up to his office at the top.

Guards: The office is well guarded. There is always
a man posted inside the door, armed with knuckledusters and a pistol, and another three are constantly
on duty in the building. But if the characters come
knocking during the day, they are let in and shown
to the top floor office where Lysander receives them.

·

Guidance: August Lysander is curious about the
player characters’ investigation and has lots of
questions. He has no concrete clues to offer them
during this mystery, but can provide hints if they
get stuck, as he is very knowledgeable about vaesen.
The Snuff Box: During the visit, the player characters may notice that Lysander has a gilded snuff box
in his waistcoat pocket. A sharp-­eyed player character (roll for VIGILANCE) may see that it is decorated
with the monogram “CL.” Under no circumstances
does Lysander let the player characters know what
is in the snuff box. It plays no role in this mystery,
but becomes important later in the campaign.
