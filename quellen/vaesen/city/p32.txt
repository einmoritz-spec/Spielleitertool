FIELD SURGEON
JENS SVÄ RDH
Doctor Jens Svärdh has served many years as a
field surgeon in the Swedish army. Inspired by
the scientific advances of this time, he has had
increasingly innovative ideas in recent years.
One such idea, which has almost become an
obsession for him, is the possibility of transplanting body parts such as arms and legs from
dying, or recently deceased, patients. This
would help the city’s factory owners keep experienced workers on the job even after work-­
related accidents, which are very common.
