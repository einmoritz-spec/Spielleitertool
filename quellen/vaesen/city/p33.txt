The Royal Opera was founded by King Gustav III in
the late 18th century, inspired by French architecture. Its facade on Gustav Adolf’s Square has four
columns, and the auditorium and foyer are decorated in white and gold.
Gustav III was murdered in this building during
a masked ball, and at the time of this mystery the
Opera is putting on Giuseppe Verdi’s A Masked Ball
which depicts this very assassination. Superstar Jenny
Lind plays Amelia, while the role of Gustav III was
played by master tenor Sverker von Essen. Since his
death, up-­and-­comer Rufus Holberg has quite literally taken up the mantle.
