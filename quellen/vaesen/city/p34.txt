RUFUS HOLBERG
The young lyric tenor Rufus Holberg is tall
and often dresses in velvet. He is intense
and expressive, gesturing with his delicate
hands as he speaks. His bright green eyes
are framed by dark eyebrows and eyelashes,
and his curly dark brown hair falls over his
shoulders. He works at the Royal Opera as
a singer but is also a frequent actor. When
the player characters meet him at the opera,
he is wearing his costume as Gustav III at
the masked ball.

Alibi: If the characters ask where Holberg was on
