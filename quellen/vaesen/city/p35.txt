THE NATIONA L A RCHI V ES
The National Archives has been collecting records,
documents, books, and other items of particular
importance to the kingdom for hundreds of years. It
is one of the nation’s oldest government authorities,
dating back to the 13th century. The impressive stone
building on Knights’ Isle is newly constructed and
built on a cast iron frame to reduce the risk of fire.
The floors rest on iron beams and are supported
by slender cast iron columns. A spiral book chute
runs through the entire stairwell. Light shines in
through beautiful leaded windows. The bookcases
are also made of iron and the doors of sheet metal.
The National Archives is not open to the public.

CLUES

Transformation: Werewolves are affected by the
phases of the moon. Most werewolves transform
on the night of a full moon or the nights immediately preceding or following it. Some werewolves
can also transform during the day if they experience strong emotions.
Killing a Werewolf: A werewolf can only be killed
with a silver weapon or by another werewolf.
The True Name: A werewolf in wolf form turns
back into a human if someone close to it calls it by
its true name. However, this method only lifts the
curse temporarily – the person transforms again
at the next full moon.
Curing the Curse: It is said that a werewolf who
tears out and devours the unborn child of a pregnant woman is permanently cured of the curse.
Calendars: The National Archives has astronomical
charts and calendars that let the characters know that
the brutal murders all occurred near the full moon
(page 18). No roll is required to realize this.

BACK TO THE NATIONAL ARCHIVES
In a later adventure in this book, the player
characters will have the opportunity to revisit
the National Archives and uncover even deeper
mysteries in the bowels of the building. It is there­
fore important that the National Archives is not
destroyed or significantly altered in this mystery.
