Saint George and the Dragon is a famous landmark in
the Town Between the Bridges. This statue of bronze
and stone on Merchants Square is a replica of a medieval wooden statue in the nearby Cathedral, which
some believe symbolizes the wars between Sweden
and Denmark. Below the statue on East Longstreet,
there is a large fountain.
The player characters have no reason to come here
until after step 2 of the Countdown (page 16), when
Doctor Jens Svärdh is attacked by the werewolf. They
are assumed to arrive here shortly thereafter. Should
they visit this location before the attack, there are no
clues to be found.

Testimony: The shocked Märta Svärdh can be
questioned right away or later at the Seraphim
Hospital. She tearfully describes how she and her
husband were on their way home to their apartment on Merchants Street after an evening at the
Golden Peace. Suddenly a dark figure appeared by
the fountain and knocked her to the ground. As she
regained her senses, she saw her husband’s blood-­
soaked body in the fountain. She saw nothing of
the attacker but remembers a growl, like that of a
big dog, and a pair of “terrible green eyes.” She knows
nothing about her husband’s surgical experiments.

DOCTOR SVÄRDH’S JOURNAL
Subject 16: 25 years old male. Good general condition.
No sign of pulmonary tuberculosis. Weight 45 kg, height
160 cm. Laudanum administered preoperatively.
Graft: right forearm, 21 years old male, retrieved on October 27th from the Sofia Churchyard. Chilled for three days.
Course of procedure: Amputation successful. Blood loss
acceptable. The graft is infused with saline. Necrotic tissue
minimal, can be attached after debridement. Indications
of perfusion in the transplanted tissue.
Day 1: Patient receives regular analgesia. Develops fever and
chills in the evening. The graft shows no signs of perfusion.
Day 2: Patient unconscious. Feverish. Clinical signs of sepsis.
The graft is removed. Time of death: 16.37. Body discarded
in the usual place.
