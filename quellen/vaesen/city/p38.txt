The Seraphim Hospital is located near the Karlberg
Canal on King’s Isle and surrounded by a lush park.
Built in the 18th century, it is the nation’s first modern hospital. Between the buildings there are several
patios where patients and nurses in white caps can
be seen during the day.
The player characters can visit the hospital to ask
about the disease in the slums, follow the trail of the
body snatchers who are dropping off bodies here (page
28), or after step 2 of the Countdown (page 16)
when Doctor Jens Svärdh is attacked by the werewolf
and brought here in an ambulance carriage.

operating room must sneak their way through the
hospital (STEALTH ) and pick the lock (STEALTH ) or
break down the door (FORCE ).
Cellar Door: If the characters follow the trail of
the body snatchers in White Hills (page 28)
and investigate the alley behind the hospital, an
INVESTIGATION roll reveals wheel tracks leading to
a basement door. The door is locked but can be
picked with STEALTH or forced open with FORCE .
Inside is Doctor Svärdh’s operating room.

Transformation: If the characters come here
after the werewolf’s attack on Svärdh (step 2 of
the Countdown) and want to see him, they are
shown to the room on the second floor where he
is being treated. His wife Märta is waiting outside. In the room they are met with a shocking
sight – a nurse is lying on the floor in a large pool
of blood, badly lacerated (Fear test against Fear
value 1). The window has been smashed from the
inside and the cold autumn wind flows into the
room. The nurse’s life cannot be saved.
Note that the above happens even if the characters arrive shortly after the attack on Svärdh –
his transformation is very rapid. From this point
on, Doctor Svärdh roams the streets as a werewolf
(see step 3 of the Countdown).
Trail of Blood: Doctor Gregorius cannot explain
Svärdh’s disappearance as his injuries were very
severe. Characters looking for clues can make an
INVESTIGATION roll to find a trail of blood in the
park outside the hospital. It leads towards the
Town Between the Bridges.
