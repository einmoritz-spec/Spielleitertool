ESTER VOLK’S A PA RTMENT
Coroner Ester Volk lives alone in an apartment on
Charity Street. At the entrance to the building grows
a small chestnut tree. Louise Stefansson, the housekeeper who cleans and cooks for Doctor Volk, lives
on the ground floor as well. The player characters
will probably have no reason to come here until the
end of the adventure (see Confrontation below), but can
visit the place earlier if they like.

CLUES

Strange Sounds: If the characters get Mrs. Stefansson talking and ask her about Volk, she tells
them that she has been hearing strange noises
from Volk’s apartment, and that the coroner often
works all night.
Talking to Volk: If Volk herself welcomes the characters in her apartment, she can provide the same
information as at the police station (page 18). She
tries to end the conversation as quickly as possible.

CH A LLENGES

The Housekeeper: If the characters arrive before
step 3 of the Countdown, Mrs. Stefansson is suspicious and will not let them in. It takes a successful MANIPULATION roll to get her to talk (see Clues).
Sneaking: Characters trying to enter unnoticed must pass a STEALTH roll to pick the lock
and another one to sneak into Volk’s apartment.
The latter is an opposed roll against Stefansson’s
VIGILANCE (8 dice in total as she has excellent hearing). If discovered, they are brusquely kicked out
by Mrs. Stefansson.
Hidden Door: Volk’s simply furnished apartment
consists of two rooms and a kitchen. There is nothing suspicious in here, but an observant character
searching the room (INVESTIGATION roll) notices
scrape marks in the shape of a quarter-­circle at
a bookcase in the living room. The bookcase can
be pulled out to reveal a narrow staircase down
to a basement vault.
Laboratory: In the basement vault there is a small
laboratory with Bunsen burners and glass flasks.
Heavy chains with iron shackles are attached to the
stone wall. On a worktable lies a revolver loaded
with silver bullets. A basement window near the
ceiling lets in a bit of sunshine or moonlight. The
air is stale and smells of chemicals and fur.
Volk: During the day, Ester Volk is at the police
station. In the evening, she may be at home, in
which case she lets characters in if it is not too late.
However, she wants to keep the conversation short
as she claims to be very tired. A successful OBSERVATION roll reveals that Volk is hiding something.

BLOOD IN THE ALLEYS
In the lead-­up to the Confrontation, it may be
a good idea to have Svärdh commit additional
murders (after step 3 of the Countdown) – the
werewolf kills indiscriminately, spreading fear
through the Town Between the Bridges. This is
an effective way to put pressure on the players
while they are trying to figure out who is behind
it all. However, do not let the characters con­
front Svärdh before they have confronted Volk.

CONFRONTATION
The player characters’ investigation can play out in
a variety of ways. A likely ending is described below,
but what actually happens depends on the player
characters’ actions and you may need to adjust the
final confrontation accordingly.
