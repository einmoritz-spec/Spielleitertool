After step 3 of the Countdown, when Svärdh has turned
into a werewolf, Volk concludes that her curse cannot be
controlled and must be stopped at all costs. She chains

(page 35). The characters should come here at night,
or at least after dark, when the streets are lit by a large
full moon.

A W EREWOLF IN CH A INS
If the player characters visit Volk’s apartment after step
3 of the Countdown, Mrs. Stefansson lets them in
immediately. She is deeply concerned as Doctor Volk
recently came home (shortly after step 3) and was very
upset. She has not been seen since, and Stefansson has
heard strange screams and cries from the apartment.
The housekeeper has not dared to enter the apartment, but she is happy that the characters are here

and lets them in with her spare key. If they search
the place, they must still pass an INVESTIGATION roll to
find the secret door. On failure the characters hear
a howling sound coming from the bookcase – do not
make it too difficult to find the entrance.
In the basement, Doctor Volk has chained herself
to the wall after realizing that she is the killer and is
losing control of herself. The key is on the floor next
to her. She explains that she has been trying to create
an elixir to control her transformations, but it is not
working. In great anguish, she asks the characters to
kill her: “It’s the only way to stop the disease. There is no cure.”

CURING A W EREWOLF
Let the player characters discuss what they want to do.
According to the information found in the National
Archives (page 31) – and the Vaesen art book – there
are three ways to break a werewolf’s curse:
The werewolf is killed with a silver weapon.
The werewolf rips out and eats the unborn child
of a pregnant woman.
Someone close to the werewolf calls it by its real
name. However, this method only lifts the curse
temporarily – the person transforms again at the
next full moon.

UTTERSON SHOWS UP
Before anything conclusive happens, Commissioner
Utterson shows up. He too has become suspicious of
Volk and wants to question her. He does not believe in
werewolves and demands that the characters immediately release Volk and take her to the police station for
questioning. If they refuse, he will do it himself.
