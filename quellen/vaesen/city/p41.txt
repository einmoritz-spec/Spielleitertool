THE SECOND W EREWOLF
In the middle of the confrontation with Volk and/
or Utterson, the player characters hear thuds and
screams coming from street level – Mrs. Stefansson
shrieks in terror. If Utterson just left the basement,
he too can be heard shouting from above.
If they rush upstairs, the characters encounter a terrible beast – Doctor Svärdh in the form
of a shaggy werewolf. Stefansson (and Utterson if
he is still there) are by the doorway, shocked but
unharmed. All player characters must make a Fear
test against Fear value 2 (1 if they have encountered
a werewolf before).
Combat: The werewolf immediately attacks, and
the characters have a very tough fight ahead of them.
For stats, see page 160 of the rulebook. Killing the
werewolf requires silver weapons, otherwise it flees
when Broken and will soon return to kill more victims on the streets.
Mrs. Svärdh: A clever way to defeat Svärdh in werewolf form is to seek out his wife Märta at the couple’s
apartment on Merchants Street. If she addresses him
by his true name, Svärdh reverts to human form and
becomes much easier to deal with.
Werewolf Versus Werewolf: If Volk is still alive when
Svärdh attacks, she begs the characters to release her
so she can fight the other werewolf. If they do, she
turns into a werewolf within one round and then
engages Svärdh. The two werewolves have at each
other in a brutal duel that spills out onto the street.

Finally, Volk rips Svärdh’s throat out with her fangs,
killing him instantly. With inscrutable eyes and bloody
jaws, Ester Volk glares at the player characters, hesitating
for a moment before rushing off into the darkness. She
then leaves Stockholm and flees into the countryside.
If the characters try to stop her, her rage takes over and
she fights to the death – or until she manages to escape.

AFTERMATH
If the player characters kill or capture Jens Svärdh,
they have prevented the Catastrophe and solved the
mystery. They should also be awarded additional
experience points if they:
Found out who murdered the poor girls from the
White Hills.
Solved the poisoning of conductor Mortimer
Cronstedt.
Exposed Doctor Svärdh’s ghoulish experiments.
Sent the money from the dying Johan Petterson in
the White Hills to his family in Smolandia.

The killings stop and calm settles over Stockholm –
for now. The City of My Nightmares campaign continues
in the next mystery, set a few months after this one.
If Ester Volk is alive, she flees the capital and is never
seen again – or maybe the player characters will meet
her again at some point?

LYSA NDER
August T. Lysander can visit the player characters
during the aftermath of the mystery, before they leave
Stockholm or when they are back in Upsala. He thanks
them for their efforts (or expresses his sympathies if
the Catastrophe occurred). He also offers to take care
of Ester Volk’s work and ensure that her findings are
preserved. Finally, Lysander bids them farewell with
the assurance that they will soon meet again.
