SONG TO THE MOON
Oh moon in the deep sky,
your light sees far,
you roam over the wide world,
and peer into human dwellings.
Oh moon, stay a while,
tell me, where is my love?
Tell him, silvery moon,
that my arms enfold him,
so that for at least a moment
he’ll remember me in his dream.
– From the opera Rusalka by Antonín DvoŘák

This mystery takes place in the Stock­holm Archipelago, made up of thousands of small islands in the
Baltic Sea. The islands can be reached by steamboat.
In the archipelago, Stock­holm’s upper class enjoy
the short Swedish summer at their summer homes.
The challenge for the player characters in this mystery is to solve a murder case with both human and
supernatural suspects.
