CONFLICTS
The primary conflict of this mystery is between Lucius
Lysander and Vesper Lysander, his first wife who died
on their wedding night and was turned into a rusalka.
The secondary conflict is between Lucius Lysander
and his friend Florian Schiller. Florian quickly suspects a connection between Lucius and the mysterious deaths in the waters around the island. As the
mystery unfolds, this leads to the death of Florian
himself (see Countdown on page 45).

IN V ITATION
This mystery takes place in early summer, when the
evenings are long and bright. The country is plagued
by unrest. There are whispers that a revolution may be
brewing, and lately there have been more and more
voices critical of the king.
One morning, an envelope with a wax seal is delivered to Castle Gyllencreutz by an errand boy. The
letter contains an invitation from August T. Lysander
to visit him at his summer estate in the Stock­holm
Archipelago (handout 2A on page 109, also available
for download on the Free League website):

PREPA R ATIONS
As usual, the player characters can prepare for the journey by purchasing equipment (page 73 of the core rulebook) or using Upgrades at Castle Gyllencreutz (page
89 of the core rulebook). They can research Goose
Island, and with a successful LEARNING test they find a
record of a death seven years earlier – a woman called
Vesper Stiernkors disappeared on the island during her
wedding night. The case was written off as a probable
suicide. The characters can also seek more information about August T. Lysander, but find nothing except
what was available in the previous mystery (page 13).

THE JOURNEY
The player characters can take the train to Stock­holm
and on to Strömkajen Quay at the King’s Garden
Park, where August Lysander’s private steamer Aegir
is waiting. The young engineer and helmsman Peter
Eriksson (page 54) welcomes them aboard.
The voyage takes a couple of hours and goes through
the Stock­holm Archipelago past the Royal Forest and
the island of Lidingö. Nuala Fitzgerald (page 46)
and Florian Schiller (page 47) are already on the
steamer, deep in conversation. Feel free to roleplay
the meeting with them and show their portraits to the
players. As usual the characters can gain an Advantage
during the journey (page 182 of the core rulebook).
