DREAMS AND VISIONS
During the player characters’ voyage and visit
to Goose Island, they may be afflicted by visions
and nightmares. These dreams may involve dark
waters, heartbreak, and revenge. However, do
not describe the image of a rusalka too clearly
in the dream. To make it more personal, you
can also weave a character’s trauma or dark
secret into the dream. A typical nightmare has
a Fear value of 1.
