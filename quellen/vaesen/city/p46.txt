THE CONCL AV E
The evening after the player characters arrive at Goose
Island, August Lysander begins his Conclavium Sub Rosa
in the grand salon of his villa. The meeting starts
with a sumptuous dinner. Lucius sabers a bottle of
champagne from a winery in France with which he
does business.

After dinner the group retires to the large, glazed
veranda to begin their conversation in the bright
early summer evening. It is ten o’clock, but the sun
has not yet begun to set. The topic of the evening is
pacts with supernatural beings, which the invited
guests have deep knowledge of. August Lysander asks
questions and listens.
If your gaming group enjoys roleplaying, you can
play the conclave as a discussion of how to make and
break pacts with supernatural beings, with player
characters and NPCs exchanging thoughts and ideas.
Alternatively, you can make the conclave more of a
lecture, with the NPCs providing information that
the players can use in this mystery or later in the campaign. Use the NPC descriptions on the following
pages and the specific points below as needed.
Nuala Fitzgeraldtells the story of her great-­aunt
who asked a fairy to bring her apple orchard a good
harvest. The fairy granted her wish in exchange
for the first fruit to ripen on the farm each year.
What the woman did not realize was that she was
also promising the fairies the first children born
into her family each year. Nuala’s own eldest sister disappeared from her cradle; she herself never
married and does not want children of her own.
Nuala believes that deals made with vaesen cannot be broken, but that the terms can be renegotiated and transferred to a vaesen with more
powerful magic.
Florian Schilleris invited to the conclave
because, in addition to being a friend of Lucius
Lysander, he has devoted his studies to the second
part of Goethe’s play Faust, in which Dr. Faust is
forgiven and admitted into Heaven. Schiller’s
theory is that the ritual to the Egyptian goddess
Isis in Faust actually describes encounters with
powerful vaesen. He is convinced that the allegorical text contains instructions on how to break
pacts with evil vaesen through a ritual and thereby
save one’s soul.
