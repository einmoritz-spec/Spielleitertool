After the Deaths: The conclave continues during
the days on Goose Island, even after the deaths begin
to occur (see Countdown on page 45). At this point,

time to provide the player characters with clues pointing to a rusalka (see sidebar).

RUSALKA
A rusalka is an undead female water spirit featured
in Slavic mythology – not Norse. As such, it is not
mentioned in the Vaesen art book that players may
use as a resource. There are several ways to help the
players understand which vaesen they may be deal­
ing with. Use the following information as needed:
Florian Schiller, Nuala Fitzgerald, or August
Lysander points out that the deaths do not seem
to fit any Nordic vaesen they know of. A lake wife
is the closest they can think of, but this vaesen
seems more murderous in nature.
Count Konstantinovich informs the player char­
acters that in his home region of Eastern Europe
there are undead creatures more terrible than the
Nordic ones. He suggests that they study the sub­
ject, perhaps in the library? If the players are com­
pletely stuck, Konstantinovich can simply explain
that the deaths seem to be the work of a rusalka.
The rusalka is described in Johan Egerkrans’s art
book The Undead. You can let the players use this
book as a resource during the mystery as there
are similar works in Lysander’s library.
If all else fails, you can have the characters make
a LEARNING test to recall an old story from Kraków,

If the player characters try to find out more about
the rusalka, the chosen NPC, the book The Undead, or
a particularly successful LEARNING test in the library
can provide one or more of the following pieces of
information:
A rusalka is the undead spirit of a woman who
has been murdered or taken her own life and
seeks revenge on the living, preferably young men.
A rusalka usually drowns its victims but is also known
to tickle them to death as it laughs with delight.
A rusalka cannot move freely on land and must
always stay in or near the water.
A rusalka has webbed feet, which can leave dis­
tinct tracks.
Garlic, lovage, or wormwood can ward off a rusalka.
A rusalka is a form of undead and cannot be
killed, only temporarily banished. The only way
to banish it permanently is to bury its remains in
consecrated ground.
