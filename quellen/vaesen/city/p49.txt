THE RUSALKA ATTACKS

As the mystery unfolds, the conflicts will escalate step
by step. The following events can be used to increase
the pace of the game when necessary, and to push the
players toward the final confrontation. Not all steps
have to occur.

In addition to the countdown, at any time during
the mystery, you can have the rusalka attack a
male player character who is near the water.
These attacks can occur multiple times.

COUNTDOW N

1. The rusalka drowns engineer Peter Eriksson
at the boathouse. Shortly afterwards, horrified
cries are heard from the steamboat jetty, where
the servants or musicians find his body floating
in the water. His feet are bound with long strands
of seaweed and his pale face is frozen in a smile.
The body is placed in the villa’s root cellar. A player
character who examines the corpse and passes a MEDICINE test can conclude that the cause of death was
drowning. There are no other injuries on the body.
August Lysander is upset and a character who
passes a VIGILANCE test hears him exclaim “Not again!”
A moment later, he summons the characters and tells
them that several similar deaths have occurred in the
waters around the island in recent years. He suspects
that something unnatural is behind these incidents
and asks for their help in solving the mystery.
2. The rusalka attacks Konstantin Konstantinovich as he walks along the water’s edge, deep in
thought. Suddenly, the Count is heard crying for
help. The player characters can hear the screams
and see him being pulled into the water and disappear into the deep.
A character who swims after him notices the
smiling face of a pale young woman under the surface. She quickly grabs the character’s legs and tries
to pull the victim down. See stats on page 60.
The lifeless body of Count Konstantinovich is
found shortly thereafter and brought to the root
cellar while awaiting the police. His body is also
wrapped in seaweed and his face frozen in a smile.
3. Florian Schiller goes missing. No one has seen
him since the night before, when one of the servants saw him walking toward Little Goose Island.

If the player characters successfully OBSERVE the
witness, they realize that there is something the
person is not telling them, and with a successful
MANIPULATION test, the witness reveals that Lucius
Lysander went after Florian shortly afterwards.
If the characters go to Little Goose Island, they
find Florian dead on the shore with dried seaweed
on his legs. If they examine his body and pass
a MEDICINE test, they notice strangulation marks
around his neck (which the previous victims did
not have). There is no smile on his lips; rather,
his face is contorted in horror.
The truth is that Florian was not killed by the
rusalka, but by Lucius. Suspecting that Vesper in the
form of a rusalka might be behind the deaths, Florian went to Little Goose Island to find her remains
and bring her peace. Lucius followed him there, and
Florian confronted him. In a moment of weakness,
Lucius told him the truth about Vesper’s death.
Florian urged him to confess to the murder, but
Lucius refused. Florian told him about rituals he
had encountered in his studies. These described a
way for even the damned to break curses and find
salvation. Lucius was interested in the ritual but
still would not confess. The conversation turned
into an argument, and eventually Lucius strangled
Florian. He placed seaweed on his friend’s body
to make the death look like the others.
