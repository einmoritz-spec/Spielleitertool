KONSTA NTIN
KONSTA NTINOV ICH
“Let the flesh instruct the mind.”
The Count of Yamburg is a tall man with dark
hair and intense eyes framed by heavy eyebrows. He wears a turn-­of-­the-­century suit
with a high collar and a purple cravat, even
in the hot Swedish summer. During the day
he always stays indoors or under an umbrella.
Konstantinovich is a charismatic figure who
likes to make long speeches to his audience.
The count is known for his imaginative
and profound books on vaesen and mythical
creatures. The Society often uses his texts as
reference material when describing encounters with some of the more unusual vaesen
of Eastern Europe, especially the undead.
Konstantinovich has come to the island
in his own two-­masted ship, manned by two
servants, and that is also where he sleeps. He
eats sparingly but drinks quite a bit and likes to
stay up late discussing supernatural mysteries.

COMBAT

Vampir: The count’s knowledge of the
undead has been gained through personal
experience and research over a supernaturally long life. He is a vampir, also known as a
vampire. He is driven by a genuine interest
in understanding more about the mysteries
of life and death, and thus his own existence.
Konstantinovich will not harm anyone on
the island. He does not need much blood and
can satisfy his thirst by drinking moderately
from his two servants.
It is unlikely that the player characters
will come into direct conflict with the count,
but his stats are presented below just in case.
Technically, Konstantinovich is treated as
a vaesen, not as a regular NPC. Vampires
are described in more detail in the separate
expansion Mythic Carpathia.
