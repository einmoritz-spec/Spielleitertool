GOOSE ISLAND
STOCKHOLM ARCHIPELAGO

LOCATIONS
Big Goose Island is a small island near Vaxholm
in the Stock­holm Archipelago, about 650 meters
long and 200 meters wide at its widest point.
East of the main island is the uninhabited Little
Goose Island, which is connected to Big Goose
Island by a narrow stone embankment.
Goose Island is owned by the Lysander family and holds only a few buildings. The rest of
the land is covered with forest, pastures, and
orchards. Its only permanent resident is Helga
Danielsson, a fisher­woman who lives in an old
cottage.
The following paragraphs summarize the most
important locations the player characters may
explore on the island. In addition to the buildings
described, there is also a barn of no importance
to the mystery. See the adjacent map.
The player characters cannot leave the island
until the end of the mystery – see the sidebar on
page 46.
