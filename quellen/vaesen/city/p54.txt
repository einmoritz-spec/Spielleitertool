On the northwest tip of Big Goose Island stands the
white villa of the Lysander family, built on a slope
down to the water. Like many summer homes in the
archipelago, the four-­story residence is made of wood
with decorative glass verandas facing the water. The
house is surrounded by a garden whose lilac hedges
provide protection from the sea breeze, and there
are several beehives in the vegetable garden.
Below the house is a steamboat jetty and a small
waiting cabin where travelers can seek shelter from
the rain or wind while waiting for the steamers that
dock on the island a few times a week. The outhouse is
located behind the villa, near the large root cellar where
food and beverages that need to be kept cool are stored.
The ground floor has a large entrance hall and a
stairwell to the upper floors, but otherwise it mostly
consists of a cellar with storage rooms. The back wall
and half of the side walls face the hillside and are
covered with stone.

The fourth floor is the Lysanders’ private quarters,
with four bedrooms, a bathroom, a private sitting
room, and a large balcony. August, Lucius, Hedwig,
and their son Alexander each have a bedroom here.
In the stairwell, a door with a window blocks the way
to this floor and is usually kept locked. Everyone in
the Lysander family has a key.

On the second floor is a large, bright kitchen with
lime green wall panels. In large windows with lace
curtains, there are demijohns with rum and fruit.
It smells lovely, and along the wall is a large wood
stove crowded with freshly baked bread, rhubarb pie,
and cinnamon buns. In the middle of the kitchen is
a large table where Lonni the cook works tirelessly.
Adjacent to the kitchen is a hallway that leads to several guest rooms and the servants’ quarters. A hidden
staircase leads from this hallway to the upper floors
(see Challenges).
