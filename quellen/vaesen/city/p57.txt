In addition to valet Karl-­Axel Trygg, there
are three servants in the house. They serve
the Lysanders and go with the family to the
archipelago every summer. Some of them
were placed with wealthy families by their
parents at an early age to work for food and
shelter. They would rather work hard than
starve at home in their own families’ cold,
cramped rooms in Stock­holm’s working-­
class neighborhoods. Several of them draw
hope from the fact that Hedwig, who came
to the island as a maid, has now become part
of the Lysander family.
