Death in the Archipelago
Vaxholm police report that Vesper Lysander,
born Stiernkors, has died on Goose Island in the
archipelago. Mrs. Lysander disappeared the
night after her wedding to Lucius Lysander, son
of cork magnate August Lysander. According
to witnesses, she was in a confused state and
went into the sea by herself. The police chief
of Vaxholm does not suspect foul play.

The boathouse has two entrances, one on land and
one offshore, and the floor is open to the water. It is
painted the same light color as the villa. Inside are
tools and fishing equipment. It smells of tar and seawater. Lysander’s steamer Aegir is moored here when
not in use. Also in the boathouse are Peter Eriksson’s
quarters, a cramped little room to the right of the
entrance. Along the walls are simple shelves with
his belongings and some books on beekeeping and
gardening.

THE BURNT PAGE
Another drowning. Somehow V is still here, in the
waters – that must be it! The drownings in recent
years cannot be a coincidence. Hedwig is beside
herself. She doesn’t even dare let Alexander out
into the garden. What have I done? I’m damned!

PETER ERIKSSON
Engineer Peter Eriksson is 25 years old, with
blonde hair and brown eyes. He is a trained
sea-­captain working as a steam engineer. He
drives August Lysander’s steamer, looks after
the Lysander family’s garden and beehives,
and sometimes takes the rowboat to Vaxholm
on small errands for the family. Peter lives in
a small side room in the boathouse.
