In the center of the island is a small red cottage with
white trim nestled in a lush garden. A path leads
from the front door through the manicured vegetable garden and on through an opening in the lilac
hedge, down to the fishing huts on the beach. Inside
the cottage, a fire blazes in a simple wood-­burning
stove, and on a small glass veranda at the back, the
fisherwoman Helga Danielsson can sit in the light of a
kerosene lamp as she mends the nets in the evenings.

VIGILANCE

Count Konstantinovich’s ship is moored at the
jetty in front of the boathouse. The crew consists of
two servants. They are quiet and pale and keep to
themselves, usually below deck. They do not speak
Swedish and refuse to answer questions or let the
player characters on board. The morning-­averse
count sleeps on his ship during the visit to Goose
Island.

Going Onboard: Sneaking aboard Count Konstantinovich’s ship requires a successful STEALTH
test against his servants’ VIGILANCE . If they are discovered, the servants will try to throw them off
the ship. If Konstantinovich himself is present,
he will not take part in the fighting, but instead
tries to talk some sense into the player characters.

·

Bite Marks: If the player characters encounter
the count’s servants up close and pass a VIGILANCE
test, they notice that both have bite marks on their
necks.
Coffin: If the player characters search the boat,
they find an ornate black coffin lined with soil
below deck. If this happens after Konstantinovich
has disappeared from the root cellar (step 4 of
the Countdown), he will be lying here, seemingly lifeless.

HELGA DA NIELSSON
“All fishermen know there are things
in the deep than we can’t even imagine.”
The fisherwoman can be seen walking around
the island in the early mornings with her
big shaggy dog. Helga Danielsson is a quiet
woman in her 70s. She has a face that looks
grumpy at first glance but breaks into a
friendly smile when she looks up. Her hair
is short and gray, her body bent but strong,
and her nimble, tanned hands fly over the
meshes as she mends her nets.
A widow for many years, Helga wears her
husband’s wedding ring on a chain around
her neck. Her two sons have left Goose Island
for jobs in Stock­holm and only come out to
visit her at Christmas and Easter. Neither of
them wanted to take over the fishing business, so Helga runs it herself, selling her fish
to Lysander and at the market in Vaxholm.
