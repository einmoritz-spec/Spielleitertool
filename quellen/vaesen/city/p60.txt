CLUES

Vesper’s Death: The fisherwoman can tell the
player characters about Lucius’s first wife, Vesper,
and her tragic passing. Helga personally heard the
weeping Vesper pass by her cottage on the way to
Little Goose Island on her wedding night seven
years ago. She was sobbing loudly. A moment later,
Helga heard the footsteps of another person on
the path behind the high hedge, but did not see
who it was.
The Drownings: Helga is convinced that the
drownings are the work of a vaesen. She explains
that fishermen in the area have occasionally
encountered lake wives, but she cannot reconcile the murders with what she has been told about
them.
Lysander’s Background: Helga can also reveal that
August Lysander’s real name is August Torstensson
and that he came from a poor working-­class family.
He earned his living as a laborer and occasionally shipped Helga’s fish to Vaxholm. Karl-­Axel

Hedwig: Here the player characters may encounter Hedwig, either by chance or by following her
here from the villa. She is upset, especially after
the first death, but can be calmed down with an
INSPIRATION test. See Clues.
Burial: If the characters find the rusalka’s remains
on Little Goose Island (page 57) and bury them
here, the rusalka will be at peace. See Confrontation.
CLUES

Hedwig’s Story: If the characters manage to calm
Hedwig down, she pours her heart out to them.
She tells them about the deaths on the island and
her belief that the island is cursed. She is terrified
that her son Alexander will be the next victim. If
pressed (MANIPULATION ), Hedwig reveals that she
thinks the curse began when Lucius’ first wife,
Vesper, took her life on the island seven years ago.
Hedwig now believes that her feelings for Lucius
drove Vesper mad and that the curse is therefore
her fault.
