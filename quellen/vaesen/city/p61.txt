LITTLE GOOSE ISL A ND
Big Goose Island and Little Goose Island are connected by a narrow stone embankment with a footpath
on it. There are no buildings on Little Goose Island,
just a place for Midsummer celebrations. Midsummer is still a few weeks away and last year’s withered
maypole is still there. There is also a small beach hut
and a bay with a shallow beach. The rest of the island
is covered with forest and great thickets of blackthorn.

CLUES

Footprints: There are strange footprints in the
sand on the beach. A successful LEARNING test reveals
that they seem to come from some kind of waterfowl with webbed feet, although they are remarkably large. These are the rusalka’s footprints.
Comb: A player character who investigates the
beach and passes an INVESTIGATION test finds a comb
in the sand. Stuck in the comb is a tuft of dark hair
and a piece of skin. This is the comb that Vesper
wore during her wedding and that the rusalka is
using to comb its hair.
Remains: Half-­buried in mud and hidden in
the reeds are Vesper’s remains. A skeleton is all
that is left, but pieces of her wedding dress can
still be seen. Her remains can only be found if
Lucius points out the place where Vesper died
or if the player characters spend several hours
actively searching the reeds around Little Goose
Island. One INVESTIGATION test is allowed per hour
until the remains are found. The rusalka will take
any opportunity to attack characters moving in
the reeds.

CONFRONTATION
The only way to bring peace to the rusalka and prevent
further deaths is to find Vesper’s remains and bury
them in consecrated ground – that is, at the small
chapel next to the villa. However, the way there can
be difficult, and the player characters’ investigation
can have many different outcomes. Some possibilities are listed below:
The player characters realize which vaesen they
are dealing with and lure the rusalka out on Little Goose Island. They can fight her, but even
if they win and banish her temporarily, she will
soon be back.
The player characters figure out that the rusalka
is connected to Lucius Lysander and confront
him. If they have overwhelming evidence and
pass a MANIPULATION test, Lucius breaks down and
confesses. He can then show them where to find
Vesper’s remains.
The player characters realize that they need to find
Vesper’s remains, but fail to make the connection
to Lucius or get him to confess. They can still find
the remains, but only after a long search in the
reeds around Little Goose Island, and at great
risk of being attacked by the rusalka (page 60).
