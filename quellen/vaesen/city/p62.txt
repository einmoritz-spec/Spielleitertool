THE RUSA LK A A PPEA RS
Lucius lies down in the coffin and closes his
eyes. The music stops and silence falls over
the clearing. Karl-­Axel makes the sign
of the cross. Suddenly a splash is heard
from the shore. The water ripples as a
woman’s head appears from the sea.
Lucius rises from the coffin and takes
a few hesitant steps toward the beach. “Oh,
goddess – you have come! Isis? Do my eyes fail me…?
Vesper?”
The rusalka steps into the torchlight,
revealing her true form – a fish-­eaten
corpse with dead eyes, her long, dark
hair partially torn and hanging
in stinking strands. She
still wears what is left
of her wedding dress
with a veil of seaweed. Long, sharp

claws protrude from her fingers. All player characters who have not previously encountered the rusalka
must make a Fear test against Fear value 2.
Unless the characters immediately intervene,
Karl-­Axel Trygg tries to stop her but is tossed aside
like a rag doll by the rusalka, leaving him unconscious on the ground. (He should preferably not
die here, as he returns later in the campaign.) She
then proceeds to rip out the musician’s throat, blood
spurting everywhere. Then she kills Lucius, unless
the characters stop her.
Lucius’s Confession: When Lucius sees the rusalka
and recognizes her as Vesper, he breaks down and
confesses to killing her – assuming the rusalka does
not kill him first. If he survives, he can tell or show
the player characters where Vesper’s remains can be
found (page 57).
The Rusalka Defeated: If the rusalka is Broken in
combat, she flees into the water and returns after a
few hours. She will continue to haunt the island’s
shores even after Lucius’s death – the only way to
permanently banish the rusalka is to bury her
remains at the chapel (however, see the Alternative Ending sidebar).

BURY ING V ESPER
The only consecrated ground on Goose
Island is the grassy area around the
chapel (page 56). If the player
characters find Vesper’s remains on
Little Goose Island, they can bury
them here. Feel free to play this out as
a moving scene. August Lysander
will participate if asked, as will
Hedwig and Lucius if he
is still alive. If Lucius is
dead when this happens, a sigh of relief
sweeps over the island.
The mystery is over.
