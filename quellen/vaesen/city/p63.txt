ALTERNATIVE ENDING

Lucius is Alive?If Lucius Lysander is alive when
the remains are buried, the players may think the
mystery is over, but that is not the case – the rusalka’s rage against Lucius is so great that she refuses
to rest until he is dead. She will keep tormenting
the island until Lucius is killed or forced to confess his crime.

If you are short on time, or if the players do not
find (or even care to look for) Vesper’s remains,
you can still end the mystery once Lucius has
met his fate. In that case the rusalka is not per­
manently banished, but she abandons Goose
Island for the time being and the player char­
acters can leave the island.

LUCIUS’S FATE
When Lucius’s crime is exposed, he agrees to turn
himself in to the police. The Vaxholm police station
is alerted and soon two policemen come rowing to
the island. Lucius lets himself be handcuffed while
August looks on with a grim expression.
The police boat leaves the island, but just a few
dozen meters from the shore there is a sudden gust of
wind that overturns the boat. Before anyone can come
to the rescue, Lucius disappears into the deep. The
policemen make it back ashore, but Lucius Lysander
is never seen again.
After this aftermath the mystery is over, provided
Vesper’s remains have been found and buried at the
chapel – otherwise she will continue to attack people
on the island until that happens (however, see the
Alternative Ending sidebar).

AFTERMATH
If the player characters uncover the truth about Vesper’s death, they will have averted the Catastrophe
and successfully solved the mystery. They should also
receive additional experience points if they:
Contributed their own stories during the conclave.
Realized that Konstantin Konstantinovich is a
vaesen.
Buried Vesper’s remains in consecrated ground.
