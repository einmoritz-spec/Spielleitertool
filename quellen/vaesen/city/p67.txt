· The Haunted Library ·

August T. Lysander (page 6) has continued
trying to free himself from the spertus’s curse. In
the course of his research he has made an amazing
discovery: the first owner of the spertus now in his
possession was none other than the world-­famous
botanist Carl Linnaeus.
Less well known is the fact that Linnaeus possessed
the Sight and for a time was the leader of the Order of
Artemis – his time’s equivalent of the Society. During
his expeditions in Sweden in the 18th century, first
to Lapland and then to Dalecarlia, Linnaeus kept a
secret journal of his encounters with supernatural
vaesen and stories told about them. He called the
journal Homo Ferus – you can read more about this on
page 81 of the core rulebook.
A few years after his expedition to Dalecarlia on
behalf of County Governor Nils Reuterholm, Linnaeus suddenly announced that he was leaving the
Society, claiming that he no longer had the Sight.
Shortly thereafter, the Society’s headquarters was
devastated by a fire that was believed to have destroyed
all records of the Dalecarlia expedition.
Lysander suspected that Linnaeus’s strange
behavior could be explained by the spertus (which
is only partly true – the whole truth will be revealed
at the end of this mystery) but refused to believe that
Linnaeus’s studies on vaesen were really gone. Perhaps the key to the spertus’s curse could be found
in Linnaeus’s lost writings? It was just a matter of
finding them.
His hopes were raised when he learned that Linnaeus had used an old cellar vault under Knights’
Isle in Stockholm as his private study and library. It
turned out that this vault was now located under the
beautiful, newly built National Archives. This could
not be a coincidence, thought Lysander, who now
believed the National Archives had been placed in
this very spot to preserve Linnaeus’s secrets.
But Lysander made a disappointing discovery
when he visited the National Archives in search of
Linnaeus’s secret library. The catacombs beneath the
National Archives were a maze, impossible to navigate. Lysander suspected that only someone with the

Sight could find their way to the library. Someone
like Linnaeus himself.
Lysander’s suspicions are correct, but what he does
not know is that the National Archives is haunted by the
ghost of none other than Carl Linnaeus himself. The
ghost will cause a lot of trouble during the characters’
visit, and to open the way to Linnaeus’s secret library,
they must seek answers in the specter’s memories…

CONFLICTS
This adventure does not follow the usual template
for mysteries with primary and secondary conflicts.
Instead, this mystery allows the player characters to
explore hidden parts of the National Archives and
complete three challenges to find Linnaeus’s secret
library and recover lost knowledge from his expedition to Dalecarlia. Each of the challenges has a conflict that the characters must resolve.

IN V ITATION
It is autumn and six months have passed since the
dramatic events on Goose Island. The Society once
again receives a letter from cork magnate August T.
Lysander (handout 3A on page 112, also available for
download on the Free League website):
Dear friends,
I hope the months since we last met have been kind to you. I wish I
could say the same for myself, but the events on Goose Island haunt
me still. I have buried my grief in work – running my cork company,
of course, but also researching the glorious history of the Society. The
latter began as a mere pastime, but in the process I have uncovered
secrets that I now feel compelled to share with you.
You may have heard of botanist Carl Linnaeus’s scientific expeditions to Dalecarlia during the last century. As you probably know,
Linnaeus was gifted with the Sight and later became the esteemed
leader of the Society – a true pioneer in vaesen studies. You may also
be aware that Linnaeus inexplicably left the Society and his research
on vaesen a year after his expedition to Dalecarlia, which he called
Societas Itineraria Reuterholmiana.
