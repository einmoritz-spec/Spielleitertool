As usual, the player characters can prepare for the
journey by purchasing equipment (page 73 of the core
rulebook) or using Upgrades at Castle Gyllen­creutz
(page 89 of the core rulebook). They can research
Linnaeus, and with a successful LEARNING test they
find information about his public life and work (see
the sidebar below).

Once back in Stockholm, the player characters are
free to go wherever they want, but sooner or later
they will likely head to the Golden Peace in the Town
Between the Bridges to meet with Lysander and possibly check into the hotel.
The tavern and hotel are described on page 22
in the first mystery of this book, Scent of a Killer. There
the characters can be reunited with tavern keeper
Henrik Öhman (page 24) and the poet Erik Johan
Stagnelius, who is in a particularly melancholy mood
and expressing it on the violin. Neither of them has a
role to play in this mystery, but it might be fun seeing
them again. Stagnelius will also return in the next mystery, so it might be good to remind the players of him.
Feel free to show them his portrait again (page 23).

Linnaeus was born in 1707 in Råshult in the
parish of Stenbrohult in the Smolandia
countryside. From an early age, Linnaeus
showed an interest in plants, especially
flowers. When Carl’s father was studying
to become a priest, he adopted the name
Linnaeus after a large linden tree that grew
on his family’s farmstead when he was a boy.
In the 1730s, Linnaeus made famous expe­
ditions to Lapland and Dalecarlia to gather
knowledge about flora and fauna.
