Lysander has reason to believe that Homo Ferus is

Society’s help, and there is no time to waste – he
has heard that the Rosenbergers, the Society’s rivals
(page 82 of the core rulebook), are also looking for
Linnaeus’s secret library. It would be disastrous if
they found it first.
TRUTHS A ND LIES

Lysander’s story is a mixture of truth and lies. His
real reason for seeking Linnaeus’s secret library and
Homo Ferus is that he believes the journal may contain
a solution to the spertus’s curse. The idea that the
Rosenbergers are looking for the library is a complete
lie, invented by Lysander to create a sense of urgency
and motivate the player characters.

WHAT HAS TRANSPIRED
IN THE CAPITAL?
The player characters may wonder what has
happened in the nation’s capital since their
last visit. If so, the following information can
be provided by any of the adventure’s NPCs:
The grisly murders of last autumn have
stopped. Coroner Ester Volk has not been
seen since (if she survived Scent of a Killer).
Protests against the royal family have con­
tinued. Workers at the steam-­powered Fire
Mill have organized several demonstrations.

COUNTDOW N
This mystery does not follow the usual template and
there is no disaster for the player characters to avert.
However, there is a countdown of escalating events
that may occur, caused by Linnaeus’s ghost that haunts
the National Archives.
The specter can take several forms – a headless
monk, a terrifying skeleton, or a ghostly apparition
of Linnaeus himself in an 18th-­century wig and old-­
fashioned clothes (see image on page 77). You are
free to choose which of the following events occur,
and in what order, or create your own ghosts to inflict
on the characters.
An icy wind suddenly sweeps through the room.
The characters hear strange sighs and the rattling
of heavy chains.
Old portraits on the walls seem to be watching
the characters.
Their lanterns inexplicably go out.
A headless figure in a monk’s cowl is seen reading a
book or examining an inscription. If approached,
the figure vanishes into smoke and the characters
must pass a Fear test against Fear 1.
The characters can see a ghost walking in front of
them in a dark hallway. Suddenly it turns toward
them, revealing its grimacing skull and letting out
a terrible scream. The characters must pass a Fear
test against Fear 2.
