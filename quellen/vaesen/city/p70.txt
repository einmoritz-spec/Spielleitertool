LOCATIONS
This mystery takes place at the National Archives on
Knights’ Isle in Stockholm (see the map below), in
tunnels and vaults beneath the building, and in hidden
chambers that supernaturally allow the player characters
to relive scenes from the life of Carl Linnaeus and his
expedition to Dalecarlia more than a century earlier.
A map of the catacombs beneath the National
Archives and the path to the secret library can be
found on page 68. The characters can find this
map in the Esoteric Section (next page).

ENTR A NCE H A LL
The National Archives has been collecting records,
documents, books, and other items of particular
importance to the realm for hundreds of years. It is
one of Sweden’s oldest government agencies, dating
back to the 13th century.
The impressive stone building on Knights’ Isle
is newly constructed and built on a cast-­iron frame
to reduce the risk of fire. It will be familiar to characters who visited the archives in the first mystery of
this campaign, Scent of a Killer. A wide stone staircase
leads up to the first floor and the front desk.

The floors rest on iron beams and are supported
by slender cast iron columns. A spiral book chute
runs through the entire the stairwell. Light shines in
through beautiful leaded windows. The bookcases are
also made of iron and the doors of sheet metal. The
National Archives is not open to the public.

CH A LLENGES

The Archivist: Sitting behind the front desk is
archivist Sigrid Holgersdotter, an ancient woman
with round glasses and a stern face. Her gray hair
is pulled back in a tight bun. Her behavior toward
the characters depends on whether they have met
before and, if so, how that meeting went. Unless
the characters are already on very good terms with
Sigrid, it takes a MANIPULATION test or a “donation to
the organization” (cost level 2) for them to be granted
access to the collections.
Basement Vaults: If the characters ask Sigrid
about vaults underneath the building, she admits
that they exist and glances at an inconspicuous
little door in the stairwell, visible from the front
desk. She says that she cannot possibly let the characters walk around down there. Persuading her is
difficult and takes another MANIPULATION test, this
time a Challenging one (two successes required).
If they fail, the characters must find a way to trick
or sneak past Sigrid, or break into the National
Archives at night or hide until it closes for the
night. Do not make it too difficult for them.
