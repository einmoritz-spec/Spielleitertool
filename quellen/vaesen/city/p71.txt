The Esoteric Section is located on the second floor.
There are reading areas with leather armchairs, large
potted plants on pedestals, and statues among the
bookcases. The Esoteric Section contains not only
writings, but also historical artifacts and large display
cases with stuffed animals and dried plants collected
during various expeditions.

Searching For Information: In the City Archives,
the characters can find useful information about
the history of the National Archives and Knights’
Isle. Each character searching for information
can make a LEARNING test and gain a clue (see below)
for each six rolled. If at least one character succeeds, they also find an old map of the labyrinthine
passages beneath the National Archives, tucked
between the ancient scrolls.
CLUES

The Grey­friars Monastery: The basement of the new
National Archives is connected to old cellar vaults
that originated from the medieval Grey­friars Monastery, one of the first buildings erected on Knights’
Isle. Back then the island was called Grey­friars Islet.
The tunnelswere expanded over centuries and
connected many of the buildings on Knights’ Isle. It
is said that in the 17th and 18th centuries, the cellar
vaults were used as meeting places for secret societies.
The Map: The old map of the passageways beneath
the National Archives is shown on page 68
(handout 3B on page 113, also available for download on the Free League website).

The Ghost of the National Archives: If a LEARNING test is
made here, whether it succeeds or not, the player
characters spot an interesting tome among a large
number of leather-­bound volumes on spirits and
ghosts. It is an unpublished manuscript entitled
The Ghost of the National Archives, written by archivist
Sigrid Holgersdotter herself.
CLUES

The Ghost of the National Archives manuscript is a
collection of humorous but bloodcurdling ghost
stories, all set at the National Archives, where
hapless visitors are scared out of their wits. It also
includes drawings of the various specters.
The Symbol: Several of the drawings show the symbol of an ouroboros in a triquetra (see illustration
below). The player characters recognize it as the
original symbol of the Society, used for centuries
by their predecessors.
