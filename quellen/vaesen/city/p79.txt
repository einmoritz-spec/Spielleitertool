HEDDA VON DER BURG

In 1734, Linnaeus is 27 years old. He is
excited about his expedition, but also a bit
worried that Reuterholm will claim any discoveries he makes about vaesen in the name
of the King. Linnaeus favors more casual
attire during expeditions, but for the evening he wears a ruffled shirt, an embroidered silk waistcoat, and white wig. In one
pocket he carries a gilded snuff box containing the spertus.

One of Linnaeus’s students and Reuterholm’s
goddaughter. She wears a white wig and a
silver brocaded silk dress with side hoops. A
botanist, orientalist, and cook, Hedda was
active in scientific circles from an early age,
and at the age of 25 she wrote her dissertation
De Acetariis on the subject of salad plants. She
is convinced of their beneficial effects on the
body. She has made several trips to India and
the Middle East and published a cookbook
on vegetarian diets and the medicinal properties of herbs. She is happy to discuss this at
length and also gives tips on salad plants to eat.
Hedda von der Burg has the Sight and
a special interest in Chinese puzzle boxes,
which comes in handy when she later builds
the magic lock for Linnaeus’ secret library
(page 5) together with him.
