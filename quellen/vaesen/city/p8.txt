INTRODUCTION
Welcome to City of My Nightmares for the Vaesen roleplaying game. This four-­part campaign shifts the
focus from the dark forests of the countryside to the
bustling city and the vaesen lurking in its alleys. It
also gives players the chance to experience mysteries
that deviate slightly from the established format in
terms of time, space, and structure.
The plot of City of My Nightmares spans over a year,
and you can easily slot in other mysteries between
the four parts of this saga. To truly make the campaign your own, we recommend that you as Game­
master connect key NPCs to the players characters
and their backstories. Some NPCs appear several
times throughout the campaign and may become the
characters’ friends and allies – or enemies.

is your game, and you know best what works for your
group. Enjoy!

THE FIRST
AND LAST OWNER
This section sets the stage for City of My Nightmares. It
focuses on two people of great importance to the
story – the famous botanist Carl Linnaeus and the
cork magnate August T. Lysander. These men are the
first and last owners of the spertus (page 154 of the
rulebook) that plays a central role in the campaign.

FOR THE GAME­M ASTER ONLY!
This entire book is intended for the Game­
master’s eyes only. If you plan to enjoy this
campaign as a player, stop reading now, or
you will ruin the suspense for yourself and the
other players.

As is often the case with Vaesen, City of My Nightmares
is a mix of fiction and real Scandinavian 19th century history. Many of the people, places, and events
described in the book have a historical basis, but most
of the details are made up. Some historical figures
and events have been altered or shifted in time and
place, and the story itself is of course entirely fictional.
Remember – this is the Mythic North, a fantasy
world where vaesen are real, and it makes no claim to
historical accuracy. That said, we did a lot of research
