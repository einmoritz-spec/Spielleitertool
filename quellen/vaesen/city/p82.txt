The musicis irresistibly catchy. Each round in
the vicinity of the three bewitched musicians (i.e.
in the same zone), the characters must pass an
OBSERVATION test to avoid being drawn into the
dance and losing their turn in the round. A new
attempt may be made each round. The music continues until all three violinists are Broken (they
do not defend themselves), the strings of their
instruments have been cut (requires a successful
CLOSE COMBAT test with a knife), the violins have
been taken from them (requires an opposed FORCE
test), or the Neck has been banished.
The Neck: If the players have access to the Vaesen
art book or the Codex Occultum booklet (included
in the game’s starter set), it can help them figure
out that the man with the lyre is the Neck (page
148 of the core rulebook) and provide tips on how
to banish him. Otherwise, you can let them make
LEARNING tests to gain clues (see below) or simply
have Linnaeus help them. The Neck attacks any
character who tries to stop the music and defends
himself if attacked.
A Familiar Face: A character who passes a VIGILANCE test finds the Neck’s face strangely familiar
but cannot figure out why (this will be revealed
in the next mystery).
The dancersdesperately seek the characters’ gaze
with a silent plea for help – they cannot stop dancing. Their eyes are wide with fear. If the enchanting music is stopped, the exhausted dancers collapse to the ground and the walking dead revert
to lifeless bones. But the Neck himself continues
to fight until he is banished.
The Lyre: When banished, the Neck turns into
a toad that jumps into the river and disappears,
leaving behind his lyre. It is beautifully carved and
looks very old, decorated with symbols that the
characters (with a successful LEARNING test) recognize as ancient Greek letters. Linnaeus picks up the
lyre and notes that it is a very valuable and potentially dangerous artifact that must be protected

Knife: If a knife that has cut the strings of a violin (e.g. one of the musicians’ instruments) is
stuck into the ground in the same zone as the Neck
(long action), he cannot cast Enchantments and
Curses in that zone or against anyone in it. The
significance of the knife is hopefully enough for
the players to realize that it is one of the three
symbols to be pressed in the floor relief in the
Chamber of Riddles.
Christian Symbols: The Neck is also sensitive
to crosses and other Christian symbols. If anyone in the same zone shows him such a symbol
(long action), he must pass a MIGHT test (not an
action) or be forced to leave the zone on his next
turn. If the Neck is completely surrounded by
Christian symbols, he is immediately Broken
and banished.
