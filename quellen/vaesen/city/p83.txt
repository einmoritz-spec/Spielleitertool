LINNA EUS A PPEA RS

CONFRONTATION
If the three correct symbols in the floor relief are
pressed at the same time, there is a loud rumble as
the inner slab sinks about a meter into the floor,
revealing a dark, narrow staircase that leads to
the secret library. The player characters can take a
gilded candelabra with them if they have no other
light source.
The stairs lead to a small, dusty stone chamber
lined with bookshelves containing hundreds of old
writings and notes. On one of the walls hangs a painting of Linnaeus on his Lapland expedition (see adjacent image). The letters “CL” are carved into the floor,
and a thick book of handwritten pages lies open on
a lectern. This is Linnaeus’s secret library, and the
book is Homo Ferus.

If the ghost of Linnaeus has not previously appeared to
the player characters in his true form (see sidebar on
page 77), he does so now, before they start examining the room and the book. A shimmering figure
appears before them. It is an older man wearing 18th
century clothes and wig – see the image of Linnaeus’s
ghost on page 77. The sight of him provokes a Fear
test against Fear 1 (lower than normal as the ghost is
not trying to scare them). If the characters have seen
the ghost before (see sidebar on page 77), they do
not need to roll this time.
“Friends of the Unseen, welcome! You have proven yourselves
more than worthy of my writings!”
Let the characters react and talk to Linnaeus’s ghost.
However, he does not have time to provide any useful
answers before the next phase of the drama occurs.
