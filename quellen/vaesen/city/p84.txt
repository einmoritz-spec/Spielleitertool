TROUBLE IN THE LIBR A RY
Suddenly, the player characters hear footsteps coming down the stairs. August T. Lysander enters the
library with an enigmatic expression on his face and
his hands behind his back. His pained look is soon
explained as Lysander’s valet Karl-­Axel Trygg walks
behind him with a drawn revolver, followed by some
burly men with guns. If Linnaeus’s ghost is here, he
fades away.
“I’m so sorry,” says Lysander bitterly, hands tied
behind his back. “They made me follow you here. I had no
idea there was a snake in my midst.”
“Shut it, swine,” replies Karl-­Axel Trygg. “I’m claiming the
book for the Rosenbergers. Give it to me, or this day will be your last.”
Karl-­Axel pulls up his coat sleeve and shows a
tattoo of a howling werewolf head – the Rosenberger
symbol. Let the player characters react. Of course,
these “Rosenbergers” are fake and the attack staged

The fake Rosenbergers, including Karl-­Axel Trygg,
are as many as they are. If the characters refuse to
hand over the book, it will likely result in combat.
Clever maneuvers and diplomacy on the characters’
part should be rewarded, but the Rosenbergers are
not easily fooled.
If there is a fight, the Rosenbergers will flee if
half of them are Broken or when Linnaeus’s ghost
appears (below). If possible, they will try to take the
book Homo Ferus with them. Lysander’s hands are tied
and he does not take part in the fight.

LINNA EUS AGA IN
If combat breaks out in the library, Linnaeus’s ghost
becomes very agitated. At the appropriate moment
during the fight, the room suddenly turns freezing
cold as the ghost reappears, hovering near the ceiling. His eyes glow red, and he wears heavy chains
around his wrists and ankles. The ghost rattles
his chains, sighing loudly: “Not again! When will the
treachery end?”
The ghost cannot be reasoned with. The player
characters must make a Fear test against Fear 1 (even
though they have already encountered the ghost).
Lysander and the Rosenbergers, who have not seen
the ghost before, are struck by panic – Lysander freezes
in terror and Karl-­Axel Trygg flees if possible. After
this, Linnaeus’s ghost vanishes until he reappears in
the next mystery.

THE TR A ITOR
Lysander pretends to be shaken and swears that
he had no idea of Karl-­Axel’s connections to the
Rosenbergers. The captured Rosenbergers (if any)
refuse to answer questions, but Lysander promises to turn them over to the police. If the players
want to know more about this organization, you
can tell them what is written on page 82 of the
core rulebook.
