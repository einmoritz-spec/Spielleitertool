CONFLICTS
The primary conflict revolves around August T.
Lysander and his spertus. Lysander seeks the lyre of
the Neck, which was found during Linnaeus’s expedition in Dalecarlia and later presented to the king
by Governor Reuterholm. He believes that in return
for the lyre, the Neck will free him from the spertus’
curse. Together with his accomplices, he plans to
break into the Royal Armory and steal the lyre.
The secondary conflict takes place on the streets
of Stockholm, where popular resentment against
the king and the upper classes is growing. Inspired
by revolutions on the continent, insurgents seek to
overthrow the monarchy and establish a republic.
The city is buzzing with unrest, and the unusally hot
spring nights see large crowds partying outside. The
atmosphere grows increasingly chaotic, fueled by the
Neck himself.
The Neck has emerged from the Stream of Stockholm to play at taverns and other venues where Stockholmers gather to dance. He walks in human form
and can be seen performing at inns and parties that
soon spiral out of control. People dance until they
collapse, and violent brawls break out. Even Stockholm’s new police force is influenced by the vaesen’s
tunes, with officers growing increasingly brutal in
their efforts to maintain order and suppress the protests in the streets.

IN V ITATION
It is springtime, and the player characters are gathered around a table on one of the glazed verandas of
Castle Gyllencreutz, overlooking the garden. The
glasses on the table are lit by the soft glow of candles,
while the buildings of Upsala form dark silhouettes
against the blue evening sky.
The player characters are lost in thought, having just received troubling news from the capital.
Protests against the royal family have gained fresh
momentum and the streets are in turmoil. Workers
at the steam-­powered Fire Mill have gone on strike
in protest against the government. Mock suns, or sun

dogs as they were once called, have been spotted in
the sky above the city – it is taken as an ominous sign,
a warning that dark times are ahead.
Suddenly the doorbell rings. A messenger delivers a letter sealed with beautiful emerald wax (handout
4A on page 117, also available for download on the
Free League website).
Friends of the Unseen,
My name is Vilma af Zimt, an artist and medium based in Stockholm. I am writing you because of a message I received from the
spirit world during a séance I recently conducted at the Swedenborg Society. The message came to me and the other participants
through automatic writing on a Ouija board. A powerful spirit
sought contact. He seemed deeply troubled and insisted on conveying the message directly to you. When I offered to pass on his
message, he became violent, knocking over several candlesticks in
the room. Several participants fainted in terror.
Therefore, I invite you to a séance at my apartment at 19 Charity Street in the Town Between the Bridges, so you may have direct
contact with the spirit.
Yours affectionately,
Vilma af Zimt

PREPA R ATIONS
As usual, the player characters can prepare for the
journey at their headquarters by purchasing equipment (page 73 of the core rulebook) or using Upgrades
in Castle Gyllencreutz (page 89 of the core rulebook).
They can also read up on Vilma af Zimt (see sidebar
on the next page).

THE JOURNEY
Once again the player characters will likely take the
steam train from Upsala Central Station to Stockholm. As usual, they can gain an Advantage during
the journey (page 182 of the core rulebook). You can
show the players the large map of Stockholm when
the characters arrive.
