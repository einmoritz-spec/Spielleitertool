COUNTDOW N

1. During the night, a huge fire breaks out at the Fire
Mill steam-­powered mill on King’s Isle. The fire
burns so brightly that night turns into day across the
city. Large crowds gather to watch the spectacle, and
rumors spread that the fire was deliberately set. Others believe the fire has supernatural causes and see it
as yet another bad omen. See the image on page 91
and read more about the Fire Mill on page 90.
2. Chaos erupts at the dance hall in King’s Garden.
Many are killed, either in the ensuing fights or by
dancing themselves to death. See more on page 92.
3. Seditionists announce a large protest against the
royal family on Castle Slope on Sunday. The player
characters can find a flyer about this on the streets or
on the walls of buildings (handout 4C on page 119,
also available for download on the Free League website). A large crowd gathers, clashing with both the
police and the Royal Guard. Riots break out and
angry mobs start breaking into the palace.

CATASTROPHE

Using the palace riots as cover, Lysander breaks into
the Royal Armory and steals the Neck’s lyre. With the
help of two associates with the Sight (Xavier Liebholz
and Helena von Arensburg), he summons the Neck
and hands him the lyre. The Neck begins to play and
those nearby are immediately trapped in a frenzied
dance. No one notices the crowd getting closer and
closer to the water’s edge, causing many to fall into
the bay and drown. Brutal fights erupt in other parts
of the crowd. Soon the chaos and violence spread to
other parts of the city, leading to bloody riots and
many deaths. By sunrise the Neck has vanished, leaving the streets of Stockholm littered with bodies.
