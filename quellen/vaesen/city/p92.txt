DREAMS AND VISIONS
During their stay in Stockholm, the player char­
acters may experience unsettling visions and
nightmares. In their dreams they might see
Stockholm plunged into chaos, its streets awash
with blood and violence, with people dancing
wildly in the streets to an increasingly frenzied
melody. A huge black bird glides over the roof­
tops. Feel free to weave in vaesen or people
from the characters’ traumas or dark secrets
to make the dreams more personal. A typical
nightmare has Fear 1.

LOCATIONS
This mystery allows the player characters to travel
freely in Stockholm, but only the locations relevant
to the investigation are described below. All locations
are marked on the map on pages 86–87. Note
that Fire Mill only becomes part of the mystery after
step #1 of the countdown. Until then there is nothing of interest at the steam mill. The same goes for
the dance hall in King’s Garden (page 92), which
only becomes relevant after step #2 of the countdown.
If the characters wish to investigate other locations, you are free to improvise, but it is perfectly
alright to simply explain that the place in question
is not part of the mystery and that there is nothing for them there. Make sure that the game does
not come to a halt because the players are unsure
where to go next. Use the countdown to keep the
plot moving forward.

V ILM A A F ZIMT’S A PA RTMENT
Vilma af Zimt’s apartment is located on Charity Street
in the Town Between the Bridges. The player characters find her door in the shade of the large chestnut tree growing in the small square outside. A steep
wooden staircase leads up to a carved wooden door.

Vilma invites the player characters to sit and join
hands around the table. She closes her eyes, and suddenly a shadow falls across the room. The flames of the
candelabra flicker and the room turns freezing cold.
Vilma af Zimt opens her eyes. As she begins to speak,
you recognize the voice and manner of Linnaeus’s
ghost. The medium fixes her eyes on the characters
and says in a shaky voice:
“Danger is at hand! Someone has been in my library looking for
information about the lyre of the Neck, the one I took from him in
Dalecarlia and that Reuterholm seized for the king. The lyre must
not fall into the wrong hands! Orpheus defied death itself with this
instrument – it holds immense power. But now someone seeks it
for their own gain. You must stop them. Whatever happens, the
Neck must never again possess the lyre, for with it he would make
the whole city dance itself to death! Only you can save Stockholm
from destruction!”
The player characters may ask the ghost a few questions if they pass an OBSERVATION test. However, he is
somewhat incoherent and his answers cryptic – see
Clues below. Before he can say more, Linnaeus suddenly interrupts himself:
“Wait! Someone is here! Someone has seen me! It is she! The
woman with the dreadful bird! Light preserve us!”
In an instant, every candle in the room goes out.
Vilma collapses into her chair, visibly shaken. She
cannot tell them much (see below) but wishes the
characters good luck.
