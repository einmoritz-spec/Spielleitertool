THE NECK BY STAGNELIUS
Evening clouds drift through the sky,
fairies dancing lightly by,
as the leaf-­crowned Neck is strumming
to the silver stream’s soft humming.

If the player characters arrive at the mill before the
fire breaks out (step #1 of the countdown), they may
encounter a group of angry workers on strike, protesting poor working conditions and low wages. (Not
on Sundays, though, but Saturdays are work days at
this time.)
If they arrive while the fire is raging, the heat is
intense even from a distance. A small team of firefighters are struggling to contain the blaze, doing
their best to keep it from spreading. A large crowd
has gathered around them – journalists, members of
the public, and workers who are watching their liveli­
hoods go up in flames. Some shout slogans against
the police and the royal family. The fire rages on well
into the morning before it finally dies out.

The Police Commissioner: Among the police
officers sent here to maintain order, the characters may run into Commissioner Gabriel Utterson, whom they have probably already met in the
mystery Scent of a Killer. He greets them but is busy

The Rescue Operation: At some point during
the characters’ visit, there is a sudden crash
as the roof of a building collapses, trapping
some of the firefighters. They scream for help.
If the characters attempt to rescue them, they
must make a FORCE test. Multiple attempts can
be made, and multiple characters may help, but
everyone involved suffers a physical Condition
with each roll.
