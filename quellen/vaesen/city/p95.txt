FIRE MILL BURNING
A most extraordinary and terrible spectacle pre­
sented itself to Stockholmers yesterday after­
noon and into the night. The Fire Mill on King’s
Isle, with its vast stores of grain and flour, was
engulfed in flames – a conflagration the like of
which is, fortunately, rarely seen in our city. The
fire erupted with such fury and spread so swiftly
that the entire capital and its surroundings were
illuminated as if by some sudden act of sorcery. In
the Royal Forest, it seemed as though a great arc
light had cast a mighty beam in that direction, and
the waters between the Royal Forest and Ships’
Isle glowed with a fiery hue. Huge crowds took to
the streets. North Bridge, Vasa Bridge, and the
walkways were packed with onlookers, among
whom wild rumors circulated about the cause of
the fire and the most outlandish tales were told.
— Dagens Nyheter newspaper
