DA NCE H A LL
In the large King’s Garden park in the center of Stockholm, there is a large open-­air dance hall, a popular haunt for the city’s pleasure-­seekers – especially
these days, when many seem particularly drawn to
dancing and revelry.
At some point during the mystery (see step #2 of
the countdown on page 86), chaos breaks out in
the dance hall. The Neck appears in human form
and plays a tune so irresistible that people literally
dance themselves to death. The player characters may
arrive in the aftermath of this horrific event, alerted
by terrified screams or summoned by Commissioner
Utterson (page 95).

The Musician: If the player characters pass a VIGILANCE test while looking for witnesses, they notice
a man in dark clothing sitting on the ground. His
eyes are hardened, his fingers bloody, and he is
drinking from a large mug of brandy. His name
is Sven Johan Garvare, a musician who was performing at the dance hall. If the characters comfort him with a successful INSPIRATION test, he tells
them what happened – see Clues below.
CLUES

The Injuries: An examination of the injured and
dead reveals that many have severe wounds on their
feet and lower legs. Others have been stabbed and
beaten. Soon police officers arrive to take the bodies to the morgue at the police station (page 94)
for further examination. The player characters
can go there if they wish to learn more.
The Testimony: Sven Johan Garvare explains that
he was hired to play at the dance hall, but when he
arrived another musician was already performing.

His music was so beautiful and mesmerizing that
he could not help but join in. Even as his fingers
began to bleed, he could not stop playing. He could
only watch as the crowd’s dancing grew increasingly frantic, and soon they started attacking each
other like animals. Finally, the strange musician
vanished, leaving Sven Johan alone with bleeding
hands. He shows the characters his bloodied fingers before taking another drink of brandy – he
does not want to talk about it anymore. The characters can hear similar stories from other witnesses.
Stagnelius: If a player character describes Stagnelius to Sven Johan Garvare or other witnesses,
they can confirm that the description matches the
strange musician.
The Neck: By now the characters should realize
that the Neck is in Stockholm and that he is a serious threat. If he were to get hold of the lyre, his
power would grow even greater. If the players do
not come to this conclusion on their own, you
can have them make a LEARNING test to figure it out.
