Mock Suns: If the player characters wish to learn
more about mock suns, they can find information
in the Esoteric Section. A mock sun is a meteorological phenomenon in which one or more
sun-­like points of light appear next to the sun.
It is related to the halo phenomenon, a circle of
light surrounding the sun. A famous painting of
mock suns over 16th century Stockholm (adjacent)
hangs in Stockholm Cathedral. Mock suns used
to be called sun dogs and have long been viewed
as bad omens of dark times ahead.

THE POLICE HEA DQUA RTERS
The police headquarters sits in the northern part
of the Town Between the Bridges, overlooking the
Stockholm Stream. The sturdy stone building rises
four stories high and, in addition to offices for the
police force, houses a detention center with a hundred
cells and a morgue run by the new coroner, Lennart
Mörk. Commissioner Utterson’s office is located on
the third floor. All visitors must check in with the
doorman before being escorted through the building’s narrow corridors.
