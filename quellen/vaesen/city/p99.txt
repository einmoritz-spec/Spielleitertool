LYSA NDER’S OFFICE
August T. Lysander’s company (which the player
characters may have already visited in Scent of a Killer)
has a palace on Shipside Street, with a magnificent
view of the Stockholm Stream and the ships that lie
anchored there. The building dates back to the 17th
century and has a large, ornate door decorated with
mermaids holding gas lamps. A beautiful marble
staircase and gilded sconces can be seen inside the
door. Lysander has had the palace renovated with the
latest technology and installed an iron-­grate elevator
that runs from the ground floor all the way up to his
office at the top.

player characters at the door. If they do not have

STEALTH . There are two more guards posted outside

Lysander: If the player characters meet Lysander,
he greets them with a big smile (assuming they
parted as friends after The Haunted Library) and welcomes them to the capital. He acts helpful, without revealing his actual plan. How Lysander reacts
to the characters’ visit depends on their behavior
during the meeting – see the Lysander’s Reaction sidebar.
Berns’ Salon: If the performance at Berns’ Salon

suggests that they meet there to continue their
conversation, since he “knows the artists personally.”
