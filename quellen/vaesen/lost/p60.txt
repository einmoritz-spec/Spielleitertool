ROYAL MOUNDS OF OLD UPSALA
Dating back to the 5th and 6th centuries,
the Royal Mounds of Old Upsala have been
shrouded in mystery for generations. Confirmed
to be the burial sites of legendary kings, they
are located north of Upsala close to where the
old Norse temple used to be – the most sacred
and important religious site during the Viking
age. The ground oozes of spirituality and memories of a great dynasty long gone.
