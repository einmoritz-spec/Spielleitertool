The Thomas farm sits at the foot of the hill below Old
Meg’s Holt. It consists of a small stone farmhouse,
a timber barn on a stone foundation, and a fenced
sheepfold, all surrounded by a low stone wall. The
farmhouse is little bigger than a cottage. The family
is headed by Fred Thomas. His wife Emma died two
years ago, leaving him and Daisy alone. Also on the
farm are two hired hands, Rob Watledge and Edwin
Horsley. Both are in their early twenties.
