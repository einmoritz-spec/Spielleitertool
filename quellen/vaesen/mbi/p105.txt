A NNONSBURY POLICE STATION
Annonsbury police station stands close to the center
of the small town, with the post office on one side
and a bank on the other. All three buildings are made
of the local yellow sandstone, which has weathered
to a pale gray. Inside, the characters find a wooden
counter manned by Sergeant Jack Worrell, who asks
their business. Inside are various small offices and a
narrow corridor with cells on either side: six in total.
