Llantywyll (pronounced th’lan-­tuh-­whull, approximately) is typical of the mining villages that are
strung along the deep valleys of Wales. Some mine
coal to power Britain’s industry and others, like
Llantywyll, mine slate to roof the houses of the
expanding towns and cities. Like mines across
Europe, the slate mine in Llantywyll is home to a
community of knockers (page 66), known here
by their Welsh name of coblynau.
