Mrs. Pugh takes great pride in her role as
housekeeper to the village’s minister, and is
very protective of him. She is determined to
keep him in the house as much as possible
while his leg heals, and doubly suspicious of
outsiders who might have come in the wake
of the accident to take advantage of him in
some way.

The minister is a tall, slender man with dark
hair and blue eyes. His right leg is in a plaster cast from the knee to the ankle, and he
gets around using a pair of wooden crutches.
Griffith was born in Carnarvon by the
coast, and named one of the founders of
the Methodist movement. He was educated
in Birmingham in England, and Llantywyll
is his first posting. He is determined to do
well here, and especially to bring the local
community up to date by promoting science
and education.
