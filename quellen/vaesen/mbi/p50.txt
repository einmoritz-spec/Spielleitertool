NEW ARCHETYPES
The Vaesen core rulebook includes a number of character archetypes, all of which can be used in Mythic
Britain and Ireland just as freely as in the Mythic
North. The following pages present a selection of
others, inspired by this setting but just as usable
elsewhere.
All are presented in the same format as the archetypes in the core rulebook. British names have been
supplied, but are easily replaced with suitable names
from Scandinavia, or anywhere else a particular campaign might be set. For campaigns taking place outside Mythic Britain and Ireland, the Gamemaster
should feel free to make any other adjustments that
will help adapt these archetypes to their setting.
