There is much debate on how a banshee can be defeated.
Some say it cannot be done, while others maintain that
a ceremony of exorcism can be effective.

The caoineag (pronounced kooh-­nyak, approximately) laments beside a waterfall, stream, or loch.
She is invisible and insubstantial, and cannot be seen,
touched, harmed, or interacted with in any way.
The bean-­nighe (pronounced ben-­nyeh, approximately) takes the form of an ugly old woman who sits
by a ford or stream washing the grave-­clothes of those
who are about to die, crooning a dirge as she does so.
If someone can creep up on her undetected and take
a firm hold of her, she can be forced to say who is
about to die, and to grant three wishes. This is risky,
because she will lash out at an attacker with her wet
linen, causing paralysis in any body part she strikes.
