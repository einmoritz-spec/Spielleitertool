OLD MEG
This mystery for Vaesen takes place in the rural west
of England, where the old ways died hard and echoes
of the pagan past are all around. It can equally well be
set in Scandinavia, where there are similar traditions
of hags and trollwives and of miracle-­working saints.
While the cause of the mystery is not difficult to
determine, the characters will find themselves faced
with a powerful foe who is not at all easy to stop.
