PRELUDE

BACKGROUND

This first section describes the background of the
mystery and the conflicts on which it is based. There
is an invitation to kick off the session, then the text
proceeds to describe the journey to Härnösand and
then on to Färnsta. The section concludes with a
countdown of events which you as the Gamemaster
will initiate at some appropriate time during the mystery – and a catastrophe that describes what happens
if the player characters do not take action or fail in
their efforts. But first we will look back at the events
leading up to the mystery.

The forest has always been one of mankind’s greatest
assets, providing building materials for our houses
and furniture, fueling our fires, and becoming the
wagons and boats that have brought us forward. But
the woods have always had many secrets, from the fear
of getting lost to fairy tales about the wood wives who
rule them. Humans would eventually try to tame the
woods, and in the mid-19th century, the forests of
the north became the cradle of Swedish industrialism. Large sawmills were built along the rivers, and
large amounts of timber were sent downstream to
