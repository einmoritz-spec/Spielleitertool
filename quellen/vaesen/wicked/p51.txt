Vilhelmina is the teenage daughter of Dorotea, raised by her mother since her father,
Anton, died from an infection when she was
very young. Her mother has always taken
good care of her, without being overprotective. Vilhelmina has always been inquisitive and Dorotea encourages her daughter
to follow her curiosity.
This has given Vilhelmina a deep interest in nature and the taming of it. She is an
excellent archer, and despite her mother’s
considerable wealth, it is often her hunting
that puts food on the table. But this curiosity also makes her dream about seeing the
world outside Färnsta, and like her mother,
she would like to leave the village behind.
Vilhelmina is in her late teens, with an
athletic build and her mother’s distinct eye
and hair color.
