This mystery for the role-playing game Vaesen brings
the player characters to the wild and barren Swedish
west coast, at the time a haven for privateers, wreckers,
herring barons and other riffraff. The mystery will test
the characters’ deductive reasoning, social skills, and – if
they are not careful – their ability to fight for their lives.
